How to Check PNR Status Online in India: Easy Steps

31 Oct 2026

Planning a train journey in India? Your Passenger Name Record (PNR) is a unique 10-digit number that holds all your travel details, from seat assignments to current booking status. Knowing how to check your PNR status is essential for a smooth and stress-free trip. It helps you stay updated on whether your ticket is confirmed, if you have a RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation) status, or if you're on the waiting list. This guide will walk you through the simplest and most reliable ways to check your PNR status online and offline, ensuring you’re always in the know about your train travel.

What is PNR and Why You Need to Know It

PNR stands for Passenger Name Record. It's a unique 10-digit number generated when you book a train ticket, whether online or at a railway counter. This single number acts as a central repository for all information related to your booking, including passenger names, age, gender, contact details, journey route, train number, date of travel, and most importantly, your current ticket status. Knowing your PNR is crucial for several reasons. It allows you to confirm your seat or berth number, track any changes in your reservation status (like moving from waiting list to confirmed), check for train delays or cancellations, and even identify your coach and berth location on the day of travel. Keeping track of your PNR ensures you're fully prepared for your journey.

Checking PNR Status on the Official IRCTC Website

The most reliable way to check your PNR status is through the Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation (IRCTC) official website. First, open your web browser and navigate to the IRCTC website. Look for a section or link typically labeled 'PNR Status' or 'PNR Enquiry' on the homepage. Once you find it, you will be prompted to enter your 10-digit PNR number into the designated field. After entering your number, click on the 'Submit' or 'Get Status' button. The website will then display all the relevant details associated with your PNR, including the current reservation status (Confirmed, RAC, Waiting List), coach number, berth number, and other journey specifics. It's a straightforward process that provides immediate and accurate information about your booking.

Using the Indian Railways Official App for PNR Checks

For smartphone users, the official 'Rail Connect' app by IRCTC provides a convenient way to check your PNR status on the go. You can download this app from your device's app store (Google Play Store for Android or Apple App Store for iOS). After installing and opening the app, you will typically find a 'PNR Enquiry' or 'PNR Status' option prominently displayed on the main screen or within the menu. Tap on this option, and it will direct you to a page where you need to enter your unique 10-digit PNR number. Once you've typed in the number, simply tap 'Get PNR Status', and all your ticket details, including the most up-to-date reservation status, will be displayed instantly on your mobile screen. This method is particularly useful when you're away from a computer and need quick access to your booking information.

Checking PNR Status via SMS Service

Even without internet access, you can still check your PNR status using the SMS service provided by Indian Railways. This is an extremely useful option, especially when you are in areas with poor network coverage or limited data. To use this service, simply open your messaging app on your phone. In the message body, type 'PNR' followed by a space, and then your 10-digit PNR number. For example, if your PNR is 1234567890, you would type 'PNR 1234567890'. Send this SMS to 139. You will soon receive a reply SMS containing your current PNR status, including details like confirmation status, train number, and other relevant information. Standard SMS charges may apply, so check with your mobile service provider.

Understanding Different PNR Status Meanings

When you check your PNR status, you'll see various abbreviations. 'CNF' means Confirmed, indicating you have a confirmed seat/berth. 'RAC' (Reservation Against Cancellation) means you are allotted a seat, but not a berth. You might have to share a side lower berth with another RAC passenger, and a confirmed berth could be assigned if there's a cancellation. 'WL' (Waiting List) means you don't have a confirmed seat or berth yet, and your ticket will only get confirmed if enough passengers cancel their bookings. The number next to WL (e.g., WL 10) indicates your position on the waiting list. 'GNWL' (General Waiting List) is the most common, while 'RLWL' (Remote Location Waiting List) is for intermediate stations. Understanding these statuses helps you manage your expectations and plan accordingly.

When to Check Your PNR Status & Key Tips

It's a good practice to check your PNR status periodically, especially if your ticket was initially on the Waiting List or RAC. Many passengers check their status a few days before their journey, and then again a few hours before departure, as the final chart preparation often happens 2-4 hours before the train leaves. If your ticket remains on the waiting list even after chart preparation, it means it will automatically get canceled, and a refund will be initiated. Always ensure you have your correct 10-digit PNR number handy. If you are travelling in a group, remember that one PNR number covers all passengers booked under that ticket. Staying updated helps you avoid last-minute surprises and ensures a smoother travel experience with Indian Railways.

Conclusion

Checking your PNR status is a simple yet crucial step for any train traveler in India. With multiple convenient options available, from official websites and apps to SMS services, staying informed about your booking has never been easier. By understanding your PNR and its various statuses, you can travel with confidence, knowing exactly what to expect from your journey. Make it a habit to check your PNR status well before your departure to ensure a comfortable and hassle-free train experience.
