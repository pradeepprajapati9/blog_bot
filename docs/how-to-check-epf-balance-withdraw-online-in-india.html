How to Check EPF Balance & Withdraw Online in India

16 Sep 2026

For millions of salaried individuals in India, the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) is a cornerstone of their retirement savings. It's crucial to keep track of your contributions and know how to access these funds when needed. While earlier processes involved a lot of paperwork and visits to EPFO offices, digital platforms have made checking your balance and even initiating withdrawals much simpler and faster. This guide will walk you through the various online and offline methods available to manage your EPF account from the comfort of your home.

Understanding Your UAN: The Key to Your EPF Account

Your Universal Account Number (UAN) is a 12-digit permanent number allotted to every employee contributing to EPF. It remains the same throughout your career, even if you change jobs. Your UAN acts as a master key to all your provident fund accounts linked with different employers, making it easy to manage your PF funds. Before you can check your balance or initiate a withdrawal, ensuring your UAN is activated and linked to your KYC details (Aadhaar, PAN, Bank Account) is essential. If you don't know your UAN, you can usually find it on your salary slip, or by asking your employer, or even generating it through the EPFO portal if your Aadhaar is linked.

Checking EPF Balance via the EPFO Member e-Sewa Portal

The EPFO Member e-Sewa portal is your primary online gateway for all EPF-related services. To check your balance here, first ensure your UAN is activated. Visit the official EPFO website and navigate to the 'e-Passbook' section. You'll need to enter your activated UAN and password, along with a captcha code. After logging in, you can view your EPF passbook, which provides a detailed statement of your contributions, employer's contributions, and interest earned over time. You can also download this passbook for your records, offering a clear and transparent view of your accumulated savings.

Using the UMANG App for Quick Balance Checks

The UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) app is a versatile platform developed by the Government of India that provides access to numerous government services, including EPFO. To check your EPF balance using UMANG, download the app from your smartphone's app store. After registering with your mobile number and completing the Aadhaar-based verification (if prompted), search for 'EPFO' services. Select 'Employee Centric Services' and then 'View Passbook'. Enter your UAN and a one-time password (OTP) sent to your registered mobile number. The app will display your latest EPF balance, making it a convenient option for on-the-go access.

EPF Balance Check Through SMS and Missed Call

For those who prefer simpler methods or have limited internet access, EPFO offers balance inquiry services via SMS and a missed call. To check via SMS, send 'EPFOHO UAN ENG' (or HIN for Hindi, MAR for Marathi, etc.) to 7738299899 from your registered mobile number. You will receive an SMS with your latest PF balance and KYC details. For a missed call, simply dial 9966044425 from your registered mobile number. Your call will automatically disconnect after a couple of rings, and you will receive an SMS containing your EPF details. Ensure your UAN is linked with your bank account, Aadhaar, and PAN for these services to work seamlessly.

Understanding EPF Withdrawal: When and Why You Can Claim Funds

While EPF is primarily a retirement corpus, there are specific situations where you can make partial or full withdrawals. Full withdrawal is generally allowed upon retirement (after 58 years of age) or if you remain unemployed for two consecutive months. Partial withdrawals, also known as advances, can be claimed for specific purposes like purchasing or constructing a house, higher education for yourself or children, marriage, medical emergencies, or even during certain periods of unemployment. It's important to understand the specific conditions and limits for each type of withdrawal to ensure you apply for the correct claim and provide necessary documentation.

Step-by-Step Guide to Online EPF Withdrawal

Withdrawing EPF online is a streamlined process, provided all your KYC details are updated and verified on the EPFO portal. First, log in to the Member e-Sewa portal using your UAN and password. Navigate to the 'Online Services' tab and select 'Claim (Form-31, 19, 10C & 10D)'. On the next page, verify your bank account number linked with your UAN. Select the type of claim you wish to make (e.g., 'PF Advance (Form 31)' for partial withdrawal or 'Only PF Withdrawal (Form 19)' for full withdrawal after retirement/unemployment). Fill in the required details, upload any necessary scanned documents, and submit your application after verifying with an OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number. The processing typically takes a few days to a few weeks, after which the funds are credited directly to your linked bank account.

Conclusion

Managing your EPF account is now easier than ever, thanks to various digital initiatives by the EPFO. By understanding your UAN and utilizing the online portal, UMANG app, or even simple SMS and missed call services, you can stay informed about your savings. When the need arises, the online withdrawal process is straightforward, allowing you to access your funds without excessive hassle. Regularly checking your EPF balance and ensuring your KYC details are up-to-date empowers you to take control of an important part of your financial future.
