How to Apply for PAN Card Online in India: A Simple Guide

21 Aug 2026

A Permanent Account Number (PAN) card is more than just a piece of plastic; it's a crucial identification document for any financial transaction in India. Issued by the Income Tax Department, your PAN is essential for filing income tax returns, opening a bank account, investing in mutual funds, buying property, and many other daily financial activities. Applying for one might seem complicated, but thankfully, the process has become much simpler and can be completed entirely online. This guide will walk you through every step, ensuring you understand exactly how to get your PAN card without any hassle.

Understanding the Online Application Portals

When applying for a PAN card online, you typically use one of two authorized portals: NSDL e-Gov (now Protean eGov Technologies Limited) or UTIITSL (UTI Infrastructure Technology And Services Limited). Both are legitimate government-approved agencies that facilitate PAN card applications. The application form, known as Form 49A for Indian citizens, is generally the same across both platforms. Choosing between NSDL and UTIITSL is often a matter of personal preference, as both offer a similar, streamlined online experience. It's important to remember that you should only apply for one PAN card. Holding multiple PAN cards is illegal and can lead to penalties. If you already have a PAN but need a correction or reprint, there are separate online procedures for that. Familiarizing yourself with these official portals is the first step towards a smooth application.

Essential Documents Required for Application

Before you begin your online application, gather all necessary documents. You will need proof of identity, proof of address, and proof of date of birth. For Indian citizens, common documents accepted as Proof of Identity include your Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport. For Proof of Address, documents like your Aadhaar Card, Driving License, Passport, utility bills (electricity, water, gas – not older than 3 months), or a bank account statement are usually accepted. Lastly, for Proof of Date of Birth, your Aadhaar Card, Passport, Birth Certificate, or a Matriculation Certificate can be used. Ensure all your documents are clear, valid, and match the details you will enter in the application form. Having these ready will save you time and prevent delays.

Step-by-Step Online Application Process (Form 49A)

To apply, visit either the NSDL e-Gov or UTIITSL website. Look for the 'Apply PAN Online' or 'PAN Card Application' section. You will need to select 'New PAN – Indian Citizen (Form 49A)'. Fill in your details carefully, including your name, date of birth, email ID, and mobile number. Make sure your name matches exactly with your Aadhaar card or other identity proofs. After submitting the basic details, a token number will be generated, which you should save. This allows you to resume your application if you get disconnected. Proceed to fill out the detailed form, providing your address, contact information, and Assessing Officer (AO) code. The AO code is crucial for tax purposes and can usually be found using a search tool on the portal based on your address.

Payment of Application Fees

Once you have accurately filled out the online application form, the next step is to pay the processing fee. For applicants within India, the fee is typically around ₹101 to ₹107 (including GST), though it's always best to check the current rates on the official portal you are using. If you are an Indian citizen residing outside India and require a physical PAN card to be dispatched internationally, the fees will be higher, usually around ₹1011 (including GST). You can pay these fees using various online methods such as credit card, debit card, or net banking. After a successful payment, you will receive a payment receipt, which you should download and save for your records. This receipt often contains the acknowledgment number needed for tracking your application.

Submitting Your Documents and Application

After successful payment, you have a few options for submitting your application and supporting documents. 1. e-Sign/Aadhaar OTP Based: If your Aadhaar card is linked to your mobile number, you can opt for an e-Sign or Aadhaar-based OTP verification. This is the fastest and completely paperless method. You simply verify your identity using an OTP sent to your registered mobile number, and your Aadhaar details are used. 2. Forward Application Documents Physically: If you choose this option, you need to print the acknowledgment form generated after payment. Paste a recent passport-sized photograph in the designated space, sign it, and attach photocopies of your proof of identity, address, and date of birth. Mail these documents to the NSDL or UTIITSL processing center address provided on the acknowledgment form within 15 days of online application. 3. eKYC/e-Sign (for NSDL): Similar to Aadhaar OTP, this allows paperless submission using digital signatures. Choose the method that suits you best, with the Aadhaar-based options being the quickest.

Tracking Your PAN Application Status

Once your application is submitted, you'll receive an acknowledgment number. This number is your key to tracking the status of your PAN card. To check the progress, revisit the NSDL or UTIITSL website where you applied. Look for a section like 'Track PAN Status' or 'Status of PAN Application.' Enter your acknowledgment number and click submit. The portal will then display the current status of your application, letting you know if it's under process, documents received, or if the PAN has been allotted and dispatched. It's a good practice to check the status periodically, especially if you opted for physical document submission, to ensure everything is progressing smoothly.

Conclusion

Obtaining a PAN card online in India is a straightforward process when you know the steps. By preparing your documents, carefully filling out the online form on an authorized portal, making the payment, and choosing a suitable submission method, you can successfully apply for your essential financial identification. Remember to keep track of your acknowledgment number to monitor your application's status. With your PAN card in hand, you’ll be ready for various financial activities, from filing taxes to making investments, all contributing to your financial literacy and compliance in India.
