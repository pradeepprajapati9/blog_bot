Manage Your Digital Documents with DigiLocker: A Simple Guide

02 Nov 2026

In today's digital age, keeping track of important documents can be a challenge. From your PAN card to your driving license, physical copies can get misplaced or damaged. DigiLocker, a flagship initiative by the Government of India, offers a smart and secure solution. This guide will walk you through how to effectively use DigiLocker to manage your digital documents, making your life simpler and more organised. It’s your personal digital repository for official certificates and documents.

What is DigiLocker and Why Use It?

DigiLocker is a virtual locker that allows Indian citizens to store their official documents digitally. Think of it as a secure online space where you can keep digital copies of your important certificates, such as educational mark sheets, birth certificates, Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, and more. The primary benefit is convenience; you can access your documents anytime, anywhere, from any device. It also reduces the need to carry physical copies, which can be lost or stolen. Furthermore, DigiLocker ensures the authenticity of your documents as they are issued directly by government bodies, making them legally valid for verification purposes.

Getting Started: Signing Up for DigiLocker

Signing up for DigiLocker is a straightforward process. You can download the official DigiLocker mobile app from your smartphone's app store or access it via the DigiLocker website. To register, you will need your mobile number, which must be linked to your Aadhaar card. You'll receive an OTP (One-Time Password) on your registered mobile number to verify your identity. After verifying your mobile number, you'll be prompted to create a username and password for your account. You can then link your Aadhaar card to your DigiLocker account, which allows for further verification and access to more services.

Issuing Documents into Your DigiLocker

Once your account is set up, you can begin issuing documents. DigiLocker works with various government departments and educational institutions to provide digital copies of your certificates. To issue a document, navigate to the 'Get Issued Documents' section. You'll see a list of departments and the types of documents they offer. For example, you can request your CBSE mark sheet or your driving license. Select the relevant department and document, and follow the prompts to authenticate your request. If your details match the issuer's database, the digital document will appear in your 'Issued Documents' section.

Storing and Managing Your Documents

After documents are issued, they are securely stored in your DigiLocker. You can access them by logging into your account on the app or website. The 'Issued Documents' section lists all the certificates you have received. You can view these documents at any time. DigiLocker also allows you to upload your own self-attested documents, such as medical reports or other personal certificates, into a separate 'Personal Documents' section. This makes it a comprehensive digital storage solution for all your important paperwork. Organise them into folders for easier access later on.

Sharing Your Digital Documents Safely

One of the most powerful features of DigiLocker is the ability to share your documents securely. When you need to provide proof of identity or qualification to an organisation, you can generate a shareable link for your digital document. This link can be sent to the requesting entity. The recipient can then access and verify the document directly from DigiLocker. This process is much more secure than sharing photocopies and reduces the risk of document tampering. The sharing is done via a secure URL, ensuring only the intended party can view the document.

Tips for Secure Usage of DigiLocker

To ensure the utmost security of your digital documents, always use strong, unique passwords for your DigiLocker account and change them periodically. Never share your login credentials with anyone. Be cautious of phishing attempts or unofficial links asking for your DigiLocker details. Always download the official DigiLocker app from trusted app stores. When sharing documents, ensure you are doing so with a legitimate organisation. Enable two-factor authentication if available for an extra layer of security. Regularly review your account activity for any suspicious behaviour.

Conclusion

DigiLocker offers a convenient and secure way to manage your essential Indian government documents. By understanding how to sign up, issue, store, and share your documents, you can streamline many daily processes, from job applications to accessing government services. Embracing DigiLocker means embracing a more organised, efficient, and secure digital life.
