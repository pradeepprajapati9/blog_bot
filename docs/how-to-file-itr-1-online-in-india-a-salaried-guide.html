How to File ITR-1 Online in India: A Salaried Guide

07 Nov 2026

Filing your Income Tax Return (ITR) is an important annual responsibility for most earning individuals in India. For many salaried taxpayers, the process is streamlined thanks to ITR-1, also known as Sahaj. Filing online is not only convenient but also ensures accuracy if done correctly. This guide will walk you through the entire process of filing your ITR-1 online, helping you fulfill your tax obligations with ease. Understanding these steps can save you time and prevent common errors, making tax season a less daunting task.

Understanding ITR-1 (Sahaj) and Its Eligibility

ITR-1, or Sahaj, is the simplest form for filing income tax returns and is designed for resident individuals with a total income up to ₹50 Lakhs. This form is typically used by salaried employees. To be eligible for ITR-1, your income must come from specific sources: salary or pension, one house property, and other sources like interest from savings accounts, fixed deposits, or family pension. It's crucial to note that if you have income from capital gains, business or profession, more than one house property, or agricultural income exceeding ₹5,000, you are not eligible to file ITR-1 and must choose a different ITR form. Ensure you meet these criteria before proceeding, as choosing the wrong form can lead to complications and non-compliance.

Essential Documents to Keep Ready Before Filing

Before you begin the online filing process, gather all necessary documents to ensure a smooth and accurate submission. You will need your Permanent Account Number (PAN) card and Aadhaar card, as these are mandatory for identity verification. Your Form 16, issued by your employer, is critical as it contains details of your salary, TDS (Tax Deducted at Source), and deductions. Additionally, have your bank account details ready, as this is where any tax refund will be credited. Keep interest certificates from savings accounts and fixed deposits handy, if applicable. It's also wise to check your Form 26AS and Annual Information Statement (AIS) on the income tax portal to cross-verify TDS and other financial transactions reported against your PAN.

Logging In to the Income Tax e-Filing Portal

The first step in filing your ITR online is to visit the official Income Tax Department e-filing portal. Open your web browser and navigate to incometax.gov.in. If you are a new user, you will need to register yourself using your PAN as your user ID. The registration process involves providing basic details and setting up a password. If you are an existing user, simply click on the 'Login' button and enter your PAN as your user ID, followed by your password and a secure OTP (One-Time Password) sent to your registered mobile number or email ID. Once logged in, you will be directed to your dashboard, which provides an overview of your tax activities and quick links to various services.

Navigating the Online Filing Process for ITR-1

After logging in, locate the 'e-File' option on the menu, then select 'Income Tax Returns' and click on 'File Income Tax Return'. You will be prompted to choose the Assessment Year (the year for which you are filing the return), select 'Online' as the mode of filing, and then specify 'Individual' as your status. Finally, choose 'ITR-1 Sahaj' from the list of available forms. The portal will then present various sections of the ITR-1 form, typically pre-filled with information available with the Income Tax Department. These sections usually include Personal Information, Gross Total Income, Deductions and Taxable Total Income, Tax Paid, and Total Tax Liability. Carefully review each section.

Verifying Pre-filled Data and Claiming Deductions

One of the most crucial steps in online filing is meticulously verifying the pre-filled data. Compare the income details, TDS amounts, and other financial information displayed on the portal with your Form 16, Form 26AS, and AIS. Make any necessary corrections or additions, especially for income sources not reported by your employer, such as interest from savings accounts. Under the 'Deductions' section, accurately enter the amounts you are eligible to claim under various sections like 80C (for investments in PPF, ELSS, life insurance, etc.), 80D (for health insurance premiums), and 80TTA (for interest on savings accounts). Ensure you have valid proofs for all deductions claimed to avoid discrepancies later.

Paying Any Due Tax and e-Verification of Your Return

After completing all sections and reviewing your tax liability, the system will show if you have any tax due or if you are eligible for a refund. If tax is payable, you can generate a challan (ITNS 280) and pay it online through net banking or debit card. Ensure you update the challan details on the portal after payment. If you are due a refund, double-check your bank account details for accurate credit. The final step is e-verification, which is mandatory to complete your filing. You can e-verify using an Aadhaar OTP, Net Banking, a Bank Account EVC (Electronic Verification Code), or a Demat Account EVC. Once e-verified, your filing is complete, and you should download the acknowledgement (ITR-V) for your records.

Important Reminders for a Smooth Filing Experience

To ensure your ITR-1 filing is successful and hassle-free, keep a few key points in mind. Always file your return before the specified deadline to avoid penalties. Double-check all figures and personal details before submitting, as even small errors can cause delays or issues. Remember that this guide is specifically for ITR-1 for salaried individuals; if your income sources are different, you may need another form. Keep all physical proofs of investments and deductions safe even after filing, as you might need them for future reference or if the Income Tax Department raises a query. Filing your ITR accurately and on time contributes to your financial responsibility and helps maintain a good financial record.

Conclusion

Filing your Income Tax Return can seem complex, but with a clear understanding of the steps involved, especially for ITR-1, it becomes a straightforward process. By gathering your documents in advance, carefully verifying pre-filled information, and accurately claiming deductions, you can complete your online filing efficiently. Remember to always e-verify your return to finalize the submission. This practical guide aims to empower you with the knowledge to manage your tax obligations confidently, contributing to your financial health and compliance as an Indian resident.
