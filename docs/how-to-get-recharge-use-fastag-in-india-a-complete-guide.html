How to Get, Recharge & Use FASTag in India: A Complete Guide

16 Sep 2026

Driving on Indian highways has become much smoother and faster thanks to FASTag. This electronic toll collection system has eliminated long queues at toll plazas, making inter-state travel more efficient for millions. If you're new to FASTag or simply want to understand its features better, you've come to the right place. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know about getting, activating, recharging, and using your FASTag effectively, ensuring a hassle-free journey every time.

What is FASTag and How Does it Work?

FASTag is an RFID (Radio-Frequency Identification) tag that's affixed to your vehicle's windshield. It enables automatic deduction of toll charges while your vehicle is in motion, allowing you to pass through toll plazas without stopping for cash transactions. Each FASTag is linked to a prepaid account, and when you drive through a FASTag-enabled lane at a toll plaza, the tag is scanned, and the applicable toll amount is automatically deducted from your linked account. This system not only saves time and fuel but also promotes digital payments and transparency in toll collection. It's mandatory for all four-wheelers and above passing through national highway toll plazas in India, simplifying millions of daily commutes and long journeys.

Where and How to Get a FASTag in India

You have several convenient options to obtain a FASTag. Most major banks, including SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, and Paytm Payments Bank, offer FASTag services. You can apply online through their respective websites or mobile banking apps by filling out an application form and submitting required documents like your Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC), KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN), and a passport-sized photograph. Alternatively, you can purchase FASTag offline at designated bank branches, point-of-sale (POS) counters at toll plazas, or select petrol pumps. Online marketplaces like Amazon also offer FASTags, which are typically linked to specific bank accounts. Compare issuance fees and minimum balance requirements before choosing your provider.

Activating and Affixing Your FASTag Correctly

Once you receive your FASTag, activation is a crucial step. If you purchased it from a bank or through their online portal, it's usually pre-activated. However, if you bought a neutral FASTag from an online marketplace, you might need to link it to your bank account via the respective bank's portal or app, or through the 'My FASTag' app by NHAI. After activation, correctly affixing the tag to your vehicle is vital for seamless operation. The tag should be placed on the upper-middle part of your car's windshield from the inside. Ensure the adhesive side is clean and dry. Avoid placing it behind the rearview mirror or where it might obstruct the RFID scanner, as this can lead to scanning issues and delays at toll plazas.

How to Recharge Your FASTag Account

Keeping your FASTag account sufficiently funded is essential to avoid inconvenience at toll plazas. There are multiple ways to recharge your FASTag. The most common method is through your issuing bank's net banking portal or mobile app. Simply log in, navigate to the FASTag section, enter your vehicle number or wallet ID, and select the desired recharge amount. You can use various payment methods like UPI, debit card, or credit card. Third-party payment apps like PhonePe, Google Pay, and Paytm also allow FASTag recharges for various issuers. Additionally, some toll plazas and petrol pumps offer recharge facilities. Always check your balance before starting a long journey and recharge proactively to ensure smooth passage.

Checking Your FASTag Balance and Transaction History

Regularly checking your FASTag balance helps ensure you always have enough funds for toll payments. Most banks provide SMS alerts for toll deductions and low balance warnings. You can check your balance and view transaction history directly through your issuing bank's mobile app or net banking portal. Look for the 'FASTag' or 'Toll Payment' section. Another convenient way is through the 'My FASTag' mobile application by NHAI (National Highways Authority of India), which allows you to check the balance for FASTags from various issuers. For Paytm FASTags, you can check the balance directly in your Paytm app. Knowing your transaction history helps in tracking your expenses and resolving any discrepancies promptly.

Important Rules and Tips for Using FASTag

To ensure a smooth FASTag experience, remember these key rules and tips. Always use the dedicated FASTag lanes at toll plazas; entering a cash lane with a FASTag might result in a double charge or penalties. Maintain a minimum balance in your account to avoid your tag being blacklisted, which can lead to delays. If your FASTag is blacklisted due to insufficient funds, recharge it immediately. One FASTag is valid for one vehicle only; do not attempt to use the same tag for multiple vehicles. In case of any issues like incorrect deductions or scanning problems, contact your FASTag issuing bank's customer care or the NHAI helpline number for assistance. Regularly update your KYC documents associated with your FASTag to prevent any service interruptions.

Conclusion

FASTag has truly revolutionized highway travel in India, making it faster, more convenient, and completely cashless. By understanding how to get one, activate it correctly, keep it recharged, and adhere to its usage rules, you can enjoy seamless journeys across the country. Embrace this digital solution for a smoother, more efficient driving experience on India's national highways. Safe travels!
