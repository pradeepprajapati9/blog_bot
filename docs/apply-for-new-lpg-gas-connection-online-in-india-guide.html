Apply for New LPG Gas Connection Online in India: Guide

17 Sep 2026

Applying for a new LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) connection used to involve multiple trips to the distributor and lengthy paperwork. Thankfully, in India today, the process has largely moved online, making it much more convenient and time-saving. Whether you're setting up a new household or switching providers, understanding the online application procedure can save you significant effort. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know, from gathering documents to the final installation and essential safety measures, ensuring a hassle-free experience for Indian readers.

Why Apply Online for an LPG Connection?

The digital age has transformed many services, and getting an LPG connection is no exception. Applying online offers numerous advantages over the traditional offline method. Firstly, it saves you precious time and effort by eliminating the need for multiple visits to the gas agency. You can complete the application from the comfort of your home, anytime, without worrying about office hours. Secondly, the online portals are designed for user convenience, often guiding you through the process with clear instructions. You can easily upload necessary documents, track your application status in real-time, and even make initial payments digitally. This transparency and efficiency make the online route the preferred choice for many Indian households looking for a quick and streamlined way to get their gas connection.

Key Documents Needed for Your Application

Before you begin your online application, it's crucial to have all the required documents ready. These typically fall into two categories: Proof of Identity (POI) and Proof of Address (POA). For POI, you can usually use your Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Driving License, or Passport. For POA, acceptable documents include Aadhaar Card, Utility Bills (electricity, water, telephone) in your name, Bank Passbook, Rental Agreement, or your Passport. You will also need a recent passport-sized photograph. Some providers might ask for an affidavit of proof of ownership of the premises or a self-declaration. Ensure you have clear scanned copies or digital photographs of these documents as you'll need to upload them during the online process. Remember to keep the originals handy, as physical verification might be required later.

Step-by-Step Guide: Applying for an LPG Connection Online

Applying for a new LPG connection online is a straightforward process. First, visit the official website of your chosen LPG provider (Indane, HP Gas, or Bharat Gas). Look for a section like "New Connection" or "Register for LPG Connection." You'll usually be prompted to select your state and district, then choose your preferred distributor based on your location. Next, fill out the online application form accurately, providing personal details, contact information, and your address. Here, you'll upload the scanned copies of your Proof of Identity and Proof of Address. Once all details are filled and documents uploaded, review your application thoroughly for any errors before submitting it. You will receive an acknowledgement number or reference ID, which is vital for tracking your application status.

Post-Application: Verification, Payment & Installation

After successfully submitting your online application, the next steps involve verification and setup. The gas agency will review your submitted documents. In some cases, a representative might visit your registered address for physical verification of the details provided and the originality of your documents. Once your application is approved, you will be notified, usually via SMS or email, to make the initial payment. This payment typically covers the cost of the LPG cylinder, regulator, and installation charges, along with the Security Deposit. You can often pay this online through various digital payment methods. After payment, the agency will schedule the delivery and installation of your new LPG connection, including the cylinder and safety hose, right to your doorstep.

Choosing Your LPG Provider & Distributor Wisely

India has three primary public sector LPG providers: Indane (Indian Oil), HP Gas (Hindustan Petroleum), and Bharat Gas (Bharat Petroleum). All offer similar services and prices, so your choice often depends on the availability of distributors in your area and their service quality. When selecting a distributor, consider their proximity to your home for easy refills and quicker service. You can usually find a list of distributors on the provider's website after entering your pin code. Also, decide on the cylinder size. The standard 14.2 kg cylinder is common for most households, while a smaller 5 kg cylinder is suitable for individuals or those with limited usage, offering more flexibility. Researching local distributor reviews, if available, can also help make an informed decision.

Essential Safety Tips for LPG Gas Users

Once your new LPG connection is installed, adhering to safety guidelines is paramount to prevent accidents. Always ensure proper ventilation in the kitchen area where the gas cylinder and stove are kept. Regularly check the rubber hose connecting the cylinder to the stove for cracks or leaks; it should be replaced every 5 years or as recommended by the distributor. Always turn off the regulator knob on the cylinder when the gas stove is not in use, especially overnight. Never store flammable materials near the gas cylinder. If you ever detect a gas leak, immediately open all windows and doors, turn off the regulator, do not switch on or off any electrical appliances, and contact your distributor's emergency helpline immediately. Educate all family members on these crucial safety practices.

Conclusion

Applying for a new LPG gas connection online in India is a convenient and efficient process that saves you time and effort. By understanding the required documents, following the step-by-step online application, and being aware of the post-application procedures, you can ensure a smooth setup. Remember to choose your provider and distributor carefully, and most importantly, always prioritize safety with your new LPG connection. Embrace the digital convenience to bring an essential utility into your home with confidence.
