How to Use UPI in India for NRIs

10 Jul 2026

As an NRI, managing finances and making payments in India can sometimes feel complex. However, the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has revolutionized how money moves within India, offering a seamless, instant, and secure way to handle transactions. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to start using UPI in India as an NRI, from setting up your account to making everyday payments, ensuring you can manage your Indian finances with ease and confidence. Whether it's supporting family, paying bills, or handling expenses during your visits, UPI can be an invaluable tool for you.

Who is Eligible and What You Need to Get Started

To use UPI in India as an NRI, the primary requirement is holding either an NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) bank account with a participating Indian bank. Most major banks in India now support UPI for NRI accounts. Crucially, this bank account must be fully KYC (Know Your Customer) compliant. The other essential component is an active Indian mobile number. This number must be registered and linked with your NRE or NRO bank account. Without an active Indian mobile number tied to your bank account, you will not be able to register for or use UPI services. Ensure your bank has your latest contact details, including this Indian mobile number, updated in their records.

The Crucial Role of Your Indian Mobile Number for UPI

Your Indian mobile number is the backbone of your UPI experience. It serves multiple critical functions. Firstly, when you download and register on any UPI application (like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or your bank’s own UPI app), the app verifies your identity through this mobile number. It sends an SMS from this number to your bank to authenticate your account. Secondly, this same number is used to receive One-Time Passwords (OTPs) for registration, setting your UPI PIN, and for various transaction confirmations, adding an extra layer of security. Even if you reside abroad, it's vital to keep this Indian mobile number active by opting for minimum recharge plans that allow incoming SMS, ensuring uninterrupted access to your UPI services.

Linking Your NRE/NRO Account to a UPI Application

Once you have your active Indian mobile number linked to your NRE/NRO account, you can proceed to link it to a UPI application. Start by downloading a popular UPI app from your smartphone's app store (e.g., Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or your specific bank's UPI app). Open the app and select the option to register. You will need to verify your Indian mobile number, which the app will do by sending an SMS. After successful verification, you can choose your bank from the list provided. The app will then fetch your NRE/NRO account linked to that mobile number. Follow the prompts to set up your unique UPI PIN using your debit card details. This PIN is crucial for authorizing all your transactions, so keep it confidential and secure.

Making Payments and Transfers with UPI as an NRI

Using UPI to make payments from your NRE/NRO account is straightforward. You can send money to other UPI IDs, bank accounts (using account number and IFSC code), or even mobile numbers linked to UPI. For merchant payments, simply scan the QR code displayed at shops, enter the amount, and confirm with your UPI PIN. You can also pay utility bills, recharge mobiles, and make various online payments directly from your UPI app. Remember, UPI transactions are instant and available 24/7. It's important to note that while you can make payments from your NRE/NRO accounts within India, UPI is currently not designed for receiving direct international remittances from outside India. Funds for your NRE/NRO account still need to come through traditional remittance channels.

Benefits and Key Considerations for NRIs Using UPI

The benefits of using UPI for NRIs are substantial. It offers unparalleled convenience for supporting family in India, managing property expenses, or handling local payments during your visits. Transactions are instantaneous, often free or with minimal charges, and available round the clock. This eliminates the need for carrying large amounts of cash or relying on slower payment methods. However, always be mindful of security: never share your UPI PIN or OTPs with anyone. Be aware of daily transaction limits set by your bank and the UPI ecosystem. Always double-check recipient details before confirming a payment. UPI empowers you with greater control over your Indian finances, making it simpler to stay connected and manage responsibilities from afar.

Conclusion

UPI has truly transformed the landscape of digital payments in India, and its accessibility for NRIs is a testament to its widespread utility. By understanding the simple requirements and following the steps outlined, you can seamlessly integrate UPI into your financial management for India. This powerful tool offers convenience, speed, and security, allowing you to easily handle payments, support family, and manage your financial commitments within India from anywhere in the world. Embrace UPI to simplify your financial interactions and stay connected to your roots with unprecedented ease.
