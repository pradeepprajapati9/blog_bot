How to File an RTI Online in India: A Simple Guide

12 Nov 2026

The Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005, is a powerful tool designed to promote transparency and accountability in governance. It empowers Indian citizens to seek information from public authorities, shedding light on government operations and decisions. Whether you need details about a government scheme, a public project, or official records, RTI can help you get answers. This guide will walk you through the entire process of filing an RTI application online in India, making it simple and accessible for everyone. Understand your right and how to use it effectively.

Understanding the Right to Information (RTI) Act

The RTI Act, 2005, grants every Indian citizen the right to access information held by public authorities. This includes central, state, and local government bodies, as well as any organization substantially funded by the government. The primary objective is to empower citizens, curb corruption, and ensure that public funds are used transparently. You can request documents, records, memos, opinions, advice, press releases, circulars, orders, logbooks, contracts, reports, papers, samples, models, data material held in any electronic form, and information relating to any private body which can be accessed by a public authority under any other law. This Act marks a significant shift towards open governance, making public servants accountable to the people they serve.

Eligibility and What You Can Ask

Any citizen of India can file an RTI application. You don't need to explain why you want the information. However, there are certain types of information that are exempt from disclosure under Sections 8 and 9 of the RTI Act. These exemptions include information related to national security, foreign relations, commercial confidence, personal privacy (which would cause unwarranted invasion), or information that would impede the process of investigation or prosecution of offenders. You can ask for information in the form of copies of documents, records, notes, or even samples of materials. The information must already exist in a documented format; public authorities are not obligated to create new information or provide opinions or interpretations. Ensure your query is specific and directly relates to verifiable records.

Required Information Before You Start

Before you begin filing your RTI application online, gather a few key pieces of information. Firstly, identify the specific "Public Authority" (ministry, department, office) that holds the information you seek. This is crucial for directing your application correctly. If unsure, try to determine the most relevant department. Secondly, clearly formulate your question(s). Be precise, specific, and concise. Avoid vague or overly broad questions, as they might be rejected or lead to incomplete responses. Thirdly, have your personal details (name, address, contact information) ready. You will also need access to online payment methods (net banking, debit/credit card, UPI) for the nominal application fee, which is typically ₹10 for central government bodies.

Step-by-Step Guide to Filing RTI Online

Filing an RTI online is straightforward. First, visit the official portal at rtionline.gov.in (for Central Government ministries/departments). On the homepage, click on the "Submit Request" option. Carefully read the guidelines for filing an RTI request. Check the box to confirm you have read and understood them, then click "Submit." A form will appear where you need to select the Public Authority you wish to send your application to. Then, fill in your personal details such as Name, Gender, Address, Pincode, State, Education, Phone Number, and Email ID. Indicate if you are Below Poverty Line (BPL); if so, provide your BPL card number and year to waive the fee. In the "Text for RTI Request" box, type your specific question(s) clearly and concisely (max 3000 characters). You can optionally upload a supporting document (PDF only, max 1MB). Finally, enter the security code displayed, click "Make Payment," and complete the ₹10 fee using your preferred online payment method. After successful payment, you'll receive a unique registration number, which is crucial for tracking.

Tracking Your RTI Application Status

Once you've filed your RTI application, you can easily track its progress online. Regularly checking the status is important to stay informed. To do this, revisit the official portal at rtionline.gov.in. On the homepage, click on the "View Status" option. You will then need to enter your unique Registration Number (obtained after filing), your Email ID, and the Security Code displayed on the screen. Click "Submit" to view the current status of your application. The status will typically show whether it's "Received" (application submitted), "Under Process" (being reviewed by the Public Information Officer or PIO), "Disposed" (information sent or request rejected), or "Transferred" (sent to another public authority). This transparency ensures you know exactly when to expect a response or if any action, like an appeal, might be necessary.

What Happens After Filing and Appeals

After filing, the Public Information Officer (PIO) of the concerned department has 30 days to respond to your request. In cases involving the life or liberty of a person, the response must be provided within 48 hours. If the PIO transfers your application to another department because the information is held by them, you will be informed, and the 30-day clock restarts for the new department. If you don't receive a response within the stipulated time, or if you are dissatisfied with the information provided, you have the right to file a First Appeal. This appeal is made to the First Appellate Authority (FAA) within the same public authority, usually a senior officer. If still not satisfied with the FAA's decision, you can file a Second Appeal with the Central Information Commission (CIC) or State Information Commission (SIC), which is the highest appellate body under the RTI Act.

Conclusion

The Right to Information Act is a cornerstone of democratic governance, empowering you, the citizen, to demand accountability and transparency from public authorities. By understanding and utilizing the online RTI filing process, you can access crucial information, monitor government functions, and play an active role in India's democracy. Remember to be clear and specific in your requests, track your application diligently, and know your rights regarding appeals. Use this powerful tool responsibly to foster a more open and accountable system for everyone.
