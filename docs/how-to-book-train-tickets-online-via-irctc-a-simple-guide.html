How to Book Train Tickets Online via IRCTC: A Simple Guide

10 Nov 2026

Booking train tickets in India has become incredibly convenient thanks to the Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation (IRCTC). Gone are the days of long queues at railway stations; now, you can reserve your seat from the comfort of your home or on the go using your smartphone. This guide will walk you through the entire process of booking train tickets online through the official IRCTC platform, ensuring a smooth and hassle-free experience for your journey. We'll cover everything from setting up your account to confirming your booking, along with essential tips to make your travel planning easier.

Why Book Train Tickets Online with IRCTC?

Booking your train tickets directly through the official IRCTC website or its Rail Connect mobile app offers numerous advantages. Primarily, it saves you valuable time and effort by eliminating the need to visit a physical reservation counter. You get 24/7 access to ticket booking, allowing you to plan your travel at any hour. The platform provides real-time information on train availability, routes, schedules, and fares, empowering you to make informed decisions. Additionally, IRCTC's robust system helps ensure secure transactions and provides digital copies of your tickets, which are easily accessible on your phone or printable for your convenience. It also allows you to manage bookings, including cancellations and checking PNR status, all in one place. Using the official platform is crucial for security and reliability, protecting you from fraudulent third-party sites.

Step 1: Setting Up Your IRCTC Account

Before you can book any tickets, you need to register for an account on the IRCTC platform. Visit the official IRCTC website (www.irctc.co.in) or download the 'IRCTC Rail Connect' app from your app store. Click on the 'Register' option, usually found at the top right corner. You will need to provide basic details such as your name, date of birth, gender, mobile number, email ID, and a preferred username and password. Ensure you use an active mobile number and email, as these will be used for verification and receiving ticket details. After filling out the form, you’ll receive an OTP (One Time Password) on your mobile and a verification link in your email. Complete both verification steps to activate your account. Keep your username and password safe, as you’ll need them for every login.

Step 2: Searching for Trains and Checking Availability

Once your account is set up and you’ve logged in, you can start searching for trains. On the homepage, you'll see a 'Plan My Journey' section. Enter your 'From' station (origin) and 'To' station (destination), your desired 'Journey Date', and select your preferred 'Class' (e.g., Sleeper, AC 3-tier, AC 2-tier, etc.) if you have one, or choose 'All Class' to see all options. You can also select 'Train Type' if you have a preference (e.g., Rajdhani, Shatabdi, etc.). Click on 'Search' or 'Find Trains'. The results page will display a list of trains running on your selected route and date. For each train, you can check the availability of seats in different classes for various dates. Click on the desired class to see the current status (Available, RAC, Waiting List) and fare for that date.

Step 3: Selecting Your Train, Class, and Berth Preference

After finding a suitable train with available seats, click on the 'Book Now' button next to your chosen class and date. This will take you to the passenger details page. Here, you will be prompted to enter the details of all passengers travelling. For each passenger, provide their name, age, gender, and berth preference (e.g., Lower, Middle, Upper, Side Lower, Side Upper), if any. You can add up to six passengers in one booking. If you are travelling with children under 5 years, you can add their details, but they do not require a separate berth unless specified. Review all entered passenger details carefully to avoid any errors that could cause issues during travel. You can also opt for travel insurance at a nominal fee, which is highly recommended for safety and peace of mind.

Step 4: Reviewing Booking Details and Making Payment

Before proceeding to payment, you'll reach a review page displaying all your entered details: train number, names of passengers, journey date, class, and fare breakdown. Take a moment to re-verify everything, especially the journey date and passenger names. Any discrepancies can lead to complications later. Below the review section, you'll find various payment options. IRCTC supports multiple payment methods, including credit cards, debit cards, Net Banking, and UPI. Select your preferred payment gateway. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the payment. Once the payment is successful, you will be redirected to the IRCTC confirmation page, and you will receive an SMS and email with your ticket details, including your PNR number. This PNR is crucial for checking your booking status and for travel.

Step 5: Accessing and Managing Your Tickets

After a successful booking, your e-ticket is generated. You can usually find a 'Print Ticket' option on the confirmation page. Additionally, a copy of your e-ticket will be sent to your registered email ID. For convenience, you can show the e-ticket on your smartphone or carry a printout. The SMS with your PNR is also valid for travel. If you need to access past bookings or manage current ones, log into your IRCTC account and navigate to the 'My Transactions' or 'Booked Ticket History' section. From here, you can view, print, or cancel your tickets. Remember that cancellation rules and charges apply, which vary based on the time remaining until departure. Always check the cancellation policy before proceeding.

Important Tips for a Smooth IRCTC Booking Experience

To ensure a smooth booking process, especially for popular routes or festive seasons, it’s advisable to book your tickets well in advance. Keep all passenger details, including names, ages, and IDs, ready before starting the booking process to save time. During peak hours, the IRCTC website can sometimes be slow; try booking during off-peak times or use the mobile app for a potentially faster experience. Always double-check your PNR status closer to your journey date, especially if your ticket was on a Waiting List or RAC. Be aware of dynamic pricing for certain premium trains like Rajdhani, Shatabdi, and Duronto, where fares can increase as seats fill up. Lastly, always use your own IRCTC account and avoid sharing your login credentials to protect your personal and financial information.

Conclusion

Booking train tickets online through IRCTC is a straightforward process once you understand the steps. By following this guide, you can confidently plan and book your rail journeys across India without any hassle. Remember to always use the official IRCTC platforms for security and to keep your login details safe. Happy travelling!
