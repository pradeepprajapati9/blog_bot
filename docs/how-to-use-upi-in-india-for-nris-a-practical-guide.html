How to Use UPI in India for NRIs: A Practical Guide

07 Jul 2026

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has revolutionized digital payments in India, offering a seamless and instant way to transfer money. For Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) visiting or managing affairs in India, UPI is an incredibly convenient tool. It eliminates the need to carry large amounts of cash and simplifies various transactions, from paying for groceries to utility bills. If you're an NRI looking to leverage the power of UPI, this guide will walk you through everything you need to know, from eligibility to setting up and making your first payment.

What is UPI and Why it Matters for NRIs

UPI is an instant real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). It allows you to link multiple bank accounts into a single mobile application, enabling you to send and receive money, pay bills, and make online purchases directly from your bank account. For NRIs, UPI offers immense convenience. Imagine no longer needing to hunt for ATMs, exchange currency for small purchases, or rely solely on credit cards. With UPI, you can pay instantly at millions of merchants by simply scanning a QR code or entering a UPI ID. This makes managing daily expenses, helping family, or handling property-related payments in India significantly easier and more efficient.

NRI Eligibility for UPI: Accounts and Mobile Numbers

To use UPI as an NRI, you primarily need an active NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) bank account in India. These accounts are specifically designed for NRIs. Crucially, your Indian mobile number must be linked to this NRE or NRO account. Traditionally, this required an active Indian SIM card. However, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has expanded this to include international mobile numbers from select countries (like USA, UK, UAE, Singapore, Australia, Canada, Hong Kong, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and others as added). Your bank must support this feature. Ensure your linked international or Indian mobile number is active and registered with your NRE/NRO account to facilitate the UPI registration and verification process.

Step-by-Step Guide to Setting Up UPI for NRIs

Setting up UPI is straightforward once you meet the eligibility criteria. First, download a UPI-enabled app from your mobile app store. Popular options include BHIM (the official NPCI app), Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm. Next, open the app and select the option to add a bank account. Choose your Indian bank where your NRE/NRO account is held. The app will then verify your mobile number. If you are using an Indian SIM, it will automatically detect the number. If you're using an international number, ensure it's registered with your bank and that the app supports it. After successful verification, select your NRE/NRO account. You will then be prompted to link your debit card details (last 6 digits and expiry date) to set up your UPI PIN. This 4 or 6-digit PIN is crucial for authorizing all your UPI transactions.

Making Common UPI Transactions in India

Once your UPI is set up, you can start making various transactions. The most common method is scanning a QR code. Look for the UPI QR code at shops, restaurants, or even for street vendors. Open your UPI app, select 'Scan & Pay', scan the code, enter the amount, and confirm with your UPI PIN. You can also send money directly to someone's UPI ID (a unique identifier like 'name@bankname' or a mobile number registered for UPI). For utility bills (like electricity or water bills for your property in India), many UPI apps allow you to pay directly by selecting the biller and entering the consumer details. You can also make online purchases on Indian e-commerce sites that accept UPI payments, simply by selecting UPI at checkout and approving the payment through your app.

Important Considerations and Security Tips

While UPI offers immense convenience, it's vital to be aware of certain aspects. Most banks and UPI apps have a daily transaction limit, typically around INR 1 lakh (100,000 rupees) for general transactions, though this can vary. For your security, never share your UPI PIN with anyone. Your PIN is for authorizing payments, not for receiving them. Be cautious of phishing attempts or fake QR codes. Always double-check the recipient's name and amount before confirming any transaction. Ensure your UPI app is secured with a screen lock or fingerprint authentication. If you change your mobile number or bank account, remember to update it within your UPI app and with your bank to avoid any service interruptions.

Troubleshooting Common UPI Issues for NRIs

Occasionally, you might encounter issues while using UPI. If a transaction fails, first check your internet connection. Ensure your mobile data or Wi-Fi is stable. Next, verify your UPI PIN. Entering an incorrect PIN multiple times can temporarily block your account. You can usually reset your UPI PIN through your app using your debit card details. If the payment shows as 'pending', wait a few minutes, as sometimes there can be delays. Check your bank account statement to confirm if the money has been debited or if a refund has been initiated. If problems persist, contact your bank's customer support or the support service for your UPI app. They can provide specific details about transaction failures or account issues.

Managing Your NRE/NRO Accounts with UPI

Using UPI for your NRE/NRO accounts helps streamline your financial activities in India. Remember that NRE accounts are fully repatriable, meaning the funds can be freely transferred back abroad, while NRO accounts are for income earned in India and are generally non-repatriable beyond certain limits. When using UPI, the funds will be debited directly from your linked NRE or NRO account in Indian Rupees. This makes it easy to track your spending and manage your Indian finances without the hassle of currency conversion at the point of sale. It's a truly practical way for NRIs to stay connected to India's digital economy.

Conclusion

UPI has emerged as an indispensable payment method in India, and for NRIs, it's a gateway to hassle-free transactions and greater control over their finances while in the country or managing affairs remotely. By understanding the eligibility criteria, following the simple setup process, and being mindful of security, you can confidently use UPI for all your payment needs. Embrace this convenient digital tool to make your visits to India smoother and your financial management simpler.
