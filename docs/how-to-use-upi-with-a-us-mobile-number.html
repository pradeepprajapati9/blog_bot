How to Use UPI with a US Mobile Number

25 Jul 2026

The convenience of UPI (Unified Payments Interface) has transformed digital payments in India. If you have an Indian bank account but use a US mobile number, or if you're an NRI wondering about UPI access, you're likely asking: "Can I use UPI with my US number?" The answer isn't a simple yes or no; it depends on your specific circumstances, particularly whether you are a Non-Resident Indian (NRI) with an NRE or NRO bank account. Let's break down how UPI typically works and the recent provisions that might allow you to link your international number.

The Standard Requirement: Indian Mobile Number and Bank Account

For most residents in India, registering for UPI is straightforward: you need an active Indian bank account and an Indian mobile number linked to that account. Your UPI app (like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or your bank's app) verifies your identity by sending an SMS from your registered mobile number. This linkage is crucial for security and authentication. Without an Indian mobile number, the system typically cannot complete the registration process, as it relies on SMS verification specific to Indian telecom networks and the KYC (Know Your Customer) details associated with your bank account. This fundamental requirement ensures that UPI transactions are secure and traceable within the Indian regulatory framework.

The NRI Exception: UPI for International Numbers (Including US)

Recognizing the growing needs of Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) made a significant announcement in January 2023. This update allows NRIs from certain countries to link their international mobile numbers to their NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) bank accounts for UPI services. The United States is one of the ten countries included in this provision. This means that if you are an eligible NRI, you might now be able to register for UPI using your US mobile number, provided your bank supports this feature. This expansion aims to simplify digital transactions for NRIs while they are in India or managing their finances remotely.

Who Can Use a US Number for UPI? Key Conditions

To be eligible to link a US mobile number to UPI, you must meet specific criteria. First and foremost, you must be a Non-Resident Indian (NRI) with an active NRE or NRO bank account in India. These accounts are designed for NRIs to manage their earnings and savings in India. Secondly, the bank where you hold your NRE/NRO account must have implemented support for international mobile numbers. While NPCI has enabled this facility, individual banks need to integrate it into their systems. You cannot use a US number with a regular resident savings account. Always confirm with your specific bank if they support UPI registration with a US number for your NRE/NRO account before proceeding.

Steps to Link Your US Number (If Eligible)

If you are an eligible NRI with an NRE/NRO account and your bank supports this feature, the process for linking your US number to UPI is similar to linking an Indian number. First, ensure your US mobile number is correctly updated and linked to your NRE/NRO bank account with your bank. You might need to visit your bank's branch or use their online portal to confirm this. Next, download a UPI app (like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or your bank's UPI-enabled app). During the registration process, the app will prompt you to verify your mobile number. Select your US number (which must be linked to your NRE/NRO account). The app will send an SMS to this number for verification. Once verified, you can proceed to link your NRE/NRO bank account and set up your UPI PIN.

What If You Don't Qualify? Alternatives for Visitors and Others

If you are not an NRI with an NRE/NRO account, or if your Indian bank does not yet support linking international numbers, you generally cannot use your US mobile number for UPI. For short-term visitors or those without specific NRI bank accounts, the most practical solution is to obtain an Indian SIM card. You can easily purchase a prepaid Indian SIM with a valid visa and passport. Once you have an Indian mobile number, you can link it to an Indian savings account (if you have one) and then register for UPI. This allows you to fully utilize UPI for all your transactions in India, offering the same convenience as any resident. Remember to complete the necessary KYC for your Indian SIM card.

Important Considerations and Security Tips

Even when eligible, always prioritize security. Ensure your UPI app is downloaded from official app stores. Never share your UPI PIN or OTPs with anyone. Be cautious of phishing attempts or suspicious links. While using an international number, be aware of potential SMS charges from your US provider for verification messages. Keep your contact details, including your US number, updated with your Indian bank. If you face any issues linking your US number, contact your bank's customer support directly, as they are best equipped to assist with specific account and service-related queries for NRIs. Remember, the rules and supported countries for international numbers on UPI may evolve, so staying updated with NPCI and your bank's announcements is advisable.

Conclusion

Using your US mobile number for UPI in India is now possible, but primarily for eligible Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) who have NRE or NRO bank accounts and whose banks support this feature. For others, particularly visitors or those with regular resident accounts, an Indian mobile number remains essential for UPI registration and usage. Always verify your eligibility and bank's support, and prioritize secure digital practices. UPI continues to evolve, making digital payments more accessible for a wider range of users, including the global Indian diaspora.
