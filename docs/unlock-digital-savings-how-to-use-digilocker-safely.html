Unlock Digital Savings: How to Use DigiLocker Safely

05 Oct 2026

In today's digital age, managing important documents can feel like a constant paper chase. From your Aadhaar card to your educational certificates, keeping track of them all can be a hassle. Thankfully, the Indian government has introduced DigiLocker, a secure digital platform to store and access your official documents. This guide will walk you through how to use DigiLocker safely and effectively, making your life simpler and more organised.

What is DigiLocker and Why Use It?

DigiLocker is a virtual locker offered by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India. It allows you to store and share your digital documents, such as driving licenses, PAN cards, and academic certificates, in a secure online space. This means you can stop carrying physical copies everywhere. Instead, you can access and present these digital versions whenever needed, whether for verification, applications, or just for your own record-keeping. It's a significant step towards a paperless India and a convenient way to manage your essential paperwork.

Getting Started: Sign Up and Verification

To begin using DigiLocker, you need to download the app or visit the website and sign up. The process is straightforward. You'll need your mobile number, which must be linked to your Aadhaar card, as Aadhaar is crucial for verification. You'll receive an OTP (One-Time Password) on your registered mobile number to verify your identity. After verifying your mobile number, you'll be asked to create a username and a secure password. It's essential to choose a strong password that you can remember but is difficult for others to guess. Follow these steps carefully to ensure your account is set up correctly.

Adding Your Documents: From Government to Personal

Once your account is active, you can start adding your documents. DigiLocker allows you to fetch documents directly from government issuers, like your Aadhaar, PAN card, and mark sheets from various boards and universities. These are 'issued documents' and are highly reliable. You can also upload your own documents, such as medical records, property papers, or any other important certificates, into your 'personal documents' section. Remember to categorise and name these files clearly for easy retrieval later. Ensure all uploaded documents are in acceptable formats like PDF, JPG, or PNG.

Sharing Your Documents Securely

The primary benefit of DigiLocker is the ability to share your documents digitally and securely. When an organisation or authority requires a copy of your certificate, they can request it through DigiLocker. You'll receive a notification and can then grant access to the specific document for a limited period. This process is much more secure than sharing photocopies, as it's directly from the issuer or your own verified digital copy. Always check who is requesting your document and what purpose it's for before granting access. This controlled sharing minimises the risk of misuse.

Security Best Practices for DigiLocker

While DigiLocker is designed with robust security features, it's crucial for users to follow best practices. Always use a strong, unique password and avoid sharing it with anyone. Enable the two-factor authentication option if available, which adds an extra layer of security. Be cautious of phishing attempts; the DigiLocker team will never ask for your password or OTP via email or phone. Log out of your account when you are finished using it, especially on public computers. Regularly review your account activity and reported issued documents to ensure everything is as expected.

Troubleshooting Common Issues

Users might encounter a few common issues. If you're not receiving OTPs, ensure your mobile number is correctly linked with Aadhaar and check your network signal. If a document isn't appearing after being issued, there might be a delay from the issuer's end, or your Aadhaar details might not perfectly match the issuer's records. For forgotten passwords, the 'Forgot Password' link on the login page is your solution, which will send an OTP to your registered mobile. If you face persistent problems, the DigiLocker 'Help' section or their support channels can provide assistance.

Conclusion

DigiLocker is an invaluable tool for every Indian citizen looking to streamline their document management. By understanding how to sign up, add documents, share them securely, and follow safety protocols, you can experience a paperless and organised digital life. Embrace this government initiative to simplify your daily interactions and keep your important credentials safe and accessible.
