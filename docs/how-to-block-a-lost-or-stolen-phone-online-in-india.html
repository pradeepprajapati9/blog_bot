How to Block a Lost or Stolen Phone Online in India

16 Oct 2026

Losing a smartphone can be an overwhelming experience. Beyond the monetary loss of the device itself, our phones carry sensitive personal photos, banking applications, social media accounts, and private messages. Thankfully, the Department of Telecommunications, Government of India, has made it significantly easier to safeguard your information through the Central Equipment Identity Register, accessible via the Sanchar Saathi portal. This nationwide initiative allows any citizen to remotely block their lost or stolen phone using its unique International Mobile Equipment Identity number. Once blocked, the device becomes completely unusable on any cellular network in India, rendering it practically useless to thieves. In this guide, you will learn the exact steps to prepare your documents, file a block request online, check the verification status, and even unblock the handset if you happen to recover it.

Essential First Steps: File an FIR and Get a Duplicate SIM

Before you can report your device on the central government portal, you must complete two foundational tasks. First, file a lost property report or an e-FIR with your local state police department. Most state police portals now allow you to report a lost mobile online and generate an immediate digital acknowledgment receipt, which is mandatory for the blocking process. Second, visit an official retail store of your telecom operator—such as Airtel, Jio, Vi, or BSNL—and apply for a duplicate SIM card with the same mobile number. When you submit your online request on the Sanchar Saathi portal, the system sends an essential one-time password to this primary mobile number to verify your identity. Having your replacement SIM active inside another working handset ensures you can receive this verification code without delays. Completing these two steps immediately also prevents unauthorized misuse of your phone number for banking fraud or identity theft.

Documents and Information You Need to Keep Handy

To ensure your blocking application goes through smoothly without rejection, collect all required information before opening the portal. The most critical detail is your phone fifteen-digit IMEI number. If your handset supported dual SIM cards, you will need both IMEI numbers, which can be found printed on the original retail packaging box or on the purchase invoice. Next, keep a scanned copy or a clear photograph of the police complaint receipt ready in PDF or image format. You will also need a digital copy of a valid government-issued identity proof, such as your Aadhaar card, PAN card, voter ID, or driving license. While not strictly mandatory, uploading your original mobile purchase bill is strongly recommended because it speeds up the backend ownership verification. Ensure that all uploaded document images are legible and within the specified file size limit, typically under one megabyte per file.

Step-by-Step Process to Block Your Mobile on Sanchar Saathi

Open your web browser and navigate to the official Sanchar Saathi website at sancharsaathi.gov.in. Scroll to the citizen-centric services section and select the option labeled Block Stolen or Lost Mobile. This action will redirect you to the CEIR portal request form. The form is divided into three distinct segments. First, enter the device details, including your lost mobile numbers, both IMEI numbers, device brand, and model. Next, fill in the incident information, specifying the date, time, state, district, and police station where the device was lost, along with the police complaint number and the uploaded copy of the complaint. Finally, provide your personal information as the owner, including your name, current residential address, identity card type, and identity card upload. Enter an alternate mobile number to receive the verification OTP, enter the OTP, check the declaration box, and click submit.

What Happens After You Submit the Blocking Request

Once you submit the form, the system immediately generates a unique Request ID. Make sure to take a screenshot and note this ID down in a secure place, as it is required for all future tracking and unblocking actions. The CEIR database automatically adds your device IMEI numbers to a nationwide central blacklist shared across all active Indian telecom service providers. This means that even if someone formats the smartphone, replaces the physical SIM card, or installs an eSIM from any network carrier in the country, the phone will fail to connect to cellular towers for calls, SMS, or mobile data. Furthermore, whenever an unauthorized person attempts to activate a SIM card inside the blocked device, the telecom network automatically flags the attempt and forwards the location coordinates to the relevant law enforcement agencies to assist in phone tracing.

How to Check the Status of Your Blocking Request

After submitting your request, it typically takes between twenty-four to forty-eight hours for the telecom operators to synchronize their equipment registers and complete the blocking protocol. You can easily monitor the progress of your application through the Sanchar Saathi portal at any point. Visit sancharsaathi.gov.in, choose the CEIR service, and click on the option that says Check Request Status. You will be prompted to enter your unique Request ID that was generated during the initial submission. After entering the captcha code and confirming, the portal will display the exact real-time status of your request, such as pending verification, approved, or blocked. If there is any discrepancy in your police complaint copy or personal identity proof, the status screen will highlight the reason, allowing you to rectify and re-upload the necessary documents promptly.

How to Unblock the Device if You Find It Again

In many instances, citizens recover their lost phones through police retrieval, lost-and-found desks, or after misplacing them in a public taxi or office. If you are fortunate enough to recover your handset, you must unblock the IMEI before you can use cellular services again. Return to the Sanchar Saathi portal and click on the Unblock Found Mobile option. You will need to enter your original Request ID, the mobile number you provided for OTP verification during the initial blocking request, and select the reason for unblocking from the drop-down menu, such as recovered by police or found by self. After completing the mobile OTP verification, submit the request. Within a few hours to a day, the IMEI number is removed from the central blacklist, and your smartphone will seamlessly connect back to your regular mobile network.

Conclusion

Losing a smartphone is stressful, but government initiatives like Sanchar Saathi have given Indian consumers genuine control over their device security. By taking prompt action to get a replacement SIM, filing an e-FIR, and blocking your IMEI via the CEIR portal, you effectively neutralize the resale and misuse value of your phone. To prepare for any future emergency, take a minute today to write down your phone IMEI numbers by dialing star-hash-zero-six-hash on your dialer and saving the details somewhere accessible outside your phone.
