How to Use UPI Offline: A Simple Guide

26 Nov 2026

In India, UPI (Unified Payments Interface) has become a revolutionary way to transfer money. But what happens when you don't have an internet connection or your mobile data is off? Worry not! It's still possible to use UPI. This guide will walk you through the surprisingly simple method of using UPI offline, so you can manage your payments anytime, anywhere, without relying on data or Wi-Fi.

Understanding UPI Offline (USSD Method)

The primary way to use UPI offline is through the USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data) service. This technology has been around for a while and works by sending codes through your phone's dialer. Unlike regular UPI payments that require an internet connection to communicate with the UPI network, USSD payments use the existing cellular network signals. This means as long as you have basic mobile network coverage, you can make UPI transactions. It's a fallback system designed to ensure financial inclusion and accessibility, especially in areas with poor or no internet connectivity. It’s a secure and widely adopted method that many banks have integrated into their services for their customers.

Prerequisites for Offline UPI

Before you can start using UPI offline, there are a few crucial things you need to ensure. Firstly, your bank must support the USSD-based UPI service. Most major banks in India do, but it's always best to confirm with your bank's customer care or check their website. Secondly, you need to have registered your mobile number with your bank and linked it to your bank account for UPI services. This is usually done through your bank's app or by visiting a branch. Finally, your SIM card needs to be active and have basic network connectivity. You don't need a specific app for this method; it works directly through your phone's dialer, making it accessible to almost all feature phones and smartphones.

Setting Up Your UPI PIN via USSD

Just like online UPI, you'll need a UPI PIN to authorize your offline transactions. If you haven't set one up or need to reset it, you can do so using the USSD method. The general code to start this process is *99#. After dialing this code, you will see a menu. Look for options related to 'Bank Account Services' or 'Manage UPI PIN'. You'll then be prompted to enter the last six digits of your debit card and its expiry date to set or change your UPI PIN. Remember to choose a strong PIN that is easy for you to remember but difficult for others to guess. This PIN is your key to authorizing all your UPI transactions, both online and offline.

How to Send Money Offline

To send money using UPI offline, dial *99# from your registered mobile number. You will be presented with a menu of options. Select the option for 'Money Transfer' or 'Send Money'. You'll then need to choose the method to identify the recipient. This could be via their bank account number and IFSC code, or if they have a UPI ID registered, you can use that. Next, enter the amount you wish to send. Finally, you will be asked to enter your UPI PIN to authorize the transaction. Once authorized, the money will be transferred, and you will receive a confirmation message on your phone. This process works seamlessly even without an active internet connection.

How to Receive Money Offline (and Check Balance)

While you can initiate sending money offline, receiving money is typically initiated by the sender. However, you can check your UPI transaction history and bank account balance offline. Dial *99# and select 'Account Statement' or 'Check Balance' from the menu. You'll be prompted to enter your UPI PIN. After successful authentication, your account balance or recent transaction details will be displayed on your phone screen. This is incredibly useful if you need to quickly verify if a payment has been received or to know your available funds when you're on the go and offline. It ensures you have visibility into your finances at all times.

Important Considerations and Limitations

While the USSD method for UPI is a lifesaver, it's important to be aware of its limitations. Transactions might take slightly longer to process compared to online UPI payments, as they rely on the cellular network's responsiveness. The interface is text-based and less visually appealing than app-based UPI, which might be a learning curve for some. Also, not all banks offer the full range of features via USSD; some advanced functionalities might only be available through their apps. Always ensure you are using the correct USSD codes provided by your bank and keep your UPI PIN secure. If you encounter any issues, contacting your bank's customer support is the best course of action.

Conclusion

Using UPI offline through the USSD service is a practical and accessible way to manage your money without needing constant internet access. It ensures that you can make payments and check your balance even in remote areas or during network outages. By understanding the simple steps and requirements, you can confidently use this feature to stay connected with your finances. Remember to keep your UPI PIN secure and confirm with your bank about their specific USSD services.
