How to Use UPI in India for NRIs

07 Jul 2026

Are you an Non-Resident Indian (NRI) looking for a quick and secure way to manage payments in India? The Unified Payments Interface (UPI) is your answer. UPI has revolutionized digital payments, making transactions instant, easy, and reliable. For NRIs, it offers a convenient bridge to handle expenses, support family, or manage investments back home without the hassle of traditional banking methods. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to get started with UPI in India.

What is UPI and Why it's Great for NRIs

UPI is a real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). It allows you to link multiple bank accounts to a single mobile application and make instant money transfers, pay bills, or purchase goods and services directly from your smartphone. For NRIs, UPI provides unparalleled convenience. You can pay utility bills for your family, manage property-related expenses, send money to relatives, or even shop online at Indian merchants – all from abroad or during your visits. It eliminates the need for carrying cash, making physical visits to banks, or relying on slower transaction methods, offering a secure and efficient digital payment experience.

Key Requirements for NRIs to Use UPI

Before you can start using UPI, there are a few essential requirements specific to Non-Resident Indians. Firstly, you must have an NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) bank account with an Indian bank. These are the designated accounts for NRIs to manage their finances in India. Secondly, and critically, you need an Indian mobile number. This mobile number must be linked to your NRE/NRO bank account and registered with your bank for SMS banking and UPI services. Recently, NPCI has also enabled certain international mobile numbers from specific countries to be linked for UPI, easing the requirement for an Indian SIM in all cases, but the NRE/NRO account linkage remains paramount. Always check with your bank for the latest international mobile number eligibility.

Setting Up UPI for NRIs: Step-by-Step Guide

Setting up UPI is straightforward once you meet the eligibility criteria. First, download a UPI-enabled payment app such as BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or your bank's own UPI app from your app store. Open the app and select your NRE/NRO bank. The app will verify your Indian mobile number (or eligible international number) linked to your bank account via an SMS. Ensure this number is active and capable of receiving SMS. Once verified, you'll be prompted to link your bank account. You will need your debit card details (last 6 digits and expiry date) to set up your UPI PIN. This 4 or 6-digit PIN is crucial for authorizing all your UPI transactions, so choose something memorable yet secure.

Linking Your NRE/NRO Account with UPI

The core of using UPI as an NRI lies in correctly linking your NRE or NRO account. When you register on a UPI app, after your mobile number is verified, the app will fetch bank accounts associated with that number. Select your NRE or NRO account from the list. If you have multiple accounts, ensure you pick the correct one. The app will then ask you to set up your UPI PIN using your debit card details. This PIN acts as your digital signature and is required for every transaction. Remember, your linked mobile number and bank account must be fully functional and KYC compliant to avoid any transaction failures. Do not share your UPI PIN or debit card details with anyone.

Making Payments with UPI from India or Abroad

Once your UPI is set up, making payments is simple. Open your chosen UPI app and select the 'Send Money' or 'Pay' option. You can pay by entering the recipient's Virtual Payment Address (VPA), their bank account number and IFSC code, or by scanning a QR code. Enter the amount you wish to transfer and a brief remark if needed. The app will display the transaction details for your review. Confirm the payment by entering your UPI PIN. The transaction will be processed instantly, and both you and the recipient will receive confirmation notifications. This process works seamlessly whether you are physically in India or managing payments remotely, provided you have internet access and your registered mobile number is active.

Common UPI Use Cases for NRIs

UPI offers a multitude of practical uses for NRIs. You can pay utility bills for properties or family members in India, including electricity, water, and internet bills. It's incredibly convenient for sending money to parents or relatives for their daily expenses, making it easier to support them from a distance. When you visit India, UPI allows you to make instant payments at shops, restaurants, and for transport, eliminating the need to carry large amounts of cash. Furthermore, you can use UPI for recharges (mobile, DTH) and even for certain online purchases on Indian e-commerce platforms, making your financial interactions with India much smoother and more efficient.

Security Tips and Best Practices for UPI

While UPI is highly secure, following best practices can enhance your safety. Always keep your UPI PIN confidential and never share it with anyone, not even bank officials. Do not fall for phishing attempts or scams asking for your PIN or OTPs. Regularly review your transaction history within the UPI app and your bank statements to spot any unauthorized activity. Use strong passwords for your phone and UPI apps. When scanning QR codes, always verify the merchant's name before approving the payment. If you lose your phone or suspect unauthorized activity, immediately block your UPI services through your bank's customer service. Staying vigilant ensures a safe and secure UPI experience.

Conclusion

UPI stands out as an indispensable tool for NRIs, simplifying financial transactions in India significantly. By understanding the key requirements—having an NRE/NRO account and a linked Indian or eligible international mobile number—and following the setup process, you can gain instant control over your Indian finances. Embrace the convenience, speed, and security that UPI offers to manage everything from bill payments to family support with ease. Always prioritize security by safeguarding your UPI PIN and staying aware of common scams. UPI truly bridges the distance, making India more accessible than ever for NRIs.
