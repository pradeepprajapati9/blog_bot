How to Use UPI Without Internet: Your Guide to *99#

28 Aug 2026

In our increasingly digital world, UPI has become the go-to method for everyday payments in India. But what happens when you’re in an area with no internet, or your data plan runs out? The thought of being unable to pay or transfer money can be stressful. The good news is, you can still use UPI without an active internet connection! This guide will walk you through the simple, effective method that ensures you’re never stranded without your digital payment options. Get ready to learn about the powerful *99# service.

The Challenge of Offline Digital Payments in India

While India has seen a massive leap in digital payment adoption, reliable internet connectivity remains a challenge in many parts of the country. From rural areas with limited network infrastructure to urban settings experiencing temporary outages, being offline is a common scenario. For many, UPI is synonymous with online transactions, creating a barrier when internet access is absent. Imagine needing to pay a vendor, send money to a family member, or even check your balance, only to find your internet isn't working. This gap highlights a crucial need for a robust, accessible solution that allows users to leverage the convenience of UPI, irrespective of their internet status. Fortunately, such a solution exists, designed specifically for these situations.

Introducing *99# Service: Your UPI Lifeline Without Internet

The answer to making UPI payments without internet is the National Unified USSD Platform (NUUP), commonly accessed by dialing *99#. This innovative service, launched by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India), allows any mobile phone user, whether they have a smartphone or a basic feature phone, to use UPI. It operates entirely on the USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data) protocol, which is a GSM communication technology that works over a mobile network connection, just like making a call or sending an SMS. This means you don't need a data connection or even a smartphone app to send or receive money, check your balance, or manage your UPI PIN. All you need is your registered mobile number linked to your bank account.

Step-by-Step Guide to Using *99# for UPI Payments

Using the *99# service is straightforward. Here’s how you can make a UPI payment when offline: 1. **Dial *99#:** From your registered mobile number, dial *99#. 2. **Select Language:** A menu will appear. Choose your preferred language (e.g., 1 for English, 2 for Hindi). 3. **Bank Selection:** The system will prompt you to enter the first 3-4 letters of your bank's name or choose from a list if available. For example, 'SBI' for State Bank of India. 4. **Choose Service:** Once your bank is selected, a new menu appears. Select 'Send Money' (usually option 1). 5. **Enter Recipient Details:** You can choose to send money using the recipient's mobile number, UPI ID, or account number and IFSC code. Select your preferred option and enter the details. 6. **Enter Amount:** Input the amount you wish to transfer. 7. **Add Remarks (Optional):** You might be asked to enter a remark, like 'Rent' or 'Groceries'. 8. **Enter UPI PIN:** Finally, enter your 4 or 6-digit UPI PIN to authorise the transaction. 9. **Confirmation:** You will receive a confirmation message on your screen and via SMS once the transaction is successful. Remember, your mobile number must be registered with UPI and linked to your bank account for this service to work.

What You Can Do with *99# (and What You Can't)

The *99# service is incredibly versatile for offline transactions, but it does have some specific capabilities and limitations: **What you CAN do:** * **Send Money:** To a mobile number, UPI ID, or bank account + IFSC. * **Check Account Balance:** Quickly view your available balance. * **View Mini Statement:** Get a quick overview of your last few transactions. * **Change UPI PIN:** Update your security PIN as needed. * **Generate UPI ID:** For users who haven't created one through an app yet. **What you CAN'T do easily:** * **Scan QR Codes:** This requires a camera and a smartphone app, not possible with USSD. * **Direct Merchant Payments:** While you can send money to a merchant's mobile number, it's not as seamless as scanning a QR code at a shop. * **High-Value Transactions:** There's usually a daily transaction limit, typically Rs. 5,000 per transaction, which is lower than app-based UPI limits. Always check with your bank for exact limits.

Important Tips for Secure Offline UPI Transactions

Security is paramount when dealing with any financial transaction, even offline ones. Here are some essential tips to keep your *99# UPI payments safe: * **Use Your Registered Number Only:** Always ensure you dial *99# from the mobile number registered with your bank and linked to UPI. Transactions from unregistered numbers will fail. * **Keep Your UPI PIN Secret:** Never share your UPI PIN with anyone, not even bank officials or customer service representatives. Your PIN is the key to your money. * **Verify Recipient Details:** Double-check the recipient's mobile number, UPI ID, or bank account details before confirming the transaction. A wrong digit can lead to money being sent to the wrong person, and recovery can be difficult. * **Beware of Scams:** Be cautious of unsolicited calls or messages asking you to dial *99# to 'receive money' or 'verify your account.' You only need to enter your PIN when *sending* money, never to receive it. * **Check Confirmation SMS:** Always wait for the transaction confirmation SMS to ensure your payment was successful and for your records.

Is UPI Lite 'Offline'? Clarifying the Difference

You might have heard of UPI Lite as another feature related to faster payments. It's important to understand how it differs from the *99# service, especially regarding 'offline' capabilities. UPI Lite is an 'on-device' wallet feature within UPI payment apps (like Paytm, Google Pay, PhonePe, etc.) that allows users to make small-value transactions (typically up to Rs. 500 per transaction, with a daily limit of Rs. 2,000) directly from their wallet balance without requiring their bank's UPI PIN for every transaction. While this makes transactions faster and can work even with slightly weaker internet signals, **UPI Lite still fundamentally requires an internet connection to load money into the wallet and for the merchant to receive immediate transaction confirmation.** The 'offline' aspect here refers to the reduced dependency on bank systems for individual transaction approvals, making it quicker, but not truly independent of the internet for the complete payment cycle. In contrast, *99# is designed for situations where there is absolutely no internet data connection available, making it the truly 'offline' UPI solution.

Conclusion

Being able to use UPI without an internet connection is a game-changer, especially in a country like India where digital inclusion is vital. The *99# USSD service empowers millions, ensuring that essential financial transactions are never hampered by a lack of internet access. By understanding how to use this simple yet powerful tool, you can navigate your daily finances with greater confidence and convenience. Always remember to prioritize security, keep your PIN safe, and verify details carefully. Now, even without the internet, UPI is always within your reach.
