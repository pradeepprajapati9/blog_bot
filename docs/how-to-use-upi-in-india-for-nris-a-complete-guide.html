How to Use UPI in India for NRIs: A Complete Guide

06 Jul 2026

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has revolutionized digital payments in India, making transactions incredibly fast and convenient. For Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) visiting or managing finances in India, UPI offers a seamless way to handle everyday expenses, send money, and pay bills. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to start using UPI in India as an NRI, ensuring you can enjoy the ease of digital payments just like a local resident.

What is UPI and Why It's Great for NRIs?

UPI is an instant real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). It allows you to link multiple bank accounts into a single mobile application and make payments directly from your bank account to another, using a Virtual Payment Address (VPA) or QR codes. For NRIs, UPI is a game-changer. It eliminates the need to carry cash for small transactions, simplifies bill payments, and makes shopping hassle-free. Whether you're paying for groceries, dining out, or even transferring money to family, UPI offers instant settlement, 24/7 availability, and robust security features, making your financial interactions in India significantly smoother and more efficient.

Eligibility Requirements for NRIs to Use UPI

To use UPI in India, NRIs must meet specific criteria. The most crucial requirement is having an active Non-Resident External (NRE) or Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO) bank account in India. These accounts allow you to manage your funds in Indian Rupees (INR). Additionally, you need an active Indian mobile number. This mobile number must be linked to your NRE or NRO bank account, as it is essential for the registration process, receiving One-Time Passwords (OTPs), and authenticating transactions. Without an Indian bank account (NRE/NRO) and an Indian mobile number linked to it, you will not be able to set up or use UPI services.

Getting Started: Linking Your Indian Bank Account

Once you have an NRE or NRO bank account and an active Indian mobile number linked to it, the next step is to link this account to a UPI application. First, ensure your Indian mobile number is registered with your bank account. If not, contact your bank to update your records. Next, download a popular UPI-enabled app from your smartphone's app store. Options include BHIM UPI (the official app), Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm. When you open the app, it will typically ask to verify your mobile number by sending an SMS from your registered Indian SIM card. This SMS confirms that the phone number is active and belongs to you, so ensure your Indian SIM is in the phone you're using and has active roaming if you're setting up from abroad.

Activating UPI on Your Smartphone and Setting PIN

After successful mobile number verification, the UPI app will fetch details of bank accounts linked to that number. Select your NRE or NRO account from the list. The app will then guide you to set up your UPI PIN. This is a 4 or 6-digit number that you will use to authorize all your UPI transactions, similar to an ATM PIN. You'll typically be asked to enter your debit card details (last few digits and expiry date) associated with your NRE/NRO account to generate this PIN. Choose a strong, memorable PIN that you don't share with anyone. Once your UPI PIN is set, your UPI account is active, and you are ready to start making transactions.

How NRIs Can Make UPI Payments in India

Using UPI to make payments is straightforward. There are several ways to pay: 1. Scan and Pay: Many merchants display a UPI QR code. Simply open your UPI app, select the 'Scan' option, scan the QR code, enter the amount, and confirm with your UPI PIN. 2. Pay to VPA: If you know the recipient's Virtual Payment Address (e.g., recipientname@bankname), you can enter it directly, input the amount, and authorize the payment. 3. Pay to Bank Account/Mobile Number: Some apps allow you to send money directly to a bank account number with IFSC code or to a mobile number registered for UPI. Remember, all transactions will be debited from your linked NRE or NRO account in Indian Rupees.

Important Considerations and Tips for NRIs

While UPI is incredibly convenient, keep these points in mind: 1. Active Indian SIM: Your Indian mobile number must remain active to receive transaction alerts and OTPs for security, especially if you need to reset your PIN. Ensure your international roaming is active if you're using UPI while outside India. 2. Security: Never share your UPI PIN with anyone. Be wary of phishing attempts or fake QR codes. Only use trusted UPI apps. 3. Transaction Limits: Banks and UPI apps typically have daily transaction limits, which vary. Familiarize yourself with these limits for your specific bank and app. 4. Internet Connection: A stable internet connection is required to process UPI transactions. 5. Bank Support: If you encounter issues with your NRE/NRO account or linking it, contact your Indian bank's NRI support desk for assistance.

Common Questions for NRIs Using UPI

Can I use my international mobile number? No, an Indian mobile number linked to your NRE/NRO account is mandatory for UPI registration and use. What if my Indian SIM is not active? You will not be able to register or receive OTPs. You must reactivate your SIM or get a new one linked to your account. Can I pay for international transactions using UPI? No, UPI is currently for transactions within India, in Indian Rupees. What happens if I forget my UPI PIN? Most UPI apps allow you to reset your PIN using your debit card details linked to your NRE/NRO account. You'll need access to your registered Indian mobile number for OTP verification during the reset process. Ensure you keep your bank account details updated with your current mobile number.

Conclusion

UPI offers unparalleled convenience for NRIs managing their finances and making payments in India. By understanding the eligibility criteria, linking your NRE/NRO bank account, and using a secure UPI application, you can enjoy seamless digital transactions whether you're visiting for a short trip or managing long-term financial commitments. Embrace the simplicity and efficiency of UPI to make your financial interactions in India smoother and more modern.
