Covid Certificate: How to Download Easily in India

14 Oct 2026

In today's world, having a digital record of your COVID-19 vaccination is essential for travel, work, and sometimes even entry into public places. Fortunately, the Indian government has made it simple to access and download your vaccination certificate. This guide will walk you through the easiest ways to get your COVID certificate, ensuring you have this important document readily available.

Using the CoWIN Portal for Your Certificate

The CoWIN (COVID-19 Vaccine Intelligence Network) portal is the primary platform for vaccination registration and certificate downloads in India. To get your certificate, visit the official CoWIN website. You will need to log in using the mobile number you registered with for your vaccination. Enter the One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your number. Once logged in, you will see your vaccination details. Look for an option to 'Download Certificate' next to each dose you've received. Clicking this will generate a PDF file of your certificate. It's a straightforward process that ensures you get an official and verifiable document.

Downloading via the Aarogya Setu App

The Aarogya Setu app, initially designed for COVID-19 contact tracing, also allows you to download your vaccination certificate. If you have the app installed, open it and navigate to the 'Vaccination' tab. You will likely need to verify your mobile number again with an OTP. Once authenticated, you can view your vaccination status and find an option to download your certificate. This is a convenient method, especially if you're already using the app for health-related information. The downloaded certificate is the same official PDF file you would get from CoWIN.

Leveraging the UMANG App for Your Certificate

UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) is another government-integrated app that simplifies access to various services, including your COVID-19 vaccination certificate. Download and install the UMANG app from your smartphone's app store. Open the app and search for 'CoWIN' or 'Vaccination Certificate'. You will be prompted to enter your mobile number used for vaccination and verify it with an OTP. After successful verification, you can access your vaccination records and download your certificate directly within the UMANG app. This consolidates many government services in one place.

What to Do If You Didn't Register on CoWIN

In cases where you might have been vaccinated at a facility that handled registration differently, or if you encountered issues with your initial registration, there are still options. Generally, your vaccination is linked to your mobile number. You should still try the methods above using the mobile number provided at the time of vaccination. If you face persistent issues, you can contact the helplines provided on the CoWIN website or visit a designated vaccination center for assistance. They can help you locate your records and guide you through the download process or re-issuance if necessary.

Understanding Your COVID Certificate Details

Your COVID-19 vaccination certificate contains crucial information. It includes your name, age, gender, the date of your vaccination doses, the name of the vaccine administered (e.g., Covishield, Covaxin), and the batch number. It also features a QR code that can be scanned to verify the authenticity of the certificate. It's important to keep this document safe and readily accessible, as it serves as proof of your vaccination status for various purposes. The digital format ensures it's always with you on your phone or can be easily printed if needed.

Conclusion

Obtaining your COVID-19 vaccination certificate in India is a simple and accessible process thanks to digital platforms like CoWIN, Aarogya Setu, and UMANG. By following these step-by-step guides, you can easily download your official certificate and have it ready whenever you need it. Keep your registered mobile number handy, and the process should be smooth. This digital proof is a vital tool in navigating daily life post-vaccination.
