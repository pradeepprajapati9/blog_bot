How to Apply for an Ayushman Card Online in India

20 Sep 2026

The Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana, commonly known as PM-JAY, is one of the world's largest government-funded health insurance schemes. It provides an annual health cover of up to five lakh rupees per eligible family for secondary and tertiary hospitalization across thousands of empanelled government and private hospitals in India. An Ayushman card serves as your proof of entitlement, enabling cashless medical care when you or a family member falls sick. While citizens previously had to visit common service centers or government hospitals to get their card issued, the National Health Authority now allows anyone to complete the entire process online using a smartphone or computer. Understanding how to verify your eligibility, submit required documents, and complete your digital verification ensures your household has immediate access to critical healthcare benefits without procedural delays.

Who is Eligible for an Ayushman Bharat Card?

Eligibility for the Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY scheme is determined automatically through government databases rather than an open enrollment form. The scheme initially identified beneficiaries using the Socio-Economic and Caste Census of 2011, covering low-income rural households and defined occupational categories of urban workers. In addition, many state governments have integrated their food security data, making active Antyodaya Anna Yojana or priority ration card holders eligible. Recently, the central government expanded the program to include all senior citizens aged 70 and above, regardless of family income or economic status. Under this senior citizen category, individuals receive a separate top-up cover of five lakh rupees annually. Because you cannot simply sign up without qualifying criteria, your first practical step is to verify whether your name or your family ration card already exists in the official National Health Authority beneficiary database.

Documents Needed Before Starting the Application

Getting your documents organized in advance makes the online application smooth and prevents verification errors. The primary identity document required is your Aadhaar card. Ensure that your mobile number is currently linked to your Aadhaar because the portal verifies your identity using an automated one-time password. In addition to your Aadhaar, you will need a valid government-issued proof of family relationship, with an active ration card being the most widely accepted option. Other accepted family documents in select states include PM-JAY family identification letters or state-specific welfare cards. If your mobile number is not linked to your Aadhaar, you can still complete verification through the Ayushman mobile application using face authentication on an Android smartphone with a working front camera, or by visiting your nearest Common Service Center for biometric fingerprint capture.

How to Check Your Name on the Beneficiary Portal

To check your family status, visit the official National Health Authority beneficiary portal at beneficiary.nha.gov.in on any modern web browser. On the homepage, select the Beneficiary option and enter your active ten-digit mobile number. Click on the button to generate a one-time password, enter the verification code received on your phone, fill in the captcha characters, and hit login. Once inside the dashboard, select your state and the PM-JAY scheme from the dropdown menus. Choose your district and your search method, which allows you to look up records using your family ration card number, Aadhaar number, family identification number, or district location details. Entering your Aadhaar or ration card number is generally the fastest and most accurate method. Click the search icon, and the portal will display a list of all family members associated with that record along with their card status.

Step-by-Step e-KYC Verification Process

If your search displays your family members with an unverified or action required status, you need to complete the electronic know-your-customer verification. Click the e-KYC button adjacent to the member name you want to verify. Choose Aadhaar OTP as your verification method, review the consent form, and click allow. A temporary authentication code is sent to the mobile number registered with your Aadhaar card, while a separate login code reaches your operational phone number. Enter both verification codes into their respective input fields. Once authentication succeeds, the screen displays your Aadhaar profile details alongside your beneficiary record. You must upload a recent clear photograph of yourself taken in good lighting against a plain background. Finally, review your communication details, select your sub-district and village or town, enter your pin code, and submit the completed application for immediate verification.

How to Download Your Digital Ayushman Card

After successful electronic verification, your Ayushman card is generated either instantaneously or within a few hours, depending on whether automated matching scores clear administrative thresholds. To download your digital card, log back into the beneficiary portal using the same mobile authentication process and run your family search. Locate the member name whose e-KYC was completed; the status column should now read verified in green with a download icon next to it. Click the download card button and select your preferred verification method, which is typically another quick Aadhaar OTP. After confirming the prompt, the portal generates a secure, print-ready PDF document bearing a unique Ayushman identification number, your photograph, and a scannable QR code. Save this digital file securely on your smartphone, and print a physical laminated copy to keep inside your medical emergency wallet.

Using Your Card for Cashless Hospital Treatment

Your Ayushman card works on a completely cashless and paperless basis at any empanelled public or private healthcare provider across the country. If you or a covered family member requires hospitalization, locate an empanelled hospital near you by using the hospital search directory on the official PM-JAY portal or calling the national helpline on 14555. When you visit the medical facility, head directly to the dedicated Ayushman Mitra help desk located in the reception area. Present your physical card or the digital copy stored on your phone along with the patient Aadhaar card for quick admission. The Ayushman Mitra coordinates pre-authorization requests with the insurance authorities, covering diagnosis, doctor consultations, surgeries, medications, and food during your stay. The hospital cannot ask for cash deposits or upfront payments for treatments approved within defined scheme limits.

Conclusion

Securing an Ayushman card is one of the most effective steps you can take to safeguard your family from sudden, catastrophic healthcare expenses. Thanks to the updated National Health Authority online portal, verifying your eligibility and generating your official card takes only a few minutes from home without dealing with touts or complex paperwork. Check your family records online today, complete any pending e-KYC verifications for all eligible household members, and keep digital copies stored safely on your phone for peace of mind.
