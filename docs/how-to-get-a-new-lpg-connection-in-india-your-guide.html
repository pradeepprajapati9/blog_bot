How to Get a New LPG Connection in India: Your Guide

14 Nov 2026

Getting a new LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) connection is a common necessity for many households in India. Whether you're setting up a new home, moving to a different city, or simply upgrading your cooking fuel, understanding the process can save you time and hassle. LPG is a clean, efficient, and widely used cooking gas, making it an essential utility. This comprehensive guide will walk you through each step of obtaining a new LPG connection in India, from selecting your provider to understanding the installation process and crucial safety measures. We'll cover both online and offline application methods, ensuring you have all the information needed for a smooth experience.

Choosing Your LPG Provider in India

In India, the primary LPG providers are government-owned companies: Indane (IndianOil), HP Gas (Hindustan Petroleum), and Bharat Gas (Bharat Petroleum). Your first step is to choose a provider that best serves your area. Factors to consider include the availability of distributors in your locality, their reputation for service, delivery timelines, and customer support. You can usually find a list of distributors by visiting the official websites of Indane, HP Gas, or Bharat Gas and using their "Distributor Locator" tool. It's often practical to choose a distributor close to your home for easier refills and quicker service. Some areas might also have private LPG providers, but the government-backed options are generally more prevalent and regulated, often offering subsidies. Evaluate these options based on your specific needs and geographic location.

Essential Documents for Your New LPG Connection

To apply for a new LPG connection, you'll need to prepare certain documents for identity and address verification. These are crucial for a smooth application process. 1. Proof of Identity (PoI): A valid photo ID such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Driving License, or Voter ID Card. Ensure your chosen document is current and clearly shows your photo and name. 2. Proof of Address (PoA): Documents like Aadhaar Card (if address is current), Utility Bills (electricity, water, telephone - less than 3 months old), Passport, Rent Agreement, House Registration document, or Bank Passbook/Statement. The address on this document must match your installation address. 3. Recent Passport-Sized Photographs: Usually, 2-3 photographs are required. 4. Self-Declaration: Some providers may require a self-declaration stating you do not have any other LPG connection in your household. Having these documents ready in both original and photocopy forms (for offline application) or digital formats (for online application) will significantly speed up your process.

The Application Process: Online & Offline Methods

You have two main ways to apply for a new LPG connection: Online Method: 1. Visit the official website of your chosen provider (Indane, HP Gas, Bharat Gas). 2. Look for "Register for New Connection" or "New LPG Connection." 3. Fill out the online application form with your personal details, address, and selected distributor. 4. Upload scanned copies of your required PoI and PoA documents, and your passport-sized photo. 5. Pay the initial deposit online, which typically includes the cost of the cylinder and regulator. 6. You will receive a reference number to track your application status. Offline Method: 1. Visit your nearest distributor of the chosen LPG provider. 2. Obtain the application form (Form 2 for general connections, or Form 5 for Ujjwala Yojana beneficiaries). 3. Fill out the form accurately and attach photocopies of your PoI, PoA, and passport-sized photographs. 4. Submit the form along with the initial deposit for the cylinder and regulator. 5. You will be provided with a waiting list number or a receipt, which serves as proof of your application. The waiting period can vary depending on demand and location.

Connection Installation and Safety Precautions

Once your application is approved and the deposit is paid, the distributor will schedule the installation of your new LPG connection. A trained technician will visit your home to: 1. Deliver and install the LPG cylinder, regulator, and safety hose. 2. Check for any gas leaks and ensure proper functioning of the setup. 3. Provide essential instructions on how to safely use the LPG stove and cylinder. Crucial Safety Tips to Remember: * Always keep the LPG cylinder in a well-ventilated area, away from direct sunlight and heat sources. * Ensure the safety hose (suraksha hose) is ISI marked and replaced as per recommendations (typically every 5 years). * Always turn off the regulator knob when the gas stove is not in use, especially overnight or when leaving home. * If you smell gas, immediately turn off the regulator, open all windows and doors, do not operate any electrical switches, and contact your distributor's emergency helpline. * Never try to fix a gas leak yourself. Adhering to these safety guidelines is paramount to prevent accidents and ensure a secure environment.

Understanding Your LPG Connection: DV, SV, and Subsidies

When you get a new LPG connection, you'll come across a few terms: * Domestic Voucher (DV): This is the initial document you receive after applying, confirming your registration for an LPG connection. It includes details like your connection number and the distributor's information. * Subscription Voucher (SV): Once your connection is active and installed, the DV is converted into an SV. The SV is a crucial document that proves your ownership of the LPG cylinder and regulator. It's essential to keep this safe as it's required for cylinder refills, transfers, or any modifications to your connection. * LPG Subsidy: The Indian government offers a subsidy on domestic LPG cylinders to eligible households. This subsidy amount is directly credited to your bank account via the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scheme, known as PAHAL (DBTL). To receive the subsidy, ensure your Aadhaar number is linked to both your LPG connection and your bank account. You can check your subsidy status on the respective LPG provider's website. Understanding these terms helps you manage your connection effectively.

Conclusion

Obtaining a new LPG connection in India is a straightforward process when you know the steps involved. By carefully choosing your provider, preparing the necessary documents, following the application procedures (online or offline), and prioritizing safety during installation and usage, you can ensure a hassle-free experience. Remember to keep your Subscription Voucher (SV) safe and stay informed about government subsidies. A reliable LPG connection is vital for every household, offering convenience and efficiency for your daily cooking needs. Stay safe and enjoy your new connection!
