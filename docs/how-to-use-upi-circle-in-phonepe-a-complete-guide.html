How to Use UPI Circle in PhonePe: A Complete Guide

23 Aug 2026

Managing shared expenses with family, friends, or roommates can sometimes be a bit of a hassle. Whether it's splitting rent, groceries, dinner bills, or a trip budget, keeping track of who owes what and who has paid can get complicated. This is where PhonePe's UPI Circle feature comes in handy. It's designed to simplify group payments, making it easy to manage shared finances directly within your PhonePe app. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to start using UPI Circle effectively, ensuring your group transactions are smooth and transparent.

What is PhonePe UPI Circle and Why Use It?

PhonePe UPI Circle is a feature that allows users to create a dedicated group for managing shared expenses. Think of it as a digital wallet or ledger specifically for a group, where members can contribute, pay, and track expenses together. Instead of manually calculating who owes whom, UPI Circle automates much of the process. It's particularly useful for roommates splitting rent and utilities, families managing household expenses, or friends sharing costs during outings or trips. The primary benefit is convenience and transparency, as all members can see the transactions within the circle, reducing confusion and the need for constant reminders. It streamlines the entire process of collecting and paying for shared costs, making financial interactions within a group much simpler.

Finding and Accessing UPI Circle in PhonePe

To begin using UPI Circle, you first need to locate it within your PhonePe application. Open the PhonePe app on your smartphone. The interface might vary slightly based on updates, but generally, you will find UPI Circle under the 'Transfer Money' or 'Recharge & Pay Bills' section. Look for an icon or text label that says 'UPI Circle' or 'Group Payments'. Sometimes, it might be listed under 'View All' services. Tap on this option to enter the UPI Circle section. If you're using it for the first time, you might see an introductory screen explaining its features. Once inside, you'll have the option to either 'Create a New Circle' or 'Join a Circle' if you've received an invitation. Take a moment to familiarise yourself with the layout before proceeding.

Creating Your First UPI Circle

Creating a new UPI Circle is straightforward. After accessing the UPI Circle section, tap on the 'Create New Circle' button. The app will then prompt you to give your circle a name. Choose a clear and descriptive name that reflects the purpose of the group, like 'Roommate Rent & Bills', 'Family Groceries', or 'Goa Trip Fund'. You can also add an optional description to provide more context. Once named, you'll be asked to add members. You can invite people from your phone's contact list who also use PhonePe. Select the contacts you wish to add and send them an invitation. They will need to accept the invitation to become active members of your circle. As the creator, you will be the administrator of this circle.

Adding Members and Setting Circle Rules

After creating your circle, inviting members is the next crucial step. You can invite new members at any time by going into the circle's settings and selecting 'Add Member'. Once an invitation is sent, the recipient will receive a notification and must accept it to join the circle. As the administrator, you can also set certain rules or preferences for the circle, though specific advanced rules might be limited. The general expectation is that all circle members contribute equally or as agreed upon outside the app. The primary function is to facilitate easy payment and tracking among agreed members, making the process of splitting bills seamless. Clear communication among circle members about shared expenses is always recommended for smooth functioning.

Making and Receiving Payments within a Circle

Once your UPI Circle is set up with members, making or requesting payments becomes incredibly easy. If you need to pay for a shared expense (e.g., you bought groceries for everyone), you can select your circle, tap on 'Pay' or 'Send Money', choose the members you are paying on behalf of, enter the total amount, and then make the payment from your linked bank account using your UPI PIN. The transaction will be recorded within the circle, showing that you have contributed. Conversely, if you need to request money from other members for a shared expense you've covered, you can use the 'Request Money' option within the circle. PhonePe will then notify the relevant members, who can then pay their share directly to you through the circle, keeping all transactions neatly organised and visible to everyone in the group.

Viewing Circle History and Managing Members

One of the biggest advantages of PhonePe UPI Circle is the transparency it offers. Within each circle, you can easily view a detailed history of all transactions. This means you can see who has paid what, who owes money, and for which expenses. Simply navigate to your specific circle, and you'll find a transaction log or history section. This feature helps in quickly resolving any discrepancies and ensures everyone is on the same page regarding shared finances. As the administrator, you also have control over managing the circle members. You can remove members if they are no longer part of the group, or add new ones as needed. This flexibility ensures your UPI Circle always accurately reflects your current shared expense group.

Conclusion

PhonePe UPI Circle is a powerful and practical tool for simplifying group payments in your everyday life. By creating and managing circles, you can effortlessly split bills, track contributions, and ensure financial transparency among friends, family, or roommates. It eliminates the awkwardness of reminders and complex manual calculations, making shared expenses a breeze. Start using UPI Circle today to experience a more organised and stress-free way to manage your group finances within the PhonePe app.
