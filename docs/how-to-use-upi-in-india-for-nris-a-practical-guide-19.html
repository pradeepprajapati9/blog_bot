How to Use UPI in India for NRIs: A Practical Guide

08 Jul 2026

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has revolutionized digital transactions in India, offering instant, secure, and seamless money transfers. For Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), UPI isn't just a convenience; it's a vital tool for managing finances during visits or supporting family back home. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to start using UPI in India as an NRI, ensuring you can make payments and send money with ease and confidence.

Understanding UPI and Its Benefits for NRIs

UPI is a real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). It allows you to link multiple bank accounts to a single mobile application and make instant, 24/7 payments using a Virtual Payment Address (VPA) or QR code. For NRIs, UPI offers significant advantages. It eliminates the need to carry cash, simplifies payments for goods and services when you're visiting India, and makes sending money to family or friends within India incredibly easy and fast. Forget traditional bank transfers that can take hours or even days; UPI transactions are usually completed in seconds, directly debiting and crediting bank accounts instantly. This convenience is a game-changer for NRIs looking for efficient financial management in India.

Key Requirements for NRIs to Use UPI in India

Before you can dive into the world of UPI, there are a couple of essential prerequisites for NRIs. First and foremost, you must have an active NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) bank account with an Indian bank. These accounts are specifically designed for NRIs to manage their income and savings in India. Secondly, and equally crucial, you need an active Indian mobile number. This mobile number must be registered and linked to your NRE or NRO bank account. Without an Indian mobile number, you won't be able to register for any UPI application. Ensure your chosen number is active and capable of receiving SMS messages, as this is essential for one-time password (OTP) verification during registration and transactions.

Linking Your Indian Mobile Number to Your NRE/NRO Account

The success of your UPI setup hinges on your Indian mobile number being correctly linked to your NRE/NRO bank account. If you haven't done this already, contact your bank. Most banks allow you to update your mobile number through their online banking portal, by visiting a branch in India, or sometimes via customer service. Clearly state that you wish to link your Indian mobile number to your NRE/NRO account for UPI and other banking services. Once linked, the bank will send transaction alerts and OTPs to this number. This step is non-negotiable, as all UPI apps use SMS verification to authenticate your bank account and prevent fraud. Double-check that the number registered with your bank exactly matches the number you will use on your smartphone for UPI.

Setting Up a UPI App as an NRI: Step-by-Step

Once your NRE/NRO account is linked to your Indian mobile number, setting up a UPI app is straightforward. Download any popular UPI application like BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm from your phone's app store. During the setup process, the app will ask to verify your mobile number. It does this by sending an SMS from your registered Indian number. Allow the app to send this SMS. After verification, you'll be prompted to select your bank from a list. Choose the bank where you hold your NRE/NRO account. The app will then automatically fetch the NRE/NRO accounts linked to your verified mobile number. Select the account you wish to use for UPI. Finally, you'll set up your UPI PIN, a 4 or 6-digit number that acts as your transaction password. Remember this PIN, as you'll need it for every transaction.

How to Transact: Sending and Receiving Money with UPI

Using UPI to send money is simple. To pay someone, open your UPI app and select 'Send Money'. You can enter the recipient's Virtual Payment Address (VPA), their linked mobile number (if they also use UPI), or scan a QR code. Enter the amount, add a note (optional), and confirm the payment using your UPI PIN. The money is instantly debited from your NRE/NRO account and credited to the recipient's account. To receive money, you can share your VPA with the sender. They can then initiate a 'Pay Request' to your VPA. Once you approve it using your UPI PIN, the funds will be credited to your NRE/NRO account. This makes paying for local services, splitting bills, or sending funds to family members in India incredibly efficient.

Important Tips and Security for NRI UPI Users

While UPI offers immense convenience, security should always be your priority. Always keep your UPI PIN confidential and never share it with anyone, not even bank employees. Be wary of unsolicited requests for money or suspicious links, and only make payments to verified merchants or known individuals. Most UPI apps have daily transaction limits, typically around INR 1 lakh (100,000 rupees), though this can vary by bank and app. Always check your transaction history regularly to monitor your spending. If you encounter any issues, such as failed transactions or unauthorized debits, contact your bank's customer service immediately. Ensure your phone's operating system and UPI app are always updated to the latest versions for enhanced security features. Remember, genuine UPI transactions always require you to enter your PIN to *send* money, never to *receive* it.

Conclusion

UPI offers an unparalleled level of convenience and efficiency for NRIs managing their finances in India. By ensuring you have an NRE/NRO account, an active Indian mobile number linked to it, and by following the simple setup steps, you can seamlessly make and receive payments. Embrace this powerful digital payment system to simplify your financial interactions during your visits or for supporting your loved ones in India. With proper security practices, UPI will be a valuable asset in your financial toolkit.
