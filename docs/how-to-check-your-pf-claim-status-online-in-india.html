How to Check Your PF Claim Status Online in India

09 Nov 2026

Applying for a Provident Fund (PF) withdrawal is an important financial step for many salaried individuals in India. Whether you’re withdrawing your full PF amount after retirement or making a partial withdrawal for specific needs, the period between applying and receiving the funds can often feel uncertain. Fortunately, the Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) has streamlined the process, allowing you to easily track your EPF claim status online. This comprehensive guide will walk you through the straightforward steps to check your provident fund withdrawal application status using both the official EPFO Unified Portal and the convenient UMANG mobile application, helping you stay informed every step of the way.

Understanding Your EPF Claim Status

When you submit an EPF withdrawal request, it goes through several stages of verification and processing. The "claim status" is essentially a real-time update on where your application stands within this journey. It lets you know if your application has been received, is currently being reviewed, has been approved and settled, or if there were any issues leading to its rejection. Understanding these statuses is crucial because it helps you anticipate when your funds might be disbursed or, importantly, if you need to take any corrective action. Common reasons for delays or rejection often include mismatched KYC details, incorrect bank account information, or incomplete forms, highlighting the importance of accurate data submission and regular status checks.

Prerequisites for Checking Your Status

Before you proceed to check your EPF claim status, ensure you have a few essential details ready. Firstly, you will need your Universal Account Number (UAN) and the associated password. Your UAN is a 12-digit number allotted by the EPFO to every employee, and it acts as a central ID for all your PF accounts. If your UAN isn't activated yet, you'll need to do that first – a process usually involving linking it with your Aadhaar and mobile number on the EPFO portal. You'll also need a reliable internet connection, whether you're using a computer or your smartphone, as the entire process is online. Having these readily available will make the status checking process quick and hassle-free.

Method 1: Checking via the EPFO Unified Portal

The most direct way to check your EPF claim status is through the official EPFO Unified Member Portal. Start by opening your web browser and navigating to the EPFO Unified Portal website. Once there, locate the 'Sign In' section. You will need to enter your 12-digit UAN and your password, then complete the captcha verification. After successfully logging in, hover over the "Online Services" tab in the top menu. From the dropdown list, click on "Track Claim Status." This page will display all your submitted claims along with their unique Claim IDs, the type of claim (e.g., partial withdrawal, full settlement), the date of application, and the most current status of each application. This portal offers a comprehensive overview of your claim's journey.

Method 2: Checking via the UMANG App

For those who prefer checking on the go, the UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) app offers a convenient alternative. If you don't have it already, download the UMANG app from your smartphone's app store (Google Play Store for Android or Apple App Store for iOS). Once installed, open the app and search for "EPFO" in the search bar. Within the EPFO services, you'll find an option like "Track Claim" or "View Passbook." Select this option. You will then be prompted to enter your UAN. An OTP (One-Time Password) will be sent to your registered mobile number. Enter this OTP to authenticate. The app will then display the details of your submitted EPF claims and their respective statuses, making it easy to monitor your application from anywhere.

Understanding Different Claim Statuses

When you check your EPF claim status, you'll encounter various terms, each indicating a specific stage of your application. "Under Process" means your application has been received and is currently undergoing verification by the EPFO. This is a common initial status. "Settled" indicates that your claim has been approved, the amount has been processed, and the funds have been transferred to your registered bank account. You should typically receive the amount within a few business days after this status appears. If you see "Rejected," it means your claim could not be processed, and the reason for rejection will usually be provided. Another status, "Payment Sent," specifically indicates that the amount has been disbursed from EPFO's end. Knowing these terms helps you understand the next steps or if any action is required from your side.

What to Do if Your Claim is Delayed or Rejected

If your EPF claim status remains "Under Process" for an unusually long time, or if your claim gets "Rejected," don't panic. First, if rejected, carefully read the reason provided. Common reasons include incorrect bank account details, mismatched Aadhaar or PAN details, or issues with the application form itself. Correct these issues and reapply. For delays, ensure your KYC details (Aadhaar, PAN, bank account) are fully updated and verified with EPFO. If all details are correct and the delay persists, you can raise a grievance through the EPF i-Grievance Management System on the EPFO portal. This allows you to formally lodge a complaint and track its resolution, ensuring your concerns are addressed promptly by the authorities.

Conclusion

Monitoring your EPF claim status online is a simple yet crucial step to ensure a smooth and timely provident fund withdrawal. By utilizing the official EPFO Unified Portal or the convenient UMANG app, you gain complete visibility into your application's progress. Regularly checking your status helps you stay informed, identify any potential issues early, and take prompt corrective action if your claim is delayed or rejected. This proactive approach ensures that your hard-earned provident fund reaches you without unnecessary stress, providing you with financial peace of mind.
