PM Kisan Yojana: Apply Online & Check Status Guide for Farmers

14 Sep 2026

The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) Yojana is a significant government initiative designed to provide financial support to eligible farmer families across India. Launched in 2019, this scheme aims to supplement the income of small and marginal farmers, helping them meet various farming expenses and domestic needs. If you're a farmer and want to understand how this scheme works, check your eligibility, apply online, or keep track of your benefits, this comprehensive guide will walk you through every essential step. It's about empowering our farmers with direct financial assistance, ensuring agricultural prosperity and stability for their families.

Understanding the PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana

The PM-KISAN scheme is a central sector scheme with 100% funding from the Government of India. Under this initiative, eligible farmer families receive income support of Rs. 6,000 per year, paid in three equal installments of Rs. 2,000 every four months. This direct income transfer helps farmers purchase inputs like seeds, fertilizers, and equipment, or use it for other essential needs. The primary objective is to alleviate the financial burdens faced by farmers and enhance their overall well-being. The funds are directly transferred to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries, ensuring transparency and efficiency in the disbursement process, bypassing intermediaries and potential delays.

Who is Eligible for PM Kisan and Who is Not?

To be eligible for PM-KISAN, you must be a landholding farmer family, which includes a husband, wife, and minor children, who collectively own cultivable land. Both rural and urban landholdings qualify. However, there are specific exclusion categories. For instance, institutional landholders, former and present holders of constitutional posts, former and present ministers, MPs, MLAs, and Mayors/Chairpersons of District Panchayats are not eligible. All serving or retired government employees, pensioners receiving over Rs. 10,000 per month, and individuals who paid income tax in the last assessment year are also excluded. Professionals like doctors, engineers, lawyers, and chartered accountants are also generally not eligible for this scheme.

Essential Documents for Your PM Kisan Application

Before you begin your online application for the PM-KISAN scheme, ensure you have all the necessary documents ready. The most crucial document is your Aadhaar Card, which is mandatory for identification and linking purposes. You will also need your land records, specifically the Khasra and Khatauni details, to prove your land ownership. A valid bank account passbook is essential, as the financial assistance will be directly deposited into this account. Make sure your bank account is Aadhaar-seeded for smooth transactions. Additionally, you will need a self-declaration of your landholding and details about your family members. Keeping these documents handy will streamline your application process significantly.

Step-by-Step Online Application Process for New Farmers

Applying for PM-KISAN online is a straightforward process. First, visit the official PM-KISAN website (pmkisan.gov.in). On the homepage, navigate to the 'Farmers Corner' section and click on 'New Farmer Registration'. You will be prompted to enter your Aadhaar number and captcha code. After successful verification, select your state and district. Fill out the application form with accurate details, including your personal information, bank account details, and land particulars as per your land records. Upload scanned copies of your required documents. Double-check all the information before submitting the form. Upon successful submission, you will receive a registration number, which you should keep safe for future reference and status tracking.

How to Check Your PM Kisan Beneficiary Status and Installments

Once you have applied or are an existing beneficiary, regularly checking your status is crucial. Go to the PM-KISAN website, navigate to 'Farmers Corner', and select 'Beneficiary Status'. You can check your status using either your Aadhaar Number, Bank Account Number, or Mobile Number. Enter the required detail and click 'Get Data'. This will display your beneficiary status, including the details of installments received, installment dates, and the 'FTO (Fund Transfer Order) is Generated and Payment confirmation is pending' status, if applicable. The FTO status indicates that the government has initiated the fund transfer, and the payment is awaiting processing by your bank. This transparency helps you stay informed about your benefits.

Completing Your PM Kisan e-KYC: Why It's Important

E-KYC (Electronic Know Your Customer) has become mandatory for all PM-KISAN beneficiaries. It is essential to complete your e-KYC to continue receiving installments under the scheme. This process helps in verifying the identity of the beneficiary and ensures that only genuine farmers receive the benefits, preventing fraud. To complete e-KYC, visit the PM-KISAN portal, click on the 'e-KYC' option under 'Farmers Corner'. You can complete it using your Aadhaar number and the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number. Alternatively, you can opt for biometric authentication by visiting your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or PM-KISAN kiosk. Ensure you complete this step promptly to avoid any disruption in your future installment payments.

Common Issues and Troubleshooting Tips for PM Kisan

Many beneficiaries encounter common issues while applying or checking their status. If your installment is 'FTO is Generated and Payment confirmation is pending', it means the funds are on their way but not yet credited to your account; wait a few days. If your installment shows 'Payment Stop by State' or 'Reason for non-payment', it could be due to incorrect Aadhaar details, bank account errors, or pending e-KYC. Always ensure your Aadhaar details match your bank account and land records. If you face 'Incorrect Bank Account details', contact your bank immediately to rectify them. For any major discrepancies or persistent issues, contact the PM-KISAN Helpline at 155261 or 011-24300606, or visit your nearest Agricultural Department office for assistance.

Conclusion

The PM-KISAN Yojana is a vital support system for many farmer families in India. By understanding the scheme, checking your eligibility, applying correctly, and regularly monitoring your status and e-KYC, you can ensure you receive the financial assistance you are entitled to. This guide aims to simplify the process, making it accessible for every eligible farmer. We encourage all eligible landholding farmer families to take advantage of this beneficial scheme and secure their financial future in agriculture. Keep your details updated and stay informed to maximize the benefits from this government initiative.
