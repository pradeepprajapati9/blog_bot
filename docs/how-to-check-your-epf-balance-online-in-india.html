How to Check Your EPF Balance Online in India

22 Aug 2026

The Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) is a crucial retirement savings scheme for salaried individuals in India. It's a mandatory contribution from both you and your employer, building a significant corpus over your working years. Knowing your EPF balance is essential for financial planning, ensuring your contributions are correctly updated, and tracking your long-term savings goal. Fortunately, checking your EPF balance has become incredibly simple and convenient thanks to various online and mobile options. Gone are the days of waiting for annual statements. This guide will walk you through the most practical and popular methods to check your EPF balance instantly from the comfort of your home or on the go, making sure you stay on top of your retirement savings.

Understanding Your EPF: Why Check Your Balance?

The Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) acts as a social security net, offering financial stability post-retirement. Both you (the employee) and your employer contribute a set percentage of your basic salary plus Dearness Allowance (DA) to this fund every month. This amount accumulates with interest, providing a substantial sum when you retire or if you switch jobs. Regularly checking your EPF balance isn't just about curiosity; it's a vital part of basic personal finance literacy. It helps you verify that your employer is consistently depositing your contributions, track the interest earned, and allows you to plan your finances more effectively. Catching any discrepancies early ensures your hard-earned money is secure and growing as it should be, preventing potential issues down the line.

Checking EPF Balance Using the UMANG App

The UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) app is a single platform for accessing various government services, including EPFO. It’s a very popular and convenient way to check your EPF balance. First, download the UMANG app from your smartphone's app store (Google Play Store or Apple App Store). Once installed, open the app and register using your mobile number. You'll need to link your Aadhaar if prompted for full functionality. Search for 'EPFO' within the app. Select the 'Employee Centric Services' option. Here, you'll find 'View Passbook' or 'View EPF Passbook'. Enter your Universal Account Number (UAN) and the OTP (One-Time Password) sent to your registered mobile number. Your detailed EPF passbook will be displayed, showing your contributions, employer's contributions, and interest credited. This method provides a comprehensive view and is very user-friendly.

How to Check EPF Balance via EPFO Member e-Sewa Portal

The official Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) Member e-Sewa Portal is another reliable way to access your EPF passbook. To use this method, you need an activated Universal Account Number (UAN) and your UAN password. Visit the official EPFO website and navigate to the 'Our Services' section, then select 'For Employees'. Click on 'Member Passbook'. This will redirect you to the UAN login page. Enter your UAN, password, and the captcha code. Once logged in, you can select the Member ID linked to your current employment (if you have multiple). Your EPF passbook will be displayed, allowing you to view and even download your detailed transaction history, including monthly contributions from both you and your employer, and the accrued interest. Ensure your internet connection is stable for a smooth experience.

Quick EPF Balance Check Through SMS Service

For those who prefer a simpler, faster method without an internet connection, the SMS service is incredibly useful. This service is available only if your UAN is activated and your mobile number is registered with EPFO. To check your balance, simply send an SMS to 7738299899. The message format should be 'EPFOHO UAN ENG'. The 'ENG' at the end is optional and indicates you want the information in English; you can replace it with other language codes like 'HIN' for Hindi, 'TEL' for Telugu, 'KAN' for Kannada, etc. Within a few moments, you will receive an SMS containing your latest EPF balance, your last contribution, and the total amount available. This method is especially convenient for quick checks and does not require a smartphone or internet access, making it accessible to a wider audience.

Checking EPF Balance with a Missed Call

Another remarkably simple method to check your EPF balance is by giving a missed call. This service is free of cost and does not require an internet connection or even a smartphone, as long as your mobile number is registered with your UAN. All you need to do is give a missed call to 011-22901406 from your registered mobile number. After a couple of rings, the call will automatically disconnect. Shortly after, you will receive an SMS from EPFO providing details of your latest EPF balance, your last contribution, and other relevant information. This method is particularly beneficial for those who might not be comfortable with apps or online portals, offering an immediate and easy way to stay updated on their provident fund balance.

Important: Your UAN and KYC for EPF Balance Access

To access any of the online or missed call/SMS services for checking your EPF balance, having an activated Universal Account Number (UAN) is paramount. Your UAN is a 12-digit unique number allotted to every employee contributing to EPF, remaining the same throughout your career irrespective of job changes. If you don't know your UAN, you can usually find it on your salary slip or ask your employer. Additionally, ensuring your Know Your Customer (KYC) details are updated with EPFO is crucial. This includes linking your Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account to your UAN. Incomplete or incorrect KYC details can prevent you from accessing your passbook or receiving SMS updates. Make sure your mobile number is also registered with EPFO and linked to your UAN, as most verification processes rely on OTPs sent to this number. You can update your KYC details through the EPFO Member Portal if needed.

Why Regularly Checking Your EPF Balance is Smart Financial Practice

Regularly checking your EPF balance is more than just a routine; it's a smart habit for sound personal finance. Firstly, it ensures transparency and helps you verify that your employer is consistently remitting your EPF contributions on time and accurately. Any delays or discrepancies can be promptly identified and addressed with your employer or the EPFO. Secondly, it gives you a clear picture of your retirement savings progress, allowing you to gauge if you're on track for your financial goals. Seeing your balance grow with interest can also be a strong motivator for continued saving. Lastly, it empowers you to make informed decisions about your long-term financial planning, providing clarity on a significant portion of your accumulated wealth for the future. Don't underestimate the power of staying informed about your EPF.

Conclusion

Staying updated on your EPF balance is a fundamental aspect of financial literacy for any salaried individual in India. With multiple convenient options like the UMANG app, EPFO portal, SMS, and missed call service, tracking your retirement savings has never been easier. Make it a habit to regularly check your balance to ensure your contributions are accurate, your money is growing, and you are well-prepared for your financial future. These simple steps empower you to take control of your provident fund and secure your long-term financial well-being.
