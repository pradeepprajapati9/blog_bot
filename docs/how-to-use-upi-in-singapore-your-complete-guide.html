How to Use UPI in Singapore: Your Complete Guide

20 Aug 2026

India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has transformed digital transactions, making them instant and effortless. Now, thanks to a landmark linkage with Singapore's PayNow, the convenience of UPI extends to cross-border payments between the two nations. This means Indian residents and travelers can leverage UPI services for certain transactions involving Singapore. This guide will walk you through exactly how this interoperability works, what you need, and how you can use UPI for sending and receiving money to or from Singapore, ensuring your financial interactions are as smooth as possible.

Understanding UPI-PayNow Interoperability

The UPI-PayNow linkage is a significant initiative that connects India's fast payment system (UPI) with Singapore's equivalent (PayNow). Launched in February 2023, this partnership allows for real-time, low-cost fund transfers between bank accounts in India and Singapore. It's a collaboration between the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS), facilitated by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) and the Association of Banks in Singapore (ABS). For you, this means an easier way to send money home to India or to a recipient in Singapore directly from your existing UPI application, without needing to go through traditional remittance channels that can be slower and more expensive.

What You Need to Use UPI for Singapore Transactions

To utilize the UPI-PayNow linkage, you primarily need an active Indian bank account linked to a functional UPI application on your smartphone. Popular UPI apps include PhonePe, Google Pay (GPay), Paytm, BHIM, and bank-specific UPI applications. Ensure your app is updated to the latest version to access the international transfer features. You will also need a stable internet connection, whether Wi-Fi or mobile data, to initiate and complete transactions. It's crucial to remember that this service is primarily for Indian account holders and facilitates remittances to or from bank accounts linked to PayNow in Singapore.

Sending Money from India to Singapore via UPI

If you wish to send money from your Indian bank account to a recipient in Singapore, you can do so directly from your UPI app. Currently, this service is offered by specific partner banks like SBI, DBS Bank India, ICICI Bank, and Axis Bank. Open your UPI app and look for the 'International Money Transfer' or 'Send to Singapore' option. You will typically need to enter the recipient's PayNow VPA (Virtual Payment Address), which is usually their Singapore mobile number or NRIC/FIN, or scan a PayNow QR code if provided. Confirm the amount in INR, which will then be converted to SGD at the prevailing exchange rate. Verify the recipient details and complete the transaction using your UPI PIN. Funds are usually credited almost instantly.

Receiving Money in India from Singapore via UPI

The UPI-PayNow linkage also works in reverse, allowing individuals in Singapore to send money to your Indian bank account using their PayNow application. For this, they will need your UPI ID (VPA). Once they initiate a transfer from their Singaporean bank's PayNow app using your UPI ID, the funds will be converted from SGD to INR at the current exchange rate and credited directly to your linked Indian bank account. You do not need to take any action on your UPI app to receive these funds, beyond ensuring your UPI ID is active and linked to the correct bank account. This provides a fast and convenient way for friends, family, or business partners in Singapore to send money to you in India.

Using UPI Apps for Payments While Traveling in Singapore (Clarification)

A common question from Indian travelers is whether they can directly use their Indian UPI app to pay merchants for goods and services in Singapore, similar to how they would in India. Currently, the primary function of the UPI-PayNow linkage is for person-to-person (P2P) remittances between India and Singapore. While advancements are being made, direct merchant payments from an Indian UPI app to a Singaporean merchant's POS (Point-of-Sale) via QR code are not yet universally supported in the same way. For your day-to-day spending in Singapore, it is still advisable to rely on international debit/credit cards, local Singaporean payment methods, or physical cash. Always check with the merchant if they explicitly accept Indian UPI payments before attempting a transaction.

Benefits, Limits, and Key Considerations

The UPI-PayNow linkage offers several benefits: transactions are typically instant, available 24/7, and generally come with lower transaction fees compared to traditional remittance services. However, there are limits to consider. For transfers from India to Singapore, there's usually a daily transaction limit of INR 60,000 (approximately SGD 1,000). For transfers from Singapore to India, the limit is SGD 1,000 per transaction. It's important to be mindful of the exchange rates applied by the participating banks, as these can vary slightly. Always double-check the final amount in both currencies before confirming any transaction. Ensure you have a secure internet connection and never share your UPI PIN or banking details with anyone.

Conclusion

The UPI-PayNow linkage represents a significant step towards greater financial connectivity between India and Singapore, offering a fast, convenient, and cost-effective way to manage cross-border remittances. While it simplifies sending and receiving money, especially for Indian account holders, it's important to understand its current scope, primarily focused on person-to-person transfers. By following the guidelines in this article, you can confidently navigate how to use UPI services for your financial needs involving Singapore, making your international transactions smoother and more efficient than ever before.
