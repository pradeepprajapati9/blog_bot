How to Apply for a New Passport Online in India: Simple Guide

19 Sep 2026

Applying for a passport might seem like a daunting task, but thanks to the Passport Seva system, the process has become significantly streamlined and user-friendly for Indian citizens. Having a passport is essential for international travel, identity verification, and sometimes even for domestic purposes. This comprehensive guide will walk you through every step of applying for a new passport online in India, making the entire journey straightforward and hassle-free. From understanding the initial requirements to receiving your passport, we'll cover everything you need to know to ensure a smooth application process.

Understanding Passport Types and Basic Requirements

Before you begin your online application, it's important to understand the different types of passports available and the basic eligibility criteria. For most first-time applicants, you will be applying for a 'Fresh Passport' under the 'Normal' category. The 'Tatkaal' category is available for urgent travel needs but involves higher fees and stricter documentation. Ensure you meet the age requirements; for minors, specific consent and document rules apply. Generally, you'll need proof of identity, proof of address, and proof of date of birth. Having these documents ready and in order will significantly speed up your application process, so gather them before you start filling out forms online.

Registering on the Official Passport Seva Portal

The first essential step is to register yourself on the official Passport Seva website (passportindia.gov.in). Look for the 'New User Registration' link on the homepage. You will need to provide basic details like your name, date of birth, email ID, and desired login ID. Choose your Passport Office carefully, as this determines where your application will be processed and where you will attend your appointment. After filling in the details, an activation link will be sent to your registered email address. Click this link to activate your account. Remember your login ID and password, as you will need them throughout the application process and for tracking your application status.

Filling Out Your Online Passport Application Form

Once your account is activated, log in to the Passport Seva portal. Click on 'Apply for Fresh Passport/Re-issue of Passport.' You will be directed to an online application form. It's crucial to fill this form accurately and completely, as any discrepancies could lead to delays or rejection. The form will ask for personal details, family details, address information, and emergency contact details. Double-check all entries, especially your name, date of birth, and address, against your supporting documents. You can save your application at any point and resume later if needed. Take your time to ensure all information is correct before submitting.

Paying Application Fees and Scheduling Your Appointment

After successfully filling out the online application form, the next step is to pay the application fee and schedule an appointment at your nearest Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK). You can pay online using a credit/debit card, net banking, or through SBI Bank Challan. The fee for a normal fresh passport (36 pages) is typically ₹1,500. Once the payment is successful, you will see an option to 'Schedule Appointment.' Choose your preferred PSK/POPSK and select an available date and time slot that works for you. Make sure to print the 'Application Receipt' which includes your Application Reference Number (ARN). This receipt is vital for your visit to the PSK.

Documents Required for Your PSK Visit

When you visit the Passport Seva Kendra, you must carry all the original documents you declared in your online application, along with self-attested photocopies. For a fresh passport, common documents include Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, driving license, birth certificate, and bank passbook (as proof of address and identity). For proof of date of birth, a birth certificate or school leaving certificate is usually accepted. Ensure your photographs meet the specified criteria (white background, no shadows, clear face). It's always a good idea to check the official Passport Seva website for the most up-to-date and specific document list based on your age and category, as requirements can sometimes vary slightly.

Visiting the Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) for Verification

On the day of your appointment, arrive at the Passport Seva Kendra with all your original documents and their self-attested photocopies, along with your printed Application Receipt (ARN). Be prepared for security checks. Inside the PSK, your application will go through a structured process involving token issuance, document verification at Counter A, biometrics (fingerprints and photograph) at Counter B, and final verification by the Passport Officer at Counter C. Maintain calm and answer all questions clearly. The officials will check your original documents against your application form. Once all checks are completed and approved, you will receive an acknowledgement slip confirming your application has been processed.

Police Verification and Passport Dispatch

After your PSK visit, the police verification process will be initiated. A police officer from your local police station will visit your declared address to verify your residency and other details. Cooperate fully and provide any requested proofs. This step is crucial for the final approval of your passport. You can track the status of your application online using your ARN on the Passport Seva website. Once the police verification report is submitted and is favorable, your passport will be printed and dispatched via speed post to your registered address. You will typically receive an SMS with tracking details once the passport is dispatched. Be patient, as the entire process can take a few weeks depending on various factors.

Conclusion

Applying for a new passport online in India is a systematic process designed for your convenience. By carefully following each step – from online registration and accurate form filling to scheduling your PSK appointment and document verification – you can navigate the application smoothly. Remember to keep all your documents ready, double-check information, and stay patient through the police verification stage. With this guide, you are well-equipped to get your passport and open doors to new opportunities, whether for travel, education, or professional pursuits.
