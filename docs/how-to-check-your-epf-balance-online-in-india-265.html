How to Check Your EPF Balance Online in India

19 Oct 2026

The Employee Provident Fund (EPF) is a vital social security scheme for salaried individuals in India, helping you build a retirement corpus. It’s crucial to keep track of your EPF balance to monitor your savings and ensure your employer's contributions are correctly deposited. While traditional methods existed, checking your EPF balance online has become incredibly simple and convenient, accessible from your smartphone or computer. This guide will walk you through various digital methods to effortlessly check your EPF balance and understand the details of your contributions, ensuring you stay updated on your financial future.

What is EPF and Why Check Your Balance Regularly?

The Employee Provident Fund (EPF) is a mandatory savings scheme managed by the Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) for employees in India. Both the employee and employer contribute a percentage of the employee's basic salary plus dearness allowance to this fund each month. These contributions grow with interest, providing a substantial corpus upon retirement or for specific withdrawals under certain conditions. Regularly checking your EPF balance is essential for several reasons. It helps you verify that your employer is making timely contributions, allows you to track the growth of your retirement savings, and helps in financial planning. Early detection of discrepancies can save you from potential issues later on, ensuring your financial security is on track. Being informed about your EPF account is a cornerstone of responsible personal finance management in India.

Accessing Your EPF Balance via Umang App

The Umang App (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) is a powerful platform developed by the Indian government that integrates various public services, including EPFO services. To check your EPF balance using this app, first download and register on the Umang App with your mobile number. Once registered, search for 'EPFO' within the app. Select 'Employee Centric Services' and then 'View Passbook'. You will need to enter your Universal Account Number (UAN) and a one-time password (OTP) sent to your registered mobile number. After successful verification, you can view your EPF passbook, which details your contributions, withdrawals, and interest credited. This method is quick, user-friendly, and provides comprehensive information directly on your smartphone.

Checking EPF Balance Through the EPFO Member e-Sewa Portal

The EPFO Member e-Sewa Portal is another official and reliable way to check your EPF balance online. To use this portal, you must have an activated Universal Account Number (UAN) and a password. Visit the official EPFO website and navigate to the 'Our Services' section, then select 'For Employees', and finally 'Member Passbook'. Alternatively, you can directly search for 'EPFO Member Passbook'. On the login page, enter your UAN and password, then input the captcha code. Once logged in, you can select your Member ID (if you have multiple accounts) to view your e-passbook. This detailed passbook shows month-wise contributions from both you and your employer, interest accrued, and any withdrawals made. It's a comprehensive view, ideal for detailed review.

Instant Balance Check Using SMS and Missed Call Services

For those without internet access or who prefer a simpler method, EPFO offers both SMS and missed call services to check your balance instantly. To check via SMS, send an SMS to 7738299899 with the message 'EPFOHO UAN ENG'. Replace 'ENG' with the first three letters of your preferred language (e.g., HIN for Hindi, BEN for Bengali). Ensure your UAN is linked with your bank account, Aadhaar, and PAN for this service to work. Within moments, you will receive an SMS containing your latest EPF balance and KYC status. For the missed call service, simply dial 9966044425 from your UAN-registered mobile number. After a couple of rings, the call will disconnect automatically, and you will receive an SMS with your EPF balance details. These methods are particularly useful for quick checks on the go.

Understanding Your EPF Passbook: Key Details

Once you access your EPF passbook through any of the online methods, it's important to understand the information presented. The passbook is more than just a balance figure; it's a detailed statement of your provident fund account. Key details include your Universal Account Number (UAN), Member ID, establishment name, and office name. You will see columns for 'Employee Share' and 'Employer Share' of contributions for each month, clearly showing how much you and your employer have contributed. The 'Interest' column shows the interest credited to your account. Any 'Withdrawals' or 'Transfers' will also be listed, along with their dates. The passbook concludes with the total balance, giving you a clear picture of your accumulated savings. Regularly reviewing these details helps ensure accuracy and allows you to track the consistent growth of your retirement fund.

Troubleshooting Common EPF Balance Check Issues

While checking your EPF balance online is generally smooth, you might encounter a few common issues. One frequent problem is an unactivated UAN; ensure your UAN is activated on the EPFO portal before attempting any balance checks. If you forget your password for the e-Sewa portal, use the 'Forgot Password' option to reset it using your UAN and registered mobile number. Another issue could be mismatched KYC details (Aadhaar, PAN, bank account) linked to your UAN; ensure these are updated and verified on the EPFO portal. If the SMS or missed call service doesn't work, verify that your mobile number is correctly registered and linked with your UAN. For any persistent issues, it's advisable to contact your employer's HR department or visit the nearest EPFO office for assistance. Timely resolution of these issues ensures uninterrupted access to your EPF account information.

Conclusion

Staying informed about your EPF balance is a crucial aspect of managing your personal finances in India. With the various digital methods available – be it the user-friendly Umang App, the comprehensive EPFO Member e-Sewa Portal, or the instant SMS and missed call services – checking your provident fund status has never been easier. Regularly monitoring your contributions and interest ensures the health of your retirement savings and helps you plan better for your future. Take advantage of these simple, practical tools to keep your financial well-being on track.
