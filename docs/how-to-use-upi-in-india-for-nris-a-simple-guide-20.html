How to Use UPI in India for NRIs: A Simple Guide

08 Jul 2026

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has revolutionized digital payments in India, making transactions incredibly fast, secure, and convenient. For Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) visiting their homeland or managing finances remotely, UPI offers an unparalleled ease of use. Whether you need to pay for groceries, send money to family, or settle bills, UPI can simplify your financial interactions significantly. This guide will walk you through everything an NRI needs to know to start using UPI effectively in India.

What is UPI and Why it Matters for NRIs?

UPI is an instant real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). It allows you to link multiple bank accounts into a single mobile application, enabling you to send and receive money instantly using a Virtual Payment Address (VPA) or QR code, without needing to share your bank account details. For NRIs, this means no more fumbling with cash or relying solely on cards. You can pay directly from your Indian bank account (NRE or NRO) at millions of merchants, small shops, and even for services like taxis or utility bills. It offers a transparent, trackable, and efficient way to manage your expenses during your stay in India, providing financial independence and convenience.

Eligibility Criteria for NRIs to Use UPI

To use UPI as an NRI, there are a few essential requirements you must meet. Firstly, you must have an NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) bank account with an Indian bank. These accounts are specifically designed for NRIs to manage their income and savings in India. Secondly, and crucially, you need an active Indian mobile number (SIM card) that is linked to your NRE/NRO bank account. This mobile number is essential for the registration process, as UPI apps verify your identity by sending an SMS from this registered number. Ensure your Indian SIM is active and operational on your phone when setting up UPI.

Steps to Link Your NRE/NRO Account to UPI

Setting up UPI for your NRE/NRO account is straightforward. First, download a UPI-enabled application on your smartphone. Popular choices include your bank's official app (e.g., HDFC PayZapp, ICICI iMobile Pay, SBI YONO), or third-party apps like BHIM, Google Pay, or PhonePe. Once installed, open the app and select the option to 'Add Bank Account' or 'Link Bank Account'. The app will prompt you to verify your Indian mobile number via SMS. Make sure the phone number registered with your NRE/NRO account is in the phone from which you are initiating the setup. After successful verification, select your bank from the list and choose your NRE/NRO account. Finally, you will set up a UPI PIN, which is a 4 or 6-digit number used to authorize your transactions.

Key Banks Supporting NRI UPI Services

Most major Indian banks now offer UPI services for their NRI customers holding NRE/NRO accounts. These include State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Punjab National Bank (PNB), Bank of Baroda, and many others. Before you plan to set up UPI, it's a good practice to confirm with your specific bank if they support NRI UPI transactions and if there are any specific internal processes you need to follow. While the general setup is similar across banks, some might have their own proprietary UPI apps or specific instructions for NRI account holders. Always use the official bank application or trusted third-party UPI apps for security.

Using UPI for Transactions in India

Once your NRE/NRO account is linked and your UPI PIN is set, you can start making transactions instantly. To send money, simply enter the recipient's VPA, their Indian mobile number (if linked to UPI), or scan a QR code displayed by the merchant. Enter the amount, verify the details, and then enter your UPI PIN to authorize the payment. You will receive an instant confirmation message. For receiving money, you can share your VPA with the sender. Many apps also allow you to pay bills, recharge mobile numbers, or even invest in digital gold directly through UPI, making it a comprehensive financial tool for your time in India. Remember to always double-check recipient details before confirming any payment.

Important Considerations and Security Tips for NRIs

While UPI is incredibly convenient, keep these points in mind. Ensure your Indian mobile number remains active and you have network coverage when performing transactions. Be mindful of any foreign exchange conversion implications if funds originate from outside your NRE/NRO accounts. For security, never share your UPI PIN with anyone, not even bank officials. Be cautious of phishing attempts or suspicious links asking for your UPI details. Set a strong, unique UPI PIN and change it periodically. Also, be aware of daily transaction limits set by NPCI and your bank, which typically range from INR 1 lakh to 2 lakh for most transactions. Always use a secure internet connection when making payments.

Troubleshooting Common NRI UPI Issues

NRIs might occasionally encounter issues with UPI. A common problem is the Indian SIM card not being active or not having sufficient balance for the SMS verification. Ensure your SIM is active and topped up. Another issue can be that your bank's records for your mobile number or NRE/NRO account are not updated. In such cases, contact your bank's NRI customer service to ensure all details are current. If you face issues with a specific UPI app, try using the BHIM app, which is the official government-backed UPI application, or another trusted third-party app. Keep your bank's customer care number handy for immediate assistance if you encounter transaction failures or other technical difficulties.

Conclusion

UPI offers immense convenience and efficiency for NRIs managing their finances in India. By linking your NRE or NRO account to a UPI app via your Indian mobile number, you unlock a world of seamless digital transactions. From paying at local shops to sending money to family, UPI simplifies your financial interactions, making your time in India more enjoyable and hassle-free. Embrace this powerful payment system for a smooth and secure experience.
