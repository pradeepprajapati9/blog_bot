How to Book Train Tickets Online (IRCTC) in India

14 Sep 2026

Travelling by train is an integral part of life for millions of Indians, offering an economical and often picturesque way to traverse the vast landscapes of the country. The era of enduring long queues at railway stations for tickets is largely behind us. Thanks to the efficient digital platforms provided by the Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation (IRCTC), booking your train tickets is now a straightforward online process, manageable from your home, office, or even on the go via your smartphone. This comprehensive, step-by-step guide is designed to empower you with all the practical knowledge needed for a smooth and successful reservation. Whether you're a first-time user navigating the IRCTC portal or simply seeking a refresher, we’ve covered every essential detail to make your next train booking experience truly hassle-free. Get ready to embark on your journey planning with newfound confidence and ease.

Registering Your IRCTC Account (First-Time Users)

Before you can book any tickets, you'll need an IRCTC account. If you don't have one, visit the official IRCTC website (irctc.co.in) or open the mobile app and look for the 'Register' button, usually at the top right of the website or within the menu on the app. Click it to open the registration form. You'll need to provide basic details such as your name, date of birth, mobile number, email ID, and create a unique username and password. It is crucial to ensure your mobile number and email are active, as these will be used for verification and subsequent ticket delivery. You will receive an OTP (One Time Password) on both to activate your account. Once verified, your IRCTC account is ready for use. Remember to choose a strong password and keep your login details secure. This registration is a one-time process, simplifying all your future bookings.

Logging In and Planning Your Journey

With your account active, proceed to log in to the IRCTC website or mobile app using your registered username and password. Upon successful login, you'll see the main search panel prominently displayed on the homepage. This is where you'll input your journey specifics. Begin by selecting your 'From' station (source) and 'To' station (destination). You can type either the station code or the full name, and the system will provide relevant suggestions. Next, choose your 'Date of Journey' using the intuitive calendar tool. It's generally wise to plan and book your tickets well in advance, especially for popular routes or during peak travel and festive seasons. Finally, select your preferred 'Class' (e.g., Sleeper, AC 3 Tier, AC 2 Tier) and 'Quota' (e.g., General, Ladies, Tatkal). Once all details are entered, click 'Find Trains' to view available options.

Selecting Your Preferred Train and Class

After you click 'Find Trains', a comprehensive list of trains operating on your chosen route and date will be displayed. This list is invaluable, showing essential details such as the train number, train name, scheduled departure and arrival times, and the total journey duration. Importantly, it also highlights the various available classes on each train. For every train, you can easily check the seat availability for different classes. Simply click on the class you prefer (for instance, '3A' for AC 3 Tier) to see the number of berths available and the corresponding fare. You'll typically see 'AVL <number>' for available seats, 'RAC <number>' for Reservation Against Cancellation, or 'WL <number>' for Waiting List. Carefully choose the train and class that best fits your travel schedule and budget, then click 'Book Now' to proceed with your reservation.

Entering Passenger Details and Reviewing Your Booking

This stage is critical for providing accurate information for all individuals travelling. For each passenger, you must enter their full name, age, gender, and any specific berth preference (such as lower, middle, or upper berth), although preferences are not always guaranteed. If you are travelling with children under 5 years of age, you can add them without requiring a separate ticket or berth. Senior citizens should ensure they select the appropriate concession option if applicable and desired. Also, provide a contact mobile number and an email address where you wish to receive your ticket details and updates. Thoroughly review all the entered information, paying close attention to passenger names, ages, the selected train details, and the date of journey. Any discrepancies could lead to complications during your travel; ensure all details match the identification proof you intend to carry.

Choosing Payment Options and Confirming Your Ticket

Once you have meticulously verified all the details, you will be redirected to the secure payment gateway. IRCTC offers a wide array of payment methods designed for your convenience and security. These typically include major credit cards, debit cards from various Indian banks, net banking options provided by numerous financial institutions, and popular UPI (Unified Payments Interface) payment options such as PhonePe, Google Pay, or Paytm. Select your preferred payment method from the available choices and carefully follow the on-screen prompts to complete the transaction securely. After your payment is successfully processed, you will receive an immediate confirmation message on the IRCTC website. Concurrently, your e-ticket will be dispatched to your registered email ID and a confirmation SMS will be sent to your mobile number. It is highly advisable to save or print this e-ticket as it serves as your essential proof of reservation.

Accessing Your E-Ticket and Checking PNR Status

After your booking is successfully confirmed, accessing your e-ticket is straightforward. Simply log back into your IRCTC account, either on the website or through the mobile app, and navigate to the 'My Bookings' section. Here, you will find a comprehensive list of your current and past journeys. From this section, you have the flexibility to view, download, or print your e-ticket whenever needed. Your e-ticket prominently features the Passenger Name Record (PNR) number, which is a unique 10-digit code assigned to your booking. This PNR is vital as it allows you to check your ticket status, track berth confirmations, understand the current status of any waiting list tickets, and even ascertain your coach position. You can conveniently check your PNR status on the IRCTC website, via SMS, or through various reliable third-party railway apps by simply entering this number.

Important Tips for a Smooth IRCTC Booking Experience

To ensure you have the most seamless and stress-free IRCTC booking experience, consider these practical tips. For urgent travel needs, explore the 'Tatkal' quota; booking for AC classes opens at 10 AM, and for Non-AC classes at 11 AM, one day in advance of the journey date (excluding the journey date itself). During Tatkal bookings, tickets sell out exceptionally fast, so keep all passenger details pre-filled or ready to enter quickly. Always meticulously double-check both station codes and train numbers to avoid booking errors. If your ticket initially shows a waiting list status, monitor its PNR status regularly, as it often confirms closer to the journey date. For faster future bookings, consider utilizing the 'Master List' feature in your IRCTC profile to save passenger details. Finally, for security reasons, it's advisable to avoid making payments or sharing sensitive information when using public or unsecured Wi-Fi networks.

Conclusion

The introduction of online train ticket booking via IRCTC has undeniably transformed how millions of Indians approach travel, making it incredibly accessible and convenient. By diligently following this practical, step-by-step guide, you are now equipped to navigate the entire booking process, from initial registration to successfully receiving your e-ticket. Always remember the importance of planning your journey in advance, meticulously double-checking all your entered details, and wisely utilising the various features and quotas that IRCTC offers. These practices will contribute significantly to a seamless and stress-free booking experience. We wish you happy journey planning and trust that this guide will make your next train reservation a complete breeze!
