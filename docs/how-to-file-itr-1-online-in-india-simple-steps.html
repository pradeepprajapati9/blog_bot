How to File ITR-1 Online in India: Simple Steps

05 Oct 2026

Filing your Income Tax Return (ITR) can seem daunting, but it's a crucial annual financial task for many Indians. If you're an individual with a relatively straightforward income, the ITR-1 form, also known as Sahaj, is likely the one for you. This guide will walk you through the entire process of filing your ITR-1 online, breaking down each step into simple, easy-to-understand instructions. Understanding and correctly filing your ITR-1 ensures compliance with tax laws, helps avoid penalties, and allows you to claim any applicable refunds. Let's demystify the process and get your tax filing done right, directly from the comfort of your home using the official Income Tax e-filing portal.

Who Can File ITR-1 (Sahaj)?

The ITR-1 form, also known as Sahaj, is specifically designed for resident individuals in India whose total income does not exceed ₹50 Lakh. It's suitable for those whose income sources are straightforward and include salary, pension, or family pension. Additionally, individuals earning income from one house property, agricultural income up to ₹5,000, and income from other sources like interest from savings accounts, fixed deposits, or dividends, are eligible to use ITR-1. This form simplifies the filing process for a large segment of taxpayers. However, if you have income from capital gains, business or profession, more than one house property, or are a director in a company, you will need to file a different ITR form. Always confirm your eligibility before proceeding to ensure you use the correct form.

Documents You'll Need Before You Start

Before you begin filing your ITR-1 online, gathering all necessary documents is essential. This preparation will streamline the process and help you avoid errors. Key documents include your PAN card and Aadhaar card, which are mandatory for e-filing. You'll also need Form 16 issued by your employer, detailing your salary income and TDS (Tax Deducted at Source). Bank statements are crucial for interest income from savings accounts and fixed deposits. For details on any other income, like dividends, ensure you have the relevant statements. If you have made tax-saving investments under sections like 80C, 80D, etc., keep proof of these investments handy, such as LIC receipts, PPF passbook, or health insurance premium receipts. Having these documents organized beforehand makes data entry much smoother.

Registering and Logging into the Income Tax Portal

The first step for filing your ITR-1 online is to register on the official Income Tax Department's e-filing portal if you haven't already. Visit www.incometax.gov.in and click on "Register" to create an account using your PAN as your User ID. If you're an existing user, simply click "Login." Once logged in, navigate to the "e-File" section and select "Income Tax Returns." Choose the assessment year for which you are filing the return and then select "Online" as the mode of filing. This online method is user-friendly and guides you through each section. Ensure your profile details, including bank account for refunds, are updated and verified on the portal. This initial setup ensures a secure and personalized filing experience.

Step-by-Step Guide to Filing ITR-1 Online

After logging in and selecting the filing mode, you will proceed through several sections. First, verify your personal information, which will be pre-filled from your profile. Then, review the "Gross Total Income" section, carefully entering details from your Form 16, bank statements, and other income proofs. The portal often pre-fills some data from Annual Information Statement (AIS) or Taxpayer Information Summary (TIS), but always cross-verify. Next, enter your deductions under Chapter VI-A (e.g., 80C, 80D), ensuring you have proof for each. The system will then compute your tax liability. Finally, proceed to the "Taxes Paid" section to check if your TDS/TCS details match your Form 26AS. If there's any tax due, you'll need to pay it before submitting.

Verifying Your Return and Important Considerations

Once all details are entered and verified, the next crucial step is to verify your ITR-1. E-verification is mandatory and can be done instantly using Aadhaar OTP, Net Banking, or a Demat account. Alternatively, you can send a signed physical copy of the ITR-V (Acknowledgement) to the Income Tax Department's CPC, Bengaluru, within 30 days of e-filing. Failing to verify your return makes the filing invalid. Always double-check all figures, especially income details, deductions, and bank account information, to avoid discrepancies and potential notices. Also, ensure you file before the due date to prevent late fees and penalties. Keep a copy of your filed return and ITR-V for your records.

Conclusion

Filing your ITR-1 online might seem like a complex task, but by following these simple, step-by-step instructions, you can complete it accurately and efficiently. Remember, proper preparation with all necessary documents, careful data entry, and timely verification are key to a hassle-free experience. Taking charge of your tax filing not only ensures compliance but also reinforces your personal financial literacy. So, gather your documents, log in to the e-filing portal, and confidently complete your ITR-1 for the relevant assessment year. A well-filed return brings peace of mind and helps you stay on top of your financial responsibilities.
