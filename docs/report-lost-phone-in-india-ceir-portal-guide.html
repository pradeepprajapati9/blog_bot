Report Lost Phone in India: CEIR Portal Guide

20 Nov 2026

Losing your mobile phone can be a distressing experience, not just because of the device's cost, but also due to the valuable personal data it holds. In India, the government has launched the Central Equipment Identity Register (CEIR) portal to help citizens block their lost or stolen mobile phones. This crucial service aims to curb mobile phone theft and prevent the misuse of your device. Understanding how to use the CEIR portal effectively can save you from potential financial fraud and identity theft. This guide will walk you through the entire process, from reporting the loss to unblocking your phone if it's recovered.

Why Report a Lost/Stolen Phone Immediately?

When your phone goes missing, time is of the essence. Reporting it quickly isn't just about recovering the device; it's primarily about protecting your personal information and financial accounts. A stolen phone can be used to access your banking apps, UPI, social media, and even your digital documents like Aadhaar or PAN, leading to serious privacy breaches and financial fraud. Blocking your phone through CEIR ensures that even if a new SIM card is inserted, the device becomes unusable on any network in India. This step helps prevent thieves from reselling or misusing your device, effectively making it a 'brick' in their hands. Additionally, reporting it officially provides a record for insurance claims if you have mobile insurance.

What is the CEIR Portal?

The Central Equipment Identity Register (CEIR) is a government initiative under the Department of Telecommunications (DoT), India. Its main objective is to reduce mobile phone theft and protect consumer interests. The CEIR system can block all services on stolen or lost mobile phones across all telecom operators, even if the SIM card is changed. It maintains a database of all mobile devices based on their International Mobile Equipment Identity (IMEI) numbers. By blocking your phone's IMEI, CEIR makes the device inoperable, deterring thieves and making it harder for them to profit from stolen handsets. This platform is a vital tool for digital security for every Indian mobile user.

Documents and Information Needed Before You Start

Before you begin the blocking process on the CEIR portal, gather the following essential details and documents. Having these ready will make the process smooth and quick. You will need your phone's IMEI number, which can usually be found on the phone's original packaging box, the purchase invoice, or by dialing *#06# if your phone was still accessible. Keep the purchase invoice as proof of ownership. You'll also need a copy of the police complaint (FIR) you filed about the lost phone. Additionally, have an identity proof (like Aadhaar card, PAN card, Driving License) and the lost mobile number readily available. Details of when and where the phone was lost are also required.

Step-by-Step Guide to Block Your Phone on CEIR

Blocking your lost or stolen phone on the CEIR portal involves a few straightforward steps. First, immediately file a First Information Report (FIR) at your nearest police station or through your state police's online portal. You will need the FIR copy or complaint number. Next, visit the official CEIR portal at ceir.gov.in. On the homepage, click on the 'Block Stolen/Lost Mobile' option. You will then be prompted to fill out a form with details like the lost mobile number(s), IMEI number(s), device brand, model, and the place and date of loss. Crucially, upload a copy of your FIR and the phone's purchase invoice. Provide your personal details, including your identity proof and an alternative contact number for OTP verification. Once all details are filled accurately, submit the form and make note of the Request ID generated. This ID is important for tracking your request.

Checking Your Request Status and Unblocking Your Phone

After successfully submitting your request to block your phone on the CEIR portal, you can easily track its status. On the CEIR homepage, look for the 'Check Request Status' option. Enter the Request ID that was generated when you submitted the blocking form. The portal will then display the current status of your request, informing you if your phone has been blocked. If, by any chance, you recover your lost phone, you can also use the CEIR portal to unblock it. Simply select the 'Un-Block Found Mobile' option on the website. You will need to provide your Request ID, the mobile number, and the reason for unblocking. Once submitted, your phone will be removed from the blocked list and its services restored on all networks.

Important Safety Tips After Reporting

Reporting your phone on CEIR is a critical step, but your digital security efforts shouldn't stop there. Immediately change passwords for all important online accounts, especially email, banking, social media, and any payment apps linked to your lost phone. Inform your bank about the lost device and monitor your account statements for any suspicious activity. If you used any payment wallets, consider contacting their customer support. Ensure you have backups of your important data, photos, and contacts regularly, preferably on cloud storage or an external drive, so a lost phone doesn't mean lost memories or crucial information. Stay vigilant against potential phishing attempts that might follow a device loss, as scammers often try to exploit such situations.

Conclusion

Losing a mobile phone can be a frightening experience, but with the CEIR portal, Indian citizens have a powerful tool to protect themselves. By acting quickly and following the steps outlined in this guide – filing a police report, gathering necessary documents, and using the CEIR portal to block your device – you can significantly reduce the risk of misuse and potential fraud. Remember to always prioritize your digital security by backing up data and changing passwords promptly. The CEIR portal is a testament to India's commitment to digital safety, empowering you to take control in unfortunate situations.
