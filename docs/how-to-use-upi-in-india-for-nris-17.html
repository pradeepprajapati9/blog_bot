How to Use UPI in India for NRIs

07 Jul 2026

India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has transformed digital payments, making transactions instant and incredibly convenient. If you're a Non-Resident Indian (NRI) visiting home or managing finances in India, you might be wondering how you too can leverage this powerful system. Good news – UPI is very much accessible to NRIs with an Indian bank account. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know, from the essential setup requirements to making your first payment, ensuring you can navigate India's digital economy with ease.

UPI for NRIs - The Basics

UPI is a real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) that allows instant fund transfers between bank accounts using a single mobile application. For NRIs, UPI is invaluable for various purposes: paying bills, shopping at local stores, transferring money to family, or even settling restaurant tabs during your visits. It eliminates the need for cash or cards for most small to medium transactions. The core principle remains the same for NRIs as for residents: link your bank account to a UPI app via your registered mobile number, create a unique UPI ID (VPA - Virtual Payment Address), and set a UPI PIN for secure transactions. Understanding these fundamentals is the first step towards seamless digital payments in India.

Essential Requirements: Indian Bank Account & Mobile Number

To use UPI as an NRI, two primary requirements are non-negotiable: an active Indian bank account and an Indian mobile number. Your Indian bank account must be either an NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) account. Most major Indian banks now support UPI for these account types. Crucially, the Indian mobile number you use must be linked to your NRE/NRO account and must be registered for mobile banking services. Foreign mobile numbers generally cannot be used for UPI registration, even if they are internationally roaming. Ensure your Indian SIM is active and operational for receiving SMS for verification during the setup process. This link between your Indian bank account and Indian mobile number is the bedrock of UPI functionality for NRIs.

Linking Your NRI Account to UPI

Once you have an active NRE/NRO bank account and an Indian mobile number linked to it, setting up UPI is straightforward. First, download a UPI-enabled app from your smartphone's app store. Popular options include BHIM UPI (the government-backed app), or mobile banking apps from your specific bank (like SBI YONO, ICICI iMobile Pay, HDFC PayZapp). After installation, open the app and select your bank from the list. The app will verify your Indian mobile number by sending an SMS from your device. Ensure this is the same number linked to your bank account. Once verified, you'll be prompted to create your UPI ID (VPA) and set a 4 or 6-digit UPI PIN using your debit card details. Remember this PIN, as it's required for every transaction.

Key Banks Supporting NRI UPI

While many Indian banks now facilitate UPI for Non-Resident Indians, it's always wise to confirm with your specific bank. Generally, major public and private sector banks have integrated NRE/NRO accounts with UPI services. These include, but are not limited to, State Bank of India (SBI), ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, Punjab National Bank (PNB), Bank of Baroda, and Canara Bank. These banks typically allow you to link your NRE/NRO savings or current accounts to UPI apps. It's advisable to check your bank's official website or contact their NRI customer service desk to confirm the exact process and any specific requirements they might have, as policies can occasionally vary or be updated. This proactive step ensures a smooth setup experience.

Making Payments with UPI as an NRI

Using UPI for payments as an NRI is incredibly similar to how residents use it. Once your account is linked and UPI PIN is set, you can initiate various transactions. The most common methods include scanning a QR code at merchant outlets, sending money to a Virtual Payment Address (VPA) or a mobile number, or directly to a bank account using IFSC and account number. To pay via QR code, simply open your UPI app, select the 'Scan & Pay' option, point your camera at the merchant's QR code, enter the amount, and confirm with your UPI PIN. For VPA payments, enter the recipient's VPA, the amount, and your PIN. UPI also facilitates bill payments and recharges. Always double-check recipient details before confirming any transaction.

Important Considerations and Tips for NRIs

To ensure a secure and smooth UPI experience, NRIs should keep a few points in mind. Firstly, always keep your Indian mobile number active and topped up, especially if you reside abroad, to avoid disruption of UPI services or losing access to OTPs. Secondly, never share your UPI PIN or banking passwords with anyone, even if they claim to be from your bank or a government agency. UPI transactions are irreversible, so exercise caution. Be aware of daily transaction limits, which vary by bank and transaction type, usually ranging from Rs 10,000 to Rs 1,00,000 per day. Regularly update your UPI app to the latest version for security patches and new features. By following these simple tips, you can enjoy a safe and efficient UPI experience in India.

Conclusion

UPI offers an unparalleled level of convenience for NRIs managing financial needs or making payments during their stays in India. By understanding the straightforward requirements – an NRE/NRO bank account and an an active Indian mobile number – and following the simple setup process, you can effortlessly tap into India's digital payment ecosystem. From quick grocery purchases to sending funds to family, UPI simplifies your financial interactions. Embrace this technology to make your life easier and your transactions seamless, connecting you more effectively with India's vibrant digital landscape.
