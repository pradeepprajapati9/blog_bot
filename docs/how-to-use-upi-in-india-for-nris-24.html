How to Use UPI in India for NRIs

10 Jul 2026

India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has revolutionized digital transactions, making them instant and seamless. If you are a Non-Resident Indian (NRI), you might wonder how you can leverage this powerful payment system for your financial needs back home. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know about using UPI in India as an NRI, from eligibility requirements to making your first transaction. It offers a convenient, secure way to manage your expenses and transfers within India, directly from your NRE or NRO accounts.

Understanding UPI and Its Benefits for NRIs

UPI is an instant real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). It allows you to link multiple bank accounts to a single mobile application and make payments or transfer funds without needing to share bank account details. For NRIs, UPI offers significant advantages. You can pay for groceries, utility bills, recharges, or send money to family in India directly from your NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) accounts. This eliminates the need for international transfers for smaller, everyday expenses and provides a secure, quick, and efficient way to handle your finances while abroad, ensuring you stay connected to the Indian financial ecosystem.

NRI Eligibility and Account Requirements for UPI

To use UPI as an NRI, the primary requirement is to have either an NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) bank account with an Indian bank. Both types of accounts are permissible for UPI transactions. Crucially, your NRE or NRO account must be linked to an international mobile number (from countries like USA, UK, UAE, Singapore, Canada, Australia, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Hong Kong, France, Germany, Malaysia, Switzerland, Netherlands, Belgium, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, Denmark, Italy, Norway, Austria, Finland) that is registered with your bank account. The NPCI expanded UPI access to include international numbers from several countries, simplifying access for NRIs. Ensure your mobile number is updated with your Indian bank for smooth registration and transaction authentication.

Getting Started: Linking Your NRE/NRO Account to UPI

Once you have an eligible NRE/NRO account and your international mobile number is registered with your Indian bank, the next step is to download a UPI application. Popular choices include BHIM UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or your specific Indian bank's UPI app. After installation, open the app and select the option to register or link a bank account. The app will typically ask for permission to verify your mobile number by sending an SMS. Ensure your international SIM card is active and capable of sending/receiving SMS, as this verification step is crucial. This SMS verification links your mobile number to your Indian bank accounts registered with the same number.

The UPI Registration Process for NRIs Explained

After mobile number verification, you will need to choose your bank from the list provided in the UPI app. The app will then fetch the NRE/NRO accounts associated with your verified mobile number. Select the account you wish to link for UPI transactions. The final crucial step is to set up your UPI PIN. This is a 4 or 6-digit number that acts as your transaction password. You will typically be prompted to enter your debit card details (last 6 digits and expiry date) associated with your NRE/NRO account to generate the PIN. Remember this PIN, as it will be required to authorize all your UPI payments and fund transfers. Keep it confidential and never share it with anyone.

Making and Receiving Payments with UPI as an NRI

Using UPI for transactions is straightforward. To send money, you can choose options like "Send Money," "Pay to Contact," or "Scan QR." You'll need the recipient's UPI ID (Virtual Payment Address or VPA), their mobile number (if they are also on UPI), or a QR code. Enter the amount, add a remark if desired, and then enter your UPI PIN to complete the transaction. Funds are transferred instantly. To receive money, you can share your UPI ID with the sender, who can then initiate a payment to you. Some apps also allow you to raise a "collect request," where you request money from a sender, who then approves it using their UPI PIN.

Important Considerations and Security Tips for NRIs Using UPI

While convenient, it's vital to use UPI securely. Always use strong, unique UPI PINs and never share them with anyone, even bank officials. Be cautious of phishing attempts or suspicious requests for your PIN. Transaction limits for UPI are generally up to INR 1 lakh per day for most transactions, though some banks or merchant categories might have different limits. Check with your bank for specific details regarding your NRE/NRO account. If you change your international mobile number or bank account, remember to update these details with your Indian bank immediately and re-register for UPI if necessary. Keeping your UPI app and operating system updated is also a good security practice to protect against vulnerabilities.

Conclusion

UPI offers an incredibly convenient and secure way for NRIs to manage their financial needs in India. By understanding the eligibility criteria, linking your NRE or NRO account to a UPI app via your international mobile number, and setting up your UPI PIN, you can seamlessly make payments and transfers. This digital payment revolution ensures that despite being abroad, you remain well-connected to India's financial ecosystem, handling everything from bill payments to sending money to loved ones with just a few taps on your smartphone. Embrace UPI for a hassle-free financial experience in India.
