How to Use UPI on Your Keypad Phone (Using *99#)

14 Aug 2026

Even if you rely on a keypad phone, you can still enjoy the convenience and security of UPI for digital payments in India. You don't need a smartphone, internet connection, or complex apps to send or receive money. The National Unified USSD Platform (NUUP), primarily accessed by dialing *99#, makes UPI accessible to millions of feature phone users. This guide will walk you through how to set up and use this powerful service, bringing the power of digital transactions directly to your everyday keypad phone. It's a truly inclusive way to manage your money.

Understanding UPI on Keypad Phones with *99#

The *99# service is a special initiative by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) designed to bring UPI to feature phone users. It operates on the USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data) protocol, which means it works on any GSM mobile phone, regardless of whether it's a basic keypad phone or a smartphone. The best part is, you don't need an internet connection to use it. Just like checking your balance or activating services by dialing a short code, *99# allows you to perform various UPI transactions using simple menu options displayed on your phone screen. This service ensures that financial inclusion reaches every corner of India, empowering more people to transact digitally with ease.

What You Need Before You Start

To begin using UPI on your keypad phone via *99#, you'll need a few essentials. First, you must have an active bank account with any UPI-enabled bank. Secondly, the mobile number you wish to use for UPI must be linked to this bank account. Crucially, this same mobile number should be inserted into your keypad phone. If you haven't used UPI before, you'll need to set up a UPI PIN. This can usually be done through your bank's mobile banking app (if you temporarily use a smartphone), internet banking, or an ATM. For *99# itself, you can also often set or reset your UPI PIN within the service by providing your debit card details (last 6 digits and expiry date). Ensure you have these details handy before initiating your first transaction.

Step-by-Step Guide to Sending Money via *99#

Sending money using *99# is straightforward. Start by dialing *99# from your keypad phone. You'll then be prompted to select your preferred language. Next, the system will ask you to enter the first few letters of your bank's name or its IFSC code. After this, a menu will appear with various options like 'Send Money', 'Receive Money', 'Check Balance', and 'UPI PIN'. Select the 'Send Money' option. You'll then be given choices on how to send money, typically by mobile number, UPI ID, or bank account number with IFSC. The mobile number option is often the simplest if the recipient has their number linked to UPI. Enter the recipient's details, the amount you wish to send, and an optional remark. Finally, enter your UPI PIN to confirm the transaction. You'll receive an SMS confirmation once the payment is successful.

Other Useful *99# Services and Features

Beyond sending money, the *99# service offers several other handy features to manage your UPI transactions. One of the most frequently used options is 'Check Account Balance'. By selecting this from the main menu and entering your UPI PIN, you can instantly view your bank account balance. If you ever forget your UPI PIN or wish to change it for security reasons, you can select the 'UPI PIN' option from the main menu. Here, you'll find choices to 'Change PIN' or 'Forgot PIN'. For resetting, you will need to provide the last 6 digits and expiry date of your debit card linked to your bank account. You can also view a limited history of your recent transactions and even raise a request to receive money from another UPI user, though this is less common for keypad phone users.

Important Tips for Safe and Secure UPI Transactions

While *99# makes UPI highly accessible, practicing good security habits is crucial. Your UPI PIN is the key to your funds; never share it with anyone, not even bank officials or customer service representatives. Always double-check the recipient's mobile number, UPI ID, or bank account details before confirming any payment to avoid sending money to the wrong person. Be wary of any calls or messages asking for your UPI PIN, OTP, or personal details, especially if they claim to be from your bank or a government agency. These are often phishing attempts. After every transaction, ensure you receive an SMS confirmation from your bank, and regularly monitor your bank statements for any suspicious activity. Remember that there are transaction limits for *99# (typically Rs. 5000 per transaction), so plan accordingly for larger payments.

Limitations and What to Remember

While the *99# service is incredibly convenient for keypad phone users, it does have a few limitations compared to smartphone UPI apps. You cannot scan QR codes to make payments, as keypad phones lack this functionality. The transaction limits are generally lower, often capped at Rs. 5,000 per transaction, and a small service charge (usually around 50 paise) may be applied by your mobile network operator for each *99# session, although some banks might subsidize this. However, these minor points do not diminish the immense value of *99# in bringing digital payments to a broader audience. It serves as a robust, secure, and widely accessible alternative for basic phone users to participate in India's digital economy, making everyday transactions simpler and quicker.

Conclusion

Using UPI on your keypad phone through the *99# service is a testament to India's commitment to digital inclusion. It empowers millions who may not have access to smartphones or internet connectivity to perform safe and efficient digital transactions. From sending money to family to checking your bank balance, *99# offers essential banking services right from your basic mobile device. By following this guide and prioritizing security, you can confidently embrace digital payments and experience the convenience of UPI, proving that modern financial tools are truly for everyone, regardless of their phone type.
