How to Use UPI with an International Number: Guide for NRIs

14 Aug 2026

For years, using India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) system primarily required an Indian mobile number linked to an Indian bank account. This presented a hurdle for Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) wanting to make seamless digital payments in India using their international phone numbers. However, recent significant developments have changed this landscape. The National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) has expanded UPI access, making it possible for NRIs to link their international mobile numbers to their NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) accounts. This guide breaks down exactly how NRIs can leverage this new facility, ensuring you can make secure and convenient UPI transactions in India, regardless of your current mobile number.

Understanding UPI and Its Traditional Requirement

The Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has revolutionized digital payments in India, offering instant, real-time transactions between bank accounts. It's a system built on a secure, mobile-first platform, allowing users to send or receive money directly through their smartphones. Traditionally, the core requirement for using UPI was a mobile number registered in India, which needed to be linked to an active Indian bank account. This linkage was essential for the system to verify the user's identity and connect them to their financial institution. While incredibly convenient for residents, this setup meant that NRIs often had to maintain an active Indian SIM card or rely on family members for payments, even if they had an Indian bank account. This created a gap in financial connectivity for the diaspora, hindering their ability to independently manage finances and make payments back home.

The Game-Changer: UPI for NRIs with International Numbers

Recognizing the growing need and demand from the NRI community, the NPCI, which operates UPI, issued a circular in April 2023. This landmark decision enabled NRIs from specific countries to link their international mobile numbers to their NRE or NRO bank accounts held in India for UPI transactions. This means that if you're an NRI with an eligible Indian bank account, you no longer necessarily need an active Indian SIM card to access the convenience of UPI. The initial list of countries included Singapore, Australia, Canada, Hong Kong, Oman, Qatar, USA, Saudi Arabia, UAE, and the UK, with more expected to be added. This expansion marks a significant step towards integrating NRIs more fully into India's digital economy, making cross-border financial interactions much smoother.

Eligibility Criteria and Account Types

To take advantage of UPI with your international mobile number, there are specific eligibility criteria primarily centered around your banking relationship in India. This facility is exclusively available to Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) who hold either an NRE (Non-Resident External) account or an NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) account with an Indian bank. An NRE account allows you to remit foreign earnings to India, and both principal and interest are fully repatriable. An NRO account, on the other hand, is for managing income earned in India, such as rent or dividends, and while the interest is repatriable, the principal has certain restrictions. It's crucial that your international mobile number is accurately registered and linked to one of these NRI bank accounts. Foreign nationals who do not hold an NRE or NRO account in India are currently not eligible for this service.

How to Get Started: The Process

Once you confirm your eligibility as an NRI with an NRE/NRO account and an international mobile number from an approved country, the process to get started is relatively straightforward. First, ensure that your international mobile number is updated and linked to your NRE or NRO account with your specific Indian bank. You might need to contact your bank's NRI services or visit a branch during a visit to India to ensure this crucial link is established and your KYC (Know Your Customer) details are complete and up-to-date. After your number is linked, download any popular UPI-enabled payment application (like Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or your bank’s UPI app) from your respective app store. During registration, select your Indian bank, and the app will typically verify your international mobile number against the one registered with your NRE/NRO account. Once verified, you can set up your UPI PIN and begin transacting.

Benefits and Use Cases for NRIs

The ability to use UPI with an international number offers immense practical benefits for NRIs. It dramatically simplifies financial transactions in India, whether you're living abroad or visiting. You can effortlessly pay for services, shop at local merchants, settle utility bills, or even send money to family and friends in India, all from your smartphone. This eliminates the need to carry cash, convert currency, or rely on traditional banking methods that might involve higher fees or longer processing times. For NRIs managing property or investments in India, this feature allows for direct and immediate payments, such as maintenance charges or utility bills, enhancing convenience and giving you greater control over your Indian financial affairs without needing to be physically present.

Important Considerations and Limitations

While the new UPI functionality for NRIs is a game-changer, there are a few important considerations. Firstly, while NPCI has enabled this, individual banks need to implement the feature. So, it's wise to confirm with your specific Indian bank if they support linking international numbers to NRE/NRO accounts for UPI. Secondly, remember that your international mobile number must be registered with your bank for your NRE/NRO account. Any mismatch will prevent successful UPI registration. Be mindful of potential SMS charges from your international mobile service provider for OTPs (One-Time Passwords) received from Indian banks or UPI apps. This facility is strictly for NRIs with NRE/NRO accounts, not for foreign visitors or residents without these specific Indian banking relationships. Always ensure your phone's security and never share your UPI PIN or banking details with anyone.

Ensuring Security and Smooth Transactions

Security is paramount when dealing with financial transactions, especially across international borders. When using UPI with your international number, always download UPI applications from official app stores (Google Play Store or Apple App Store) to avoid counterfeit apps. Create a strong, unique UPI PIN and never share it with anyone. Be cautious of phishing attempts or fraudulent messages asking for your UPI PIN or other sensitive information. Always verify the recipient's details before initiating a payment. Keep your international mobile number updated with your bank, and ensure your phone's operating system and the UPI apps are always updated to the latest versions for enhanced security features. If you encounter any issues, always contact your Indian bank's official customer support or the UPI app's helpline directly for assistance.

Conclusion

The NPCI's initiative to allow NRIs to use UPI with their international mobile numbers is a significant leap forward, bridging the geographical gap in financial connectivity. By linking your NRE or NRO account to your international number, you can now enjoy the unparalleled convenience and security of UPI for all your payment needs in India. Remember to ensure your banking details are up-to-date and follow the steps outlined, always prioritizing security. This move empowers NRIs with greater control and ease in managing their financial interactions with India, making digital payments truly global.
