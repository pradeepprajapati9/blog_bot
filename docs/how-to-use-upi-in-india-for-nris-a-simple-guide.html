How to Use UPI in India for NRIs: A Simple Guide

06 Jul 2026

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has transformed digital transactions in India, making payments instant and seamless. For Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), UPI offers an incredibly convenient way to manage expenses, send money to family, or make purchases while visiting India. Imagine paying for groceries, a taxi, or even utility bills with just a few taps on your phone. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to set up and use UPI effectively and securely with your NRE or NRO account in India.

Eligibility and Essential Requirements for NRIs to Use UPI

To start using UPI as an NRI, there are a few fundamental requirements you must meet. Firstly, you need an active NRE (Non-Resident External) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) bank account with a bank in India that supports NRI UPI services. Many major banks like ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank, SBI, Axis Bank, and others now offer this facility. Secondly, and critically, you must have an Indian mobile number. This number needs to be registered with your Indian bank account and be active for receiving SMS. This is because UPI transactions are authenticated via your registered mobile number. Currently, NRIs from specific countries (like Singapore, Australia, Canada, Hong Kong, Oman, Qatar, USA, Saudi Arabia, UAE, UK) are gradually being enabled to use international mobile numbers registered to their Indian bank accounts for UPI, but an Indian number is still the most widely supported and reliable option.

Setting Up Your UPI App on Your Smartphone

Once you have your Indian mobile number linked to your NRE/NRO account, the setup process is straightforward. First, download any popular UPI-enabled app from your smartphone's app store (Google Play Store or Apple App Store). Popular choices include BHIM UPI, Google Pay (GPay), PhonePe, or Paytm. Open the app and select your bank from the list. The app will then initiate an SMS verification process. Ensure the SIM card for your Indian mobile number is in your phone, as the app will send an SMS from it to verify your identity and link your bank account. Once verified, you will be prompted to set a UPI PIN (usually 4 or 6 digits) using your debit card details linked to your NRE/NRO account. This MPIN (Mobile Personal Identification Number) is crucial for authorizing all your transactions.

Linking Your NRE/NRO Account for UPI Transactions

After successful mobile number verification, your chosen UPI app will display a list of bank accounts linked to that number. Carefully select your NRE or NRO account. It is vital to ensure you pick the correct account type to avoid any issues with foreign exchange regulations or tax implications. Most UPI apps will clearly indicate the account type (NRE/NRO) if your bank provides that detail. If you have multiple accounts with the same bank linked to your mobile number, double-check that you're selecting the specific NRE/NRO account you intend to use for UPI. Once selected, you're ready to transact. If you face any issues linking, ensure your mobile number is correctly updated and active with your Indian bank for your NRE/NRO account.

How to Send Money Using UPI as an NRI

Sending money via UPI is incredibly simple. Once your account is linked and MPIN set, open your UPI app. You'll typically see options like 'Scan any QR code', 'Pay to UPI ID/VPA', or 'Pay to Bank Account'. For quick payments, scanning a merchant's QR code is common. Point your phone's camera at the QR code, enter the amount, and then your UPI PIN to authorize the payment. To send money to a specific person, you can use their UPI ID (also known as Virtual Payment Address or VPA, e.g., 'yourname@bankname'). Enter their VPA, the amount, and confirm with your UPI PIN. The money is transferred instantly from your linked NRE/NRO account to the recipient's account. This is ideal for paying family, friends, or local service providers in India.

Receiving Money Through UPI into Your NRI Account

Yes, as an NRI with a linked NRE/NRO account, you can also receive money via UPI. Your UPI ID (VPA) acts as your address for receiving funds. You can share your VPA with anyone who wants to send you money. They simply need to open their UPI app, select the 'Pay to UPI ID/VPA' option, enter your VPA, the amount they wish to send, and authorize the transaction with their UPI PIN. The funds will then be instantly credited to your linked NRE/NRO account. This feature is particularly useful if someone in India needs to send you money for legitimate purposes, or if you receive refunds from Indian merchants.

Important Considerations and Transaction Limits for NRIs

While UPI offers immense convenience, NRIs should be aware of certain guidelines. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) sets daily transaction limits for UPI, typically up to INR 1,00,000 (One Lakh Rupees) for general transactions and higher for specific categories like IPOs or capital markets. Your bank might also impose its own limits, so it's wise to check with them. Always ensure your mobile phone is secure and your UPI PIN is kept confidential. Never share your PIN with anyone, and be wary of phishing attempts. UPI transactions are irreversible once authenticated, so always double-check recipient details before confirming. For any account-related queries or disputes, contact your Indian bank's customer support directly.

Conclusion

UPI truly empowers NRIs with a fast, secure, and incredibly user-friendly method for managing finances in India. By following these simple steps, you can confidently set up your UPI app, link your NRE or NRO account, and enjoy the convenience of instant digital payments. Whether you're paying bills, shopping, or supporting family, UPI simplifies your financial interactions in India, making your visits and connections smoother than ever.
