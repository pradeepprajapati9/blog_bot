How to Use UPI in India for NRIs

10 Jul 2026

As an NRI (Non-Resident Indian), staying connected with financial transactions in India can be incredibly convenient with the Unified Payments Interface (UPI). Whether you're sending money to family, paying bills, or making purchases back home, understanding how to use UPI as an NRI is essential. This guide will walk you through the process, ensuring you can leverage this powerful digital payment system seamlessly.

Understanding UPI for NRIs

UPI is a real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). It allows you to transfer funds between bank accounts instantly. For NRIs, the key difference is that you must link your Indian bank account to a UPI-enabled app. Your foreign bank account cannot be directly linked to UPI. This means you'll need an existing savings or current account with an Indian bank that supports UPI. The process is designed to be secure and user-friendly, allowing you to make transactions using just your phone number or a unique UPI ID, without needing to share sensitive bank account details frequently. It's a crucial tool for managing your finances in India remotely.

Prerequisites for NRI UPI Users

Before you can start using UPI, ensure you have a few things in place. First, you need an active Indian bank account. This account must be a savings or current account, and it needs to be KYC (Know Your Customer) compliant. Your bank must also support UPI services. Secondly, you'll require a mobile number that is registered with your Indian bank account. This mobile number will be used for verification and receiving One-Time Passwords (OTPs). It's important to note that this registered mobile number doesn't necessarily have to be an Indian SIM card, but it must be the one your bank recognizes for your account. Lastly, a smartphone with internet access is necessary to download and operate any UPI application.

Choosing a UPI App and Linking Your Account

Several UPI-enabled apps are available in India. Popular choices include Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM (the official UPI app), and your own bank's mobile banking app. Download your preferred app from your smartphone's app store. Once installed, open the app and select the option to link your bank account. You'll be prompted to select your Indian bank from the list. The app will then send an SMS to your registered mobile number to verify it. After successful verification, you can proceed to link your bank account. You'll usually need to enter the last six digits of your debit card and its expiry date to set up your UPI PIN.

Setting Up Your UPI PIN

The UPI PIN (Personal Identification Number) is a critical security feature. It's a 4 or 6-digit code that you'll use to authorize all your UPI transactions. You will be asked to set this PIN during the account linking process. If you already have a UPI PIN set up for your bank account with a different app, you can often reuse it. However, if you're setting it up for the first time or want to reset it, follow the app's instructions. You'll typically receive an OTP from your bank on your registered mobile number. Enter this OTP and then create your new UPI PIN. Remember to choose a PIN that is easy for you to remember but difficult for others to guess. Never share your UPI PIN with anyone, not even bank employees.

Making Transactions as an NRI

Once your account is linked and your UPI PIN is set, you're ready to make transactions. You can send money to other UPI users by entering their UPI ID or mobile number linked to their bank account. You can also pay merchants by scanning their QR codes. To initiate a payment, open your UPI app, select the send money option, enter the recipient's details, enter the amount, and then authenticate the transaction with your UPI PIN. For bill payments or recharges, navigate to the relevant section within the app and follow the prompts. Remember, all transactions will be debited from your linked Indian bank account. Ensure you have sufficient funds in your account before initiating any payment.

Important Considerations for NRIs

There are a few specific points for NRIs to keep in mind. While you can use your overseas mobile number for registration with the UPI app, it must be linked to your Indian bank account. If you change your Indian bank account or your registered mobile number, you will need to re-link your account and set up your UPI PIN. Also, be aware of any transaction limits set by your bank or the UPI app. Some banks may have daily or per-transaction limits for UPI transactions. It's always a good idea to check with your bank for any specific policies or restrictions that might apply to NRI accounts using UPI. Stay vigilant about phishing attempts and ensure you are using official apps from trusted sources.

Conclusion

Using UPI in India as an NRI is a straightforward process that offers immense convenience. By following these steps, linking your Indian bank account correctly, and securing your UPI PIN, you can confidently manage your financial transactions in India. This digital payment system empowers you to stay connected and in control of your finances, no matter where you are in the world.
