How to Check Land Records Online in India: A Simple Guide

08 Sep 2026

Checking land records is a crucial task for any property owner or prospective buyer in India. Gone are the days of endless visits to government offices. Today, most states offer online portals, often known as 'Bhulekh,' to access these vital documents from the comfort of your home. These digital records provide transparency and make it easier to verify property details. This guide will walk you through the simple steps to access your land records online across India, helping you understand what information to look for and how to use these digital platforms effectively.

Why Checking Land Records Online is Essential

Accessing land records online offers numerous benefits, especially in preventing property disputes and fraud. For buyers, it ensures the property title is clear and free from encumbrances, verifying the actual owner and land history. For sellers, it streamlines the documentation process. Online records also provide transparency, reducing the need for intermediaries and potential for manipulation. Whether you're applying for a loan, planning construction, or simply want to confirm your own property details, having easy access to these digital records is a powerful tool for safeguarding your assets and making informed decisions. It empowers citizens with critical information that was once difficult to obtain.

What Information Can You Find in Online Land Records?

When you access land records online, you're typically looking for several key pieces of information. The most common are Khasra and Khatauni numbers. A Khasra number is a plot number, essentially an identification number assigned to a specific parcel of land in rural areas. A Khatauni number is an account number provided to a set of farmers who jointly own a parcel of land, detailing their shares. You might also find Jamabandi records (especially in states like Punjab and Haryana) which are records of rights that contain land ownership details, cultivation information, and other rights. Additionally, you can often see the type of land (agricultural, residential), its area, and details of any mortgages or liens.

General Steps to Check Land Records Online Across India

While specific portal interfaces may vary by state, the general process to check land records online remains largely consistent. First, you need to identify and visit your state's official land records portal (e.g., Bhulekh UP, MahaBhulekh, Bhoomi Karnataka). Look for an option like "View Land Records," "Khasra Khatauni," or "ROR (Record of Rights)." You will then typically select your district, tehsil (sub-district), and village from dropdown menus. Next, you'll be prompted to search for the records using various criteria – usually by Khasra number, Khatauni number, owner's name, or even by mutation date. Enter the required details accurately to retrieve the relevant land record information.

Key State-Specific Portals and Terminology Examples

Understanding state-specific portals and their terminology is key to navigating the online land record system in India. For instance, in Uttar Pradesh, the portal is widely known as "Bhulekh UP," where you can search for Khasra and Khatauni. Maharashtra uses "MahaBhulekh" to access 7/12 (Saat Baara Utara) and 8A extracts, which are crucial land records. In Karnataka, the "Bhoomi" portal is used to check RTC (Record of Rights, Tenancy, and Crops) documents. Rajasthan has "Apna Khata," and Haryana uses "Jamabandi Haryana." While the core information is similar, the names of documents or search methods might differ, so always familiarize yourself with your specific state's portal and its common terms before you begin your search.

How to Download or Print Your Online Land Records

Once you have successfully searched for and viewed your land records on the state portal, you will usually find an option to either download or print the document. Most portals offer two types of copies: a viewable/un-certified copy and a certified copy. The un-certified copy is for general information and viewing purposes and is usually free to download as a PDF. For official purposes, such as applying for a loan or during a property transaction, you will likely need a certified copy. These certified copies typically involve a small fee and can be applied for online, often with a digital signature, or collected from a designated government office after online payment. Always ensure you are on the official government portal when making any payments.

Important Tips and Cautions When Using Online Land Records

While online land records offer convenience, it's crucial to exercise caution. Always ensure you are accessing the official government portal of your respective state. Beware of third-party websites that may offer similar services but could be inaccurate or fraudulent. Cross-verify the details found online with any physical documents you possess or acquire. If there are discrepancies, contact your local Patwari, Tehsildar, or the concerned revenue department office immediately for clarification and correction. Remember that a digitally generated un-certified copy may not be legally acceptable for all official transactions. For legal validity, always obtain a certified copy, for which a nominal fee may be charged.

Conclusion

Accessing land records online through various state Bhulekh portals has revolutionized how property information is managed and shared in India. This digital initiative promotes transparency, reduces bureaucratic hurdles, and significantly empowers citizens with vital property details. By following the simple steps outlined in this guide and understanding the key terminology, you can easily verify ownership, check for encumbrances, and make informed decisions related to your land. Always prioritize official sources and take necessary precautions to ensure the accuracy and legal validity of the documents you obtain.
