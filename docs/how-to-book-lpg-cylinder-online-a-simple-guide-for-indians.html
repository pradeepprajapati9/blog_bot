How to Book LPG Cylinder Online: A Simple Guide for Indians

30 Oct 2026

Booking your LPG gas cylinder might seem like a small task, but doing it efficiently can save you time and effort. Gone are the days when you had to visit your distributor in person or wait endlessly on the phone. With the rise of digital services, booking your cooking gas refill online has become incredibly simple, convenient, and often comes with added benefits. This guide will walk you through the various methods to book your LPG cylinder online, catering to users of Indane, HP Gas, and Bharat Gas, ensuring a hassle-free experience right from your home, office, or even while on the go.

Why Online LPG Booking is Your Best Bet

Opting for online LPG cylinder booking offers a multitude of advantages over traditional methods. Firstly, it provides unparalleled convenience; you can book a refill 24/7, from anywhere, without being tied to distributor office hours. This saves significant time and eliminates the need to stand in queues. Secondly, online platforms offer transparent tracking, allowing you to monitor the status of your booking and expected delivery. You'll receive real-time updates via SMS or app notifications. Moreover, digital payments mean you don't need to worry about carrying cash or exact change. Many third-party payment apps and even official distributor portals often provide attractive cashback offers or discounts on online gas bookings, adding a financial incentive. It's a smart, efficient way to manage a regular household chore.

Booking Through Official Distributor Websites

The most direct way to book your LPG cylinder online is through the official websites of your gas service provider. Whether you use Indane (IndianOil), HP Gas, or Bharat Gas, each has a dedicated portal. Start by visiting the respective website – for example, cx.indianoil.in for Indane, myhpgas.in for HP Gas, or my.ebharatgas.com for Bharat Gas. If you're a first-time user, you'll need to register using your consumer number, registered mobile number, or Aadhaar number. Once registered and logged in, look for the 'Book Cylinder' or 'Refill Booking' option. Verify your consumer details, confirm your booking, and then proceed to the payment gateway. You can choose from various online payment methods like debit cards, credit cards, Net Banking, or UPI. A confirmation message will be sent to your registered mobile number.

Using Mobile Apps for Quick Refills

For smartphone users, mobile applications offer an even faster way to book an LPG cylinder. All major distributors have their own official apps: 'IndianOil One' for Indane, 'HP Pay' for HP Gas, and 'Bharat Gas' (from BPCL) for Bharat Gas users. Download the relevant app from your app store, register or log in, and you'll find a straightforward option to book a refill. Beyond these, popular third-party payment apps like Paytm, Google Pay, PhonePe, and Amazon Pay also facilitate LPG cylinder bookings. Simply open the app, navigate to the 'LPG' or 'Gas Cylinder' booking section, select your gas provider, enter your registered mobile number or consumer ID, confirm details, and complete the payment. These apps often integrate loyalty programs or offer instant discounts, making them a popular choice.

Alternative Methods: SMS and IVRS

For those who prefer not to use internet portals or smartphones, or in situations with poor internet connectivity, booking via SMS or IVRS (Interactive Voice Response System) remains a reliable option. Each LPG distributor has a specific SMS format and a dedicated IVRS number for each state or region. For SMS booking, you typically send a specific keyword (like 'REFILL') to a designated number from your registered mobile number. For IVRS, you dial the toll-free number provided by your distributor (e.g., 7718955555 for Indane across India), and follow the automated voice prompts. You'll need to input your consumer number. These methods ensure that even users with basic mobile phones or limited digital literacy can easily book their LPG cylinder refill, maintaining accessibility for everyone.

Tracking Your Booking and Payment Options

Once your LPG cylinder is booked, keeping track of its status is simple. If you booked through an official website or app, you can usually check the 'Order History' or 'Track Booking' section for real-time updates on delivery. Many distributors also send SMS notifications at different stages, from booking confirmation to dispatch and delivery. When it comes to payment, online booking offers flexibility. You can pre-pay using Net Banking, credit/debit cards, UPI, or various digital wallets. Some services also offer a 'Cash on Delivery' option, allowing you to pay the delivery person when your cylinder arrives. This flexibility ensures that you can choose the payment method that is most convenient and secure for you, aligning with your preferences and financial habits.

Essential Tips for a Hassle-Free Booking Experience

To ensure your online LPG booking experience is always smooth, keep a few key tips in mind. Firstly, always ensure your mobile number is registered and updated with your LPG distributor. This is crucial for receiving confirmations, tracking updates, and OTPs if needed. Keep your consumer number handy, as it's often required for logging in or booking. Always use official websites or trusted apps to avoid scams or fraudulent activities. Double-check your delivery address before confirming the booking to prevent any delays or misdeliveries. If you are eligible for an LPG subsidy under the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scheme, ensure your Aadhaar is linked to your LPG connection and bank account to receive the subsidy directly. Following these simple steps will make your LPG cylinder booking process efficient and secure.

Conclusion

Booking your LPG cylinder online is no longer a luxury but a standard convenience that saves you time, effort, and often money. By utilizing official distributor websites, mobile apps, or even simpler SMS and IVRS methods, you gain control over your refills, track deliveries, and benefit from secure digital transactions. Embrace these digital tools to streamline one of your household's most essential needs, ensuring you always have cooking gas ready when you need it. Make the switch today and experience the ease of modern LPG booking!
