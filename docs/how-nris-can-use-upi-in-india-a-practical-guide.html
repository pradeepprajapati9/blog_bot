How NRIs Can Use UPI in India: A Practical Guide

09 Jul 2026

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has revolutionized digital payments in India, making transactions instant and seamless. Until recently, its full utility for Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) was limited. However, with recent updates, NRIs can now conveniently use UPI for various payments within India, linking their NRE or NRO bank accounts to their international mobile numbers. This guide will walk you through the practical steps to set up and use UPI, ensuring you can manage your Indian financial transactions with ease, whether it's supporting family, paying bills, or making purchases during your visits.

What is UPI and Why it Matters for NRIs?

UPI is an instant payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). It allows you to transfer money between any two parties' bank accounts by using a single identifier, called a Virtual Payment Address (VPA), or a mobile number, without needing to share bank account details. For NRIs, this is a game-changer. It means you can quickly send money to family in India, pay utility bills for your properties, manage subscriptions, or even make purchases while visiting, all from your phone. No more relying solely on international wire transfers or carrying excessive cash. UPI offers unparalleled convenience, speed, and security for your financial dealings in India.

Eligibility for NRIs: Your NRE/NRO Account and International Mobile Number

To use UPI in India as an NRI, there are a few key prerequisites. Firstly, you must have an active Non-Resident External (NRE) or Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO) bank account with an Indian bank that supports UPI for NRIs. Most major Indian banks now offer this facility. Secondly, and crucially, your NRE or NRO account must be linked to your international mobile number. This is a recent and significant update, as previously only Indian mobile numbers were supported. Many countries are now supported for this feature, allowing you to use your overseas phone number to register. Ensure your bank account details, especially your mobile number, are updated with your bank to avoid any issues during registration.

Linking Your Account: Step-by-Step with Your Bank or UPI App

Once you meet the eligibility criteria, the process of linking your account is straightforward. You will need to download a UPI-enabled mobile application. This could be your bank's official mobile banking app (e.g., SBI YONO, ICICI iMobile, HDFC MobileBanking) or a third-party UPI app like Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm. Open the app and select the option to register for UPI. You will be prompted to verify your international mobile number via an SMS. Ensure that the international SIM card is active in your phone during this step. After successful verification, select your Indian bank account (NRE/NRO) from the list provided by the app. The app will fetch your account details linked to that mobile number.

Activating UPI and Setting Your PIN

After linking your NRE/NRO account, the next step is to activate UPI for that account and set your UPI PIN. The UPI app will typically guide you through this. You will need your debit card details associated with your NRE/NRO account for this one-time setup. Input the last six digits of your debit card number and the expiry date. Following this, you will receive an OTP (One-Time Password) on your registered international mobile number. Enter the OTP in the app and then create a 4 or 6-digit UPI PIN. This PIN is crucial as it will be required to authorize all your UPI transactions. Remember this PIN and keep it confidential. Once your UPI PIN is successfully set, your NRE/NRO account is ready for UPI transactions.

Making Payments and Key Usage Scenarios

With your UPI account active, you can now enjoy effortless payments in India. You can send money to any Indian bank account using their UPI ID, mobile number, or by scanning a QR code. For instance, you can easily pay your family's bills, transfer funds to their accounts for daily expenses, or even pay for services and shopping in India. The transaction limits for NRIs using UPI are generally consistent with resident Indians, typically up to INR 1,00,000 per transaction, though daily limits might vary slightly by bank and app. Always check the specific limits imposed by your bank or the UPI app you are using. UPI works 24/7, even on holidays, making it incredibly reliable for urgent payments.

Security, Limits, and Important Tips for NRI UPI Users

While UPI offers immense convenience, it is essential to prioritize security. Always use a strong screen lock on your phone and keep your UPI PIN confidential. Never share it with anyone. Be wary of phishing attempts or suspicious requests for your PIN or OTP. Regularly review your transaction history. While individual transaction limits are typically INR 1 Lakh, remember that some banks or apps may have lower daily or monthly cumulative limits. It's wise to be aware of these. If you change your international mobile number, ensure you update it with your Indian bank promptly to avoid service disruption. If you face any issues, contact your bank's NRI customer support for assistance.

Troubleshooting Common Issues and Staying Updated

Occasionally, you might encounter issues like transaction failures or difficulty in linking your account. Most common issues stem from incorrect mobile number registration with the bank, an inactive international SIM card during registration, or exceeding transaction limits. Double-check that your international mobile number linked to your NRE/NRO account is the one you are using for UPI registration. Ensure sufficient balance in your NRE/NRO account for transactions. If issues persist, uninstalling and reinstalling the UPI app or contacting your bank's customer service with transaction details or error messages can help. The UPI ecosystem is constantly evolving; stay updated by checking your bank's official announcements or the NPCI website for any new features, supported countries, or changes in policies regarding NRI UPI usage.

Benefits Beyond Payments: How UPI Simplifies NRI Finances

Beyond simple payments, UPI offers NRIs a broader simplification of their Indian financial management. It eliminates the need for complex international transfers for smaller, frequent remittances, reducing potential fees and delays. You can easily set up recurring payments for subscriptions, loan EMIs, or regular contributions to family. This level of control and immediacy over your Indian funds, irrespective of your geographical location, is a significant advantage. It fosters greater financial independence and ease, making it simpler to manage your ties to India without the logistical hurdles of traditional banking methods. UPI truly bridges the distance, making India feel closer through seamless financial interactions.

Conclusion

The ability for NRIs to use UPI with their international mobile numbers marks a significant stride in integrating non-resident Indians into India's digital payment revolution. By following the steps outlined in this guide – ensuring your NRE/NRO account is linked to your international number, registering through a UPI app, and setting your PIN – you can unlock a world of convenient, instant, and secure payments. Whether it's for supporting loved ones, managing property expenses, or simply enjoying cashless transactions during your visits, UPI simplifies your financial interactions in India like never before. Embrace this powerful tool for a truly hassle-free experience.
