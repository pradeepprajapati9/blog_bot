How to Find Your EPF UAN Number in India

15 Nov 2026

Your Universal Account Number (UAN) is absolutely crucial for managing your Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) account in India. It's a unique 12-digit number that remains constant throughout your working life, irrespective of how many jobs you change. Without your UAN, accessing vital services like checking your EPF balance, initiating withdrawals, or transferring funds between different employers' accounts becomes incredibly difficult. It’s a common scenario for many individuals to forget or misplace their UAN, especially when switching employers or if they never actively noted it down. This comprehensive guide aims to simplify the process, walking you through various straightforward methods to locate your UAN, both online and offline. By understanding how to quickly retrieve this vital number, you can ensure seamless management of your retirement savings and stay in control of your financial future.

Understanding Your UAN and Why It's Important

Your Universal Account Number (UAN) is a unique 12-digit identification number assigned by the Employees' Provident Fund Organization (EPFO) to every employee contributing to the EPF scheme. Think of it as your primary key to all your provident fund accounts linked across different employers. Each time you join a new company, a new Member ID (also called PF Account Number) is generated, but your UAN remains the same. All these Member IDs are linked under your single UAN. This consolidation simplifies EPF management, allowing you to view all your PF accounts and balances from a single platform. It’s essential for services like online withdrawal claims, checking your passbook, initiating transfers, and linking your Aadhaar and PAN. Knowing your UAN is the first step towards digitally managing your EPF.

Finding Your UAN Through Your Employer

One of the simplest and most reliable ways to find your UAN is by contacting your current or previous employer. Your employer is responsible for generating your UAN and contributing to your EPF account. For your current employer, reach out to your HR department or finance team. They typically provide your UAN on your salary slip or can easily retrieve it from their records. Many companies include the UAN on payslips as a standard practice. If you need the UAN from a past employment, contact the HR or accounts department of that specific company. They should have your records and can provide your UAN details. While this method might take a day or two depending on your employer's response time, it is usually straightforward and doesn't require any prior online registration or credentials from your side. Always ensure you are asking for the UAN associated with your specific Member ID from that company.

How to Find Your UAN Online (If You Have Your Member ID)

If you know your EPF Member ID (also known as PF Account Number) from any of your employers, you can easily find your UAN online. First, visit the EPFO Member Portal at unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/. On the homepage, look for and click on the "Know Your UAN Status" option, which is usually found under 'Important Links'. Next, select your state and the specific EPFO office where your employer's EPF account is registered. Carefully enter your Member ID, which is typically in the format XX/XXX/XXXXXXX/XXX and can be found on your salary slip. After that, provide other requested details such as your name, date of birth, registered mobile number, and complete the captcha code. Click "Get Authorization Pin" to receive an OTP (One-Time Password) on your mobile. Enter the OTP and click "Validate OTP and Get UAN" to have your UAN displayed on the screen. This method is quick and efficient if you have access to your Member ID and a registered mobile number.

Finding Your UAN Without Member ID (Using Aadhaar/PAN)

Even if you don't know your Member ID, you can still find your UAN online using your Aadhaar or PAN details, provided these are linked to your EPF account. Start by visiting the EPFO Member e-Sewa portal at unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/memberinterface/. On the login page or under "Important Links," look for the "Know Your UAN" option. On the subsequent page, instead of Member ID, you will find input fields allowing you to enter your Aadhaar Number or PAN Number. Fill in the required details including your date of birth, your full name, and your registered mobile number. Enter the CAPTCHA code correctly and then click on "Get Authorization Pin." An OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number for verification. Input the OTP and click "Validate OTP and Get UAN" to successfully retrieve your UAN, which will then be displayed on your screen. This method relies on your Aadhaar or PAN being successfully linked to your EPF account.

Using the UMANG App to Retrieve Your UAN

The UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) app is a central platform for accessing various government services, and it offers a convenient way to find your UAN. If you haven't already, download the UMANG app from your smartphone's app store (Google Play Store or Apple App Store) and complete the one-time registration using your mobile number. Once you are successfully logged in, use the app's search bar to look for "EPFO." From the list of EPFO services, select "Employee Centric Services." Within this section, you will find an option clearly labeled "Know Your UAN." Click on this, and you will likely need to enter your registered mobile number (the one linked to your Aadhaar/EPF) and then verify your identity with an OTP. After successful verification, your UAN will be displayed directly within the app. The UMANG app provides a user-friendly interface and consolidates multiple services, making it a very handy tool for various government-related tasks, including managing your EPF.

What to Do If You Cannot Find Your UAN

If you've diligently tried all the above methods and are still unable to retrieve your UAN, do not despair, as there are further steps you can take. Firstly, you can contact the EPFO's toll-free helpline number 1800 118 005. When you call, be prepared to provide personal details such as your name, date of birth, Aadhaar/PAN, and details of your previous employers. The helpline agents are equipped to guide you or assist in retrieving your UAN. Secondly, if online methods are not yielding results, a direct visit to your nearest EPFO regional office can be very helpful. Remember to carry your identity proofs like Aadhaar and PAN, along with any employment details or old salary slips. An officer there can assist you in person to find your UAN and help you activate it if needed. As a last resort, you can raise a grievance on the EPF i-Grievance Management System (EPFiGMS) portal. Select "PF Member" status, fill in your details, and clearly explain your issue regarding UAN retrieval. The EPFO will then investigate and respond to your complaint.

Securing Your UAN for Future Reference

Once you successfully find your UAN, it's highly recommended to keep it safe and accessible for future reference. Write it down in a secure personal diary, save it in a password-protected digital document, or even send it to yourself via email. Make sure to activate your UAN on the EPFO Member Portal if you haven't already. This activation process usually involves creating a password, which then allows you to log in to the portal using your UAN and password. Regularly checking your EPF passbook and ensuring your details are up-to-date on the portal are good practices. Additionally, linking your Aadhaar and PAN to your UAN is now mandatory and streamlines many EPF-related services. By keeping your UAN secure and activated, you ensure smooth and hassle-free management of your provident fund throughout your career.

Conclusion

Successfully finding your UAN is a foundational step towards effectively managing your Employees' Provident Fund. Fortunately, multiple practical avenues are available, whether you prefer reaching out to your employer, utilizing the EPFO's dedicated online portal with your Member ID, Aadhaar, or PAN, or leveraging the convenient UMANG app for mobile access. Should you encounter any persistent difficulties, remember that the EPFO's toll-free helpline and regional offices are readily available to provide direct, personalized assistance. Taking the initiative to locate and securely record your UAN ensures that you can effortlessly access all your provident fund services, which is paramount for your long-term financial security and retirement planning. Don't let a missing UAN be a barrier; empower yourself by using these practical steps to take full control of your EPF today.
