How to Check Vehicle RC Details Online in India

02 Nov 2026

Your vehicle's Registration Certificate (RC) is one of its most important legal documents, verifying ownership and vehicle specifications. In today's digital age, you no longer need to visit a Regional Transport Office (RTO) to check or verify these details. Whether you're buying a used car, selling your old bike, or simply need to confirm some information, accessing your RC details online is quick, convenient, and essential. This comprehensive guide will walk you through the simple steps to check your vehicle's RC details online using official government portals, ensuring you have accurate and up-to-date information at your fingertips.

Why Checking RC Details Online is Important

Accessing your vehicle's RC details online offers several practical benefits. Firstly, if you're buying or selling a used vehicle, verifying the RC details ensures transparency and helps prevent fraud. You can cross-check the owner's name, vehicle specifications, and ensure there are no pending challans or legal issues. Secondly, it helps you confirm crucial information like the vehicle's make, model, fuel type, and registration date, which is vital for insurance renewals and tax payments. Thirdly, it ensures all the data matches your physical RC, preventing discrepancies during any official checks. Having this information readily available online saves you time and effort, eliminating the need for RTO visits and providing peace of mind.

Information You'll Need to Check Your RC

To successfully check your vehicle's RC details online, the primary piece of information you will need is your vehicle's registration number. This is the alphanumeric code prominently displayed on your license plates (e.g., MH-01-AB-1234). For most basic inquiries on the official government portal, just the registration number is sufficient. In some cases, for more detailed checks or specific services, you might be asked for additional identifiers like the chassis number or engine number. You can find these numbers on your existing physical RC document, as well as physically stamped on the vehicle's frame (typically under the bonnet or near the driver's door for cars, or on the frame for bikes). Keep your vehicle registration number handy before starting the online process.

Step-by-Step Guide: Using the Parivahan Portal

The official Parivahan Sewa portal is the most reliable way to check your vehicle's RC details. Here's how to do it: First, open your web browser and navigate to the official Parivahan Sewa website. Look for an option like 'Online Services' and then 'Vehicle Related Services,' or directly search for 'Know Your Vehicle Details'. Once on the relevant page, you will be prompted to enter your vehicle's registration number. Carefully type in the full registration number without any spaces or special characters. Next, complete the captcha verification to prove you are not a robot. Finally, click the 'Search' or 'Vahan Search' button. The portal will then display your vehicle's key RC details, including owner information, registration date, and other specifications.

Deciphering Your RC Details: Key Information Explained

Once you access your vehicle's RC details online, you'll see a range of information. Understanding each field is crucial. The 'Registration Number' is your vehicle's unique identity. 'Owner Name' verifies the legal owner. The 'Registration Date' indicates when the vehicle was first registered. You'll also find the 'Vehicle Class' (e.g., Motorcycle, Car, Goods Carrier), 'Fuel Type' (Petrol, Diesel, Electric), and 'Make/Model' (manufacturer and specific model). Crucially, the portal displays 'Chassis Number' and 'Engine Number', unique identifiers for your vehicle's core components. Additionally, it shows the validity dates for 'Fitness' (especially for commercial vehicles), 'Insurance', 'PUCC' (Pollution Under Control Certificate), and 'Road Tax'. Regularly checking these validity dates helps ensure your vehicle remains compliant with regulations.

Alternative Method: Checking RC Details via SMS Service

For a quick check when you might have limited internet access or simply prefer an alternative, there's an SMS-based service to retrieve basic RC details. This service is provided by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways. To use it, simply open your phone's messaging application. Type the message 'VAHAN' followed by a space, and then your full vehicle registration number (e.g., VAHAN MH01AB1234). Send this SMS to the designated number: 7738299899. Within a few moments, you should receive a reply SMS containing essential information about your vehicle, such as the owner's name, vehicle type, and fitness validity. Please note that standard SMS charges may apply as per your mobile network plan for using this service.

Keeping Your Vehicle Documents Safe & Updated

While online access to RC details is incredibly convenient, it's vital to ensure your physical and digital documents are always safe and accurate. Always carry either your original RC, a valid photocopy, or a digital copy stored securely in DigiLocker, as mandated by traffic regulations. Regularly check the online details to confirm they match your physical RC and are up-to-date, especially after any vehicle modifications or changes. If you change your address, it is important to update it on your RC promptly, following the RTO procedures, to avoid any legal complications. Maintaining accurate and accessible vehicle documentation is a cornerstone of responsible vehicle ownership in India.

Conclusion

Having immediate access to your vehicle's Registration Certificate details is a significant convenience offered by India's digital initiatives. By using the official Parivahan portal or even a simple SMS service, you can quickly verify crucial information, ensuring compliance and peace of mind. Regularly checking these details helps you stay updated on your vehicle's status, aids in transactions, and contributes to responsible vehicle ownership. Embrace these digital tools to manage your vehicle documents efficiently and safely.
