How to Change Address on Aadhaar Card Online: A Simple Guide

24 Oct 2026

Your Aadhaar card serves as a vital identity proof in India, essential for linking to various government services, banking, and accessing subsidies. Life changes often involve moving to a new home, city, or even state. When this happens, ensuring your address on your Aadhaar card is up-to-date becomes incredibly important to prevent any disruptions in receiving critical communications and services. Thankfully, the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) offers a straightforward online process to update your address without needing to visit an Aadhaar centre. This comprehensive guide will walk you through every step, detailing the required documents, online procedures, and key considerations to make your Aadhaar address update smooth and error-free. Let’s ensure your Aadhaar reflects your current reality.

Why Update Your Aadhaar Address? The Importance

Changing your address on your Aadhaar card might seem like a minor task, but its implications are far-reaching. An updated address ensures that all your government correspondence, postal deliveries, and crucial documents reach you correctly. It's essential for services requiring address verification, such as opening a new bank account, applying for a passport, or accessing various subsidies and welfare schemes. An outdated address can lead to significant inconveniences, including missed communications or even delays in availing essential services. Keeping your Aadhaar details current not only simplifies your administrative tasks but also reinforces its role as a universally accepted proof of identity and address across India. Ensure your details reflect your current residence to avoid any future complications.

Documents Required for Online Address Change

To update your Aadhaar address online, you'll need a valid Proof of Address (PoA) document. The UIDAI accepts several types of documents, so make sure you have a scanned copy (preferably PDF or JPEG format) of at least one of these ready. Common PoA documents include: Passport; Bank Statement/Passbook (not older than 3 months); Post Office Account Statement/Passbook (not older than 3 months); Ration Card; Driving License; Government Photo ID Cards/Service Photo Identity Card issued by PSU; Electricity Bill (not older than 3 months); Water Bill (not older than 3 months); Telephone Landline Bill (not older than 3 months); Property Tax Receipt (not older than 1 year); Credit Card Statement (not older than 3 months); Registered Sale/Lease/Rent Agreement; LPG Gas Connection Bill (not older than 3 months); Insurance Policy; Voter ID Card. It's vital that the document you choose clearly displays your name and the new address you wish to update. Without a valid PoA, your online request cannot be processed.

Step-by-Step Guide to Update Online

The online address update process is straightforward if you follow these steps carefully. First, visit the official UIDAI website. Navigate to the 'My Aadhaar' section and select 'Update Aadhaar Address Online'. You will then need to log in using your 12-digit Aadhaar number and the captcha code displayed. An OTP (One Time Password) will be sent to your registered mobile number. Enter this OTP to proceed. Once logged in, choose the 'Update Demographics Data & Check Status' option. Select 'Address' from the list of demographic details you wish to modify. Now, carefully enter your new address details exactly as they appear on your Proof of Address document. Double-check all spellings and numbers before moving to the next stage to avoid errors.

Uploading Documents and Making Payment

After entering your new address, the portal will prompt you to upload your Proof of Address (PoA) document. Make sure the scanned copy is clear, legible, and within the specified file size limits. Select the type of PoA document you are uploading from the dropdown menu and then browse to upload your file. Once uploaded, review the address details one final time. If everything is correct, proceed to make the online payment. A nominal fee is usually charged for online demographic updates. You can pay this fee using various methods like net banking, debit card, or credit card. Upon successful payment, a Unique Request Number (URN) will be generated. This URN is extremely important, so make sure to save it for tracking your request.

Tracking Your Update Request and Verification

Once you have successfully submitted your address update request and received your Unique Request Number (URN), the waiting period begins. You can track the status of your request at any time by visiting the UIDAI website. Go to the 'Check Aadhaar Update Status' section and enter your URN along with your Aadhaar number. The portal will display the current status of your request, which typically progresses through stages like "Submitted," "Verification Stage," and finally "Completed." The UIDAI team verifies the submitted documents against the information provided. This verification process can take a few days to a couple of weeks, depending on the volume of requests. Patience is key during this period.

Receiving Your Updated Aadhaar and Important Tips

Once your address update request is successfully processed and verified, you will receive an SMS notification on your registered mobile number confirming the completion. You will not receive a physical copy of the updated Aadhaar card by post automatically for online updates. To get your updated Aadhaar, you can download your e-Aadhaar from the UIDAI website. Go to 'Download Aadhaar', enter your Aadhaar number or URN, and the OTP. Your e-Aadhaar will be available for download, which is digitally signed and valid for all purposes. Remember to save a copy and print it if needed. Always double-check the updated address on your e-Aadhaar to ensure accuracy. If you notice any discrepancies, you might need to raise a new update request or visit an Aadhaar enrolment center.

Conclusion

Keeping your Aadhaar card address updated is a crucial administrative task that directly impacts your access to essential services and government benefits in India. This online process, though requiring attention to detail and accurate documentation, provides immense convenience. By carefully following the steps outlined in this guide – from preparing your Proof of Address to tracking your Unique Request Number – you can successfully update your Aadhaar details from anywhere. An accurate Aadhaar card not only ensures you receive important mail and communications but also strengthens your digital identity, making everyday transactions and government interactions much smoother. Embrace the convenience and ensure your Aadhaar is always current.
