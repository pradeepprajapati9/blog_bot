Download Aadhaar Card Online: A Simple Guide

27 Oct 2026

The Aadhaar card is a vital document for every Indian resident, serving as proof of identity and address. While carrying the physical card is important, having a digital copy or e-Aadhaar is equally useful for various services and verification processes. Fortunately, downloading your Aadhaar card online is a straightforward process. This guide will walk you through the simple steps to get your e-Aadhaar from the official UIDAI website, ensuring you always have a readily accessible copy.

What is e-Aadhaar?

e-Aadhaar is a digitally signed electronic version of your Aadhaar card, issued by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI). It holds the same validity as the physical Aadhaar card and can be used for various purposes like opening bank accounts, applying for government schemes, and proving your identity. The e-Aadhaar is secured with a password, typically derived from your PIN code and name, making it a safe and convenient digital document to possess. It is a downloadable PDF file that you can store on your phone, computer, or cloud storage for easy access whenever needed. This digital format eliminates the worry of misplacing or damaging your physical card.

Prerequisites for Downloading

Before you begin the download process, ensure you have a few key pieces of information handy. Firstly, your 12-digit Aadhaar number is essential. If you don't have your Aadhaar number, you can use your registered mobile number or email address if you know it. Secondly, your mobile number must be registered with UIDAI. This is crucial because you will receive an OTP (One-Time Password) on your registered mobile number, which is mandatory for verifying your identity and completing the download. If your mobile number is not updated, you will need to visit a Aadhaar Enrollment Centre to update it first. Having a stable internet connection is also important for a smooth download experience.

Step-by-Step Download Process

To download your e-Aadhaar, visit the official UIDAI website. Navigate to the 'My Aadhaar' section and click on 'Download Aadhaar'. You will be prompted to enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number or your Virtual ID (VID). After entering your Aadhaar number, type the security code displayed on the screen. Next, you will have the option to choose between receiving an OTP on your registered mobile number or requesting a masked Aadhaar. Select 'Send OTP' if you wish to download the unmasked Aadhaar. An OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number. Enter this OTP in the designated field. Finally, click 'Verify & Download'. Your e-Aadhaar PDF will be downloaded to your device.

Opening Your e-Aadhaar PDF

Once the e-Aadhaar PDF is downloaded, you will find that it is password-protected. To open the file, you need to enter the correct password. The password for your e-Aadhaar is a combination of the first four letters of your name (in capital letters, as they appear on your Aadhaar card) and your four-digit year of birth (YYYY format). For example, if your name is RAMESH KUMAR and you were born in 1990, your password would be RAME1990. Ensure you enter the password exactly as specified. This password protection ensures the security and privacy of your Aadhaar details, preventing unauthorized access.

Troubleshooting Common Issues

If you encounter issues while downloading your e-Aadhaar, several common problems and their solutions can help. If you do not receive the OTP, ensure your mobile number is correctly registered with UIDAI and that you have a stable network connection. Try requesting the OTP again after a short while. If you enter the wrong OTP or password multiple times, your download might be temporarily blocked. In such cases, wait for some time before trying again. If your Aadhaar details are incorrect on the downloaded card, you will need to visit an Aadhaar Enrollment Centre to get them updated. Remember to check the Aadhaar number and personal details carefully when entering them.

Conclusion

Downloading your e-Aadhaar online is a simple yet powerful way to keep a secure and accessible copy of your identification. By following these steps, you can easily obtain your digital Aadhaar card and use it whenever necessary. Remember to keep your registered mobile number updated to ensure smooth OTP delivery. The e-Aadhaar is a testament to the digital India initiative, making essential documents readily available to citizens.
