New Electricity Connection: How to Apply in India

08 Nov 2026

Setting up a new home or business often comes with the essential task of getting an electricity connection. In India, this process might seem complex due to varying state regulations and documentation requirements. However, with the right information, it can be quite straightforward. This comprehensive guide will walk you through every step of applying for a new electricity connection in India, whether you prefer to apply online or offline. We'll cover the necessary documents, the application procedure, potential costs, and what to expect during the installation phase, ensuring you have power in your new premises without unnecessary delays. Our aim is to demystify the process and provide you with clear, actionable steps.

Understanding the Process & Who Can Apply

Applying for a new electricity connection involves registering with your local electricity distribution company (DISCOM). This could be agencies like BSES, Tata Power, Adani Electricity, or state-specific boards like MSEDCL, TANGEDCO, etc. Typically, any individual or entity who owns or legally occupies a property (residential, commercial, or industrial) and requires electricity supply is eligible to apply. The process primarily ensures that your premises are suitable for a connection and that you meet the regulatory requirements. It starts with submitting an application, followed by document verification, site inspection, fee payment, and finally, meter installation. Knowing which DISCOM serves your area is the crucial first step, usually determined by your address.

Essential Documents Required

The documentation required for a new electricity connection is critical and generally includes proof of identity, address, and property ownership or occupancy. For identity, you'll need Aadhaar card, PAN card, Voter ID, or Passport. For address proof, utility bills (previous residence), Aadhaar, or a driving license can be used. Property ownership can be proven with a sale deed, property tax receipt, or allotment letter. If you are a tenant, a valid rent agreement along with a No-Objection Certificate (NOC) from the landlord is usually required. Additionally, a wiring contractor's test report or completion certificate for your internal wiring might be requested. Always keep both original and photocopies handy, and ensure all documents are self-attested.

Application Modes: Online vs. Offline

Most electricity boards in India now offer both online and offline application methods for convenience. The online method is generally faster and allows you to track your application status from the comfort of your home. You'll visit the official website of your local DISCOM, fill out an online form, and upload scanned copies of your documents. The offline method involves visiting the nearest customer service center or a designated office of your DISCOM. Here, you'll obtain an application form, fill it manually, and submit it along with physical copies of your documents. Both methods lead to the same outcome, but the online route saves time and offers more transparency in tracking, which is often preferred by applicants today.

The Online Application Process Explained

To apply online, first, identify your local DISCOM and visit their official website. Look for sections like 'New Connection,' 'Apply for Connection,' or 'Consumer Services.' You'll likely need to register on the portal to create an account. Once logged in, select the 'New Connection' option and fill in the detailed application form. This form will ask for personal details, property address, type of connection (residential, commercial), and the desired load (in kW). Carefully upload scanned copies of all required documents in the specified formats (e.g., PDF, JPEG). After submission, you'll receive an application reference number, which is crucial for tracking your application status online. Keep this number safe.

The Offline Application Process Explained

For those preferring the offline route, begin by locating the nearest customer service center or regional office of your electricity distribution company. Once there, request a 'New Electricity Connection Application Form.' Fill out all sections of the form accurately and legibly, providing details such as your name, address, contact information, type of premises, and proposed electricity load. Gather all the necessary documents mentioned earlier, make photocopies, and self-attest them. Submit the completed form along with the attested document copies to the designated counter. The official will provide you with an acknowledgement receipt or a reference number, which you should retain for future follow-ups and inquiries. Don't hesitate to ask clarifying questions if anything is unclear during the submission.

Fees, Inspection & Meter Installation

Once your application and documents are verified, the DISCOM will likely schedule a site inspection. An official will visit your premises to assess the feasibility of the connection, check the internal wiring, and confirm the details provided in your application. Following a successful inspection, you will receive a demand note or an invoice for the connection charges. These charges typically include application fees, security deposit, service line charges, and meter cost. Pay these fees through the specified online or offline payment modes. After payment confirmation, the DISCOM will proceed with the physical installation of the electricity meter and connect your premises to the grid. The timeline for this final step can vary based on the DISCOM and local conditions.

Key Things to Remember & Common Hurdles

To ensure a smooth application process, always double-check all documents for accuracy and ensure they are up-to-date before submission. Keep track of your application reference number, whether online or offline, for easy follow-up. Be proactive in checking your application status regularly via the online portal or by calling the customer service helpline. Common hurdles include incomplete documentation, discrepancies in address proof, or delays in site inspection. If you encounter issues, visit the customer service center with your application details for direct assistance. Remember to only use licensed electricians for internal wiring to avoid complications during inspection, and ensure your wiring certificate is ready when requested. Patience and clear communication are key.

Conclusion

Getting a new electricity connection in India is a fundamental step for any new household or business. While the procedures might seem daunting at first, breaking them down into simple steps as outlined in this guide makes the process manageable. By preparing your documents in advance, understanding the application modes, and knowing what to expect at each stage, you can significantly reduce delays and ensure a hassle-free experience. Powering your premises is now simpler with these clear instructions, helping you focus on settling into your new space or starting your venture effectively.
