Best Phone Under 10000 5G: Top Picks for India (2024)

29 Aug 2026

Finding a 5G smartphone that perfectly fits a budget of under ₹10,000 in India can feel like a challenge, but it's definitely possible! With technology advancing rapidly, even this entry-level price segment now offers some compelling 5G-ready devices. For Indian users, having 5G capabilities future-proofs your device, allowing you to experience blazing-fast internet speeds as 5G rollout continues across the country. In this guide, we'll help you navigate the options, highlighting what truly matters in a budget 5G phone and presenting some of the best choices available right now that deliver genuine value for your hard-earned money. Let's find your next reliable companion.

Why 5G Matters (Even on a Budget)

Even if 5G coverage isn't fully ubiquitous in your area yet, opting for a 5G-enabled phone today is a smart move for future-proofing your investment. As major telecom operators continue their rapid expansion of 5G networks across India, you'll be ready to tap into significantly faster download and upload speeds, smoother streaming, and lower latency for online activities. Imagine downloading movies in minutes, enjoying lag-free video calls, or browsing complex web pages with instant loading times. While a 4G phone might save you a little money now, a 5G device ensures you won't miss out on these enhanced experiences as the network becomes more prevalent. It’s about getting the most out of your smartphone for years to come, without needing an upgrade just for connectivity.

Key Specifications to Look For Beyond 5G

While 5G is the headline feature, other specifications are crucial for a good user experience under ₹10,000. Look for a competent processor, ideally a MediaTek Dimensity or a Snapdragon 4 series chip, as these handle daily tasks and 5G connectivity efficiently. A minimum of 4GB RAM is recommended for smooth multitasking and basic app usage; anything less might feel sluggish. Storage-wise, 64GB is a good starting point, but ensure it's expandable via a microSD card slot, as system files and apps quickly fill up space. A large battery, preferably 5000mAh or more, is essential for all-day usage, especially with 5G consuming more power. Lastly, while cameras won't be flagship-level, a 50MP main sensor can capture decent photos in good lighting conditions, which is sufficient for social media and casual photography.

Top Budget 5G Picks: Our Recommendations

Navigating the sub-₹10,000 5G market requires careful selection, as not all phones offer the same value. Based on current offerings and user feedback in India, a few models consistently stand out. The Lava Blaze 2 5G and the Redmi 12 5G (or its rebrand, Poco M6 Pro 5G) are often at the forefront, known for balancing decent performance with reliable 5G connectivity within this strict budget. Samsung also occasionally brings down its M-series prices, making the Galaxy M14 5G a strong contender if you can find it during a sale. These phones usually offer a good mix of battery life, basic camera capabilities, and a reasonably smooth user experience. Let's delve into what makes a couple of these options particularly strong choices for Indian users.

Lava Blaze 2 5G: A Solid Indian Contender

The Lava Blaze 2 5G has emerged as a strong contender in the budget 5G segment, often lauded for its 'Made in India' appeal and a surprisingly clean Android experience. It typically features the MediaTek Dimensity 6020 processor, which is capable of handling daily tasks and 5G connectivity without major hiccups. The phone usually comes with a 50MP primary camera, which performs adequately in well-lit environments, alongside a decent front camera for selfies and video calls. Its 5000mAh battery ensures you get through a full day of moderate use, a critical factor for many Indian users who rely heavily on their smartphones. If you appreciate a stock Android interface without too much bloatware, the Lava Blaze 2 5G offers a refreshing experience, making it a reliable and straightforward choice for everyday practical use.

Redmi 12 5G: Xiaomi's Feature-Packed Offering

Xiaomi's Redmi 12 5G (often similar to the Poco M6 Pro 5G) is another excellent option if you're looking for a feature-rich experience under ₹10,000. This device usually packs the Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 processor, which is known for its efficiency and consistent performance, making it suitable for casual gaming and smooth app navigation. It often boasts a large, immersive display, frequently with a 90Hz refresh rate, providing a smoother visual experience than standard 60Hz screens. The Redmi 12 5G also features a capable 50MP main camera, delivering respectable image quality for its price segment. With a 5000mAh battery, it offers commendable endurance. While it runs on Xiaomi’s MIUI, which is known for its extensive features and customization options, it might come with some pre-installed apps, a common trade-off in this price category.

Making Your Choice: What Matters Most to You?

When picking the best 5G phone under ₹10,000, remember that compromises are inevitable. Your ideal choice depends on your personal priorities. If a clean, bloatware-free Android experience and supporting an Indian brand are important, the Lava Blaze 2 5G might be your go-to. If you prefer a feature-rich interface, a slightly more powerful processor for some gaming, and a higher refresh rate display, the Redmi 12 5G could be a better fit. Always check for the latest pricing and any ongoing sales, as prices in this segment can fluctuate frequently. Consider factors like build quality, software updates policy, and local service center availability before making your final decision. No matter your choice, rest assured that getting a capable 5G phone under ₹10,000 is well within reach today.

Conclusion

The market for 5G phones under ₹10,000 in India has truly evolved, offering genuinely useful and reliable devices for budget-conscious consumers. While you might not get all the bells and whistles of premium smartphones, the options highlighted here deliver on the promise of 5G connectivity combined with essential features like good battery life, decent cameras, and satisfactory performance for everyday tasks. By understanding your specific needs and prioritizing what's most important to you, you can confidently choose a 5G smartphone that not only fits your budget but also serves you well for the coming years as India's digital landscape continues to advance.
